About this archive

Keep the original recipe

We preserve what was written down originally and leave room for a modern version later if needed.

Search by story

Search by family name, holiday, ingredient, or the kind of dish you are craving.

Grow without reformatting

Each recipe stands on its own, so additions can be made over time without changing the structure.

What this site is meant to do

This website is designed to hold a family collection in a simple, durable way. It makes it easy to browse favorites, find recipes by ingredient or occasion, and connect every dish back to the person who shared it with the family.

The site is intentionally simple so it can live on GitHub Pages without any complicated setup. That means it is easy to maintain, easy to publish, and easy to keep adding recipes for years to come.
