# Crescent Rolls

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

2 Tbsp. yeast
3/4 cup warm water
3/4 cup buttermilk or sour milk
3/4 cup sour cream
5 ½ cups flour
½ cup shortening
2 Tbsp. sugar
2 tsp. baking powder
2 tsp. salt

## Procedure

Dissolve yeast in warm water. Add 2 ½ cups flour and the rest of the ingredients; blend on low. Stir in remaining flour and knead for 5 minutes. Divide dough into 3 parts. Roll dough into 12-inch circle about ½ inch thick. Spread with soft butter, cut into 16 wedges and roll up beginning at the rounded edge. Place rolls with point underneath on greased baking sheet. Curve slightly and brush with butter. Let rise 1 hour before baking. Heat oven to 375°. Bake rolls 20-25 min.

## Notes

Makes 48 rolls.
