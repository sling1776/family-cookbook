# Alfredo Sauce

Author: Becky Concoby

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1/4 cup flour
- 2 to 3 cups half and half or whole milk
- 3/4 cup parmesan cheese
- 2 Tbsp, parsley
- ½ tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. Melt butter then add the rest.  Pour over pasta.
2. Note: This recipe doubled is about right for a 22 oz. package of noodles.
