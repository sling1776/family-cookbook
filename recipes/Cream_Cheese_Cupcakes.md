# Cream Cheese Cupcakes

## Author

Alice Chantry

## Ingredients

### Crust
1 ½ cups graham cracker crumbs
½ cup sugar
½ cup melted butter
### Filling
3 pkgs. (8 oz) cream cheese (room temperature)
1 cup sugar
1/8 tsp. salt
4 eggs extra large
2 Tbsp. vanilla

## Procedure

Mix together crust ingredients with fork. Spoon one Tbsp. mixture into cupcake papers and press down.
Filling:
Mix in mixer until smooth on high. Spoon in each cupcake 2/3 full. Bake at 350° about 40 minutes or until tooth pick comes out clean. Cool. Add favorite topping (fresh fruit) Serve at room temperature.
