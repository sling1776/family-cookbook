# Potato Candy

## Author

Laurie Fryatt

## Ingredients

1 small potato (egg size) boiled until soft. 
1/2 package of powdered sugar
Peanut butter

## Procedure

Remove skin from potato and mash. 
Add: about ½ package of powdered sugar or until it is play dough stiff. Roll or pat out into square. Spread peanut butter on it, then roll like jelly roll. Slice into 1/4" slices. As slices sit they dry and stiffen. Enjoy!

## Notes

This recipe came from my former roommate Nancy Askew. It is her Grandmother’s recipe.
