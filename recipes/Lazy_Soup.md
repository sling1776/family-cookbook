# Lazy Soup

## Author

Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients

1 lb. ground beef
1 cup potatoes
1 cup celery
1 cup carrots
1 quart tomatoes
1 tsp. salt
1/4 tsp. pepper
1/8 tsp. basil
1 tsp. Kitchen Bouquet
1 bay leaf

## Procedure

Brown hamburger and simmer all ingredients until vegetables are done.
