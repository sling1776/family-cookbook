# Sawmill Gravy

Author: Tawna Marsh

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup cold bacon grease
- 2/3 cup flour
- 1 1/2 cups milk, heated
- 2 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp salt

## Procedure
1. Melt grease in heavy saucepan over medium heat. Sprinkle in flour, whisking constantly.  Cook about 1 minute, then whisk in hot milk, pepper and salt.  Cook and stir 5 minutes (It will keep several days in the refrigerator, but you may need to add a bit of milk to thin it out when reheating.)  Yield: about 2 3/4 cups
