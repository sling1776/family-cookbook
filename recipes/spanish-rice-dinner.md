# Spanish Rice Dinner

Author: Chrissy Probst

## Ingredients
- 1 pound cooked and drained hamburger
- 1 can stewed tomatoes
- 1 can cut green beans
- 1 cup cooked long grain rice
- 1 Tbsp. dried minced onion
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. Worcestershire Sauce
- ½ tsp. ground mustard
- ¼ tsp. garlic powder
- 1/8 tsp. pepper

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients together in a bowl.  Put in a gallon size freezer bag and freeze for later!  To eat, defrost and warm in microwave.  Enjoy!
