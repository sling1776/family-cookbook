# Ohukaisets (Finnish Pancakes)

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 1/2 cups milk
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1 3/4 cups featherlight mix
- 2 eggs

## Procedure
1. Beat ingredients together Let the batter sit in the fridge for 20-30 minutes.  Pour about a 1/4 cup of batter into pan. It will be thin. Fry until slightly brown, flip and cook about 1 minute more.   Top with whip cream and jam- roll and eat. Note: Most of the recipes in this section are from Betsy Thomas and Kristi Kirkland on www.eatingglutenfree.com
