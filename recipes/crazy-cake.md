# Crazy Cake

Author: Ruby Fielding

## Ingredients
- 3 cups flour
- 6 Tbsp. cocoa
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 tsp. soda
- 3/4 cup oil
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 2 Tbsp. vinegar

## Procedure
1. Mix above ingredients. Place in ungreased 9x13 pan.  Make three wells or holes.  Pour into the wells:
2. Pour over all 2 cups of cold water then mix everything together with a fork. Bake 350° for 30-35 minutes.
