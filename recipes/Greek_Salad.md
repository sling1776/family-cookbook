# Greek Salad

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

Tomato wedges
Feta Cheese crumbled
Slices cucumbers
Pitted Olives
Green pepper strips
Salt
Onion slices, separated rings
Oregano
Dressing of olive oil and vinegar.

## Procedure

Mix everything.

## Notes

Serving in Greece on our mission exposed us to lots of neat new foods.
