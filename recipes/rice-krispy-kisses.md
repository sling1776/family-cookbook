# Rice Krispy Kisses

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 cup white corn syrup
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 6 cups rice krispies

## Procedure
1. Directions:
2. Mix corn syrup and sugar in a sauce pan. Bring to boil.  Remove from heat and add peanut butter.	Mix well.  Fold in rice krispies. While the mixture is still warm mold it into a small funnel sprayed with cooking spray to make a “kiss” shape.  Or just use your hands to make a kiss type shape.  Make small strips of paper with printed sweet sayings on them to personalize your kiss.  Put strip on tin foil square.  Place krispy kiss on square of tin foil and wrap it up and around.
