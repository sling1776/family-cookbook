# Egg Bread

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- ¾ cup milk
- 2 eggs
- 3 cups bread flour
- 1 ½ tsp. salt
- 3 Tbsp. butter or margarine
- ¼ cup sugar
- 2¼ tsp. yeast

## Procedure
1. Place all ingredients into bread pan, select Light Crust & regular setting and push Start.
2. Note:  Heat up a slice for 10 seconds in the microwave to make it taste melt in your mouth smooth even two or three days after baking.
