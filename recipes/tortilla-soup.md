# Tortilla Soup

Author: Alice Chantry

## Ingredients
- 4 pieces chicken
- 2 cups cheese
- ½ cup celery, chopped
- ½ cup carrots, chopped
- 4 cups crushed corn chips

## Procedure
1. Boil chicken in 2 quarts water.  Remove from broth, skin, debone and dice chicken. Add chicken, carrots and celery in broth. Cook until carrots are tender.  Add cheese and chips. Cook 5 minutes and enjoy.
