# Sponge Cake

## Author

Aunt Zeona Walker

## Ingredients

8 egg whites
¼ tsp. salt
½ tsp. cream of tartar
Beat until stiff. In separate bowl mix the following:
8 egg yolks, beaten
1¼ cups water
1 tsp. vanilla
1¼ cups flour
1¼ cups sugar

## Procedure

Beat until smooth, fold in egg white mixture. 
Bake in ungreased tube pan. Bake 1 hour at 325°.
