# Sunset Salad

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

2 small pkgs. orange jello
1 grated carrot
1 tsp. finely grated orange rind
1 small can pineapple, drained
3 ¾ cup water
### Dressing
¾ cup water
3 Tbsp. sugar
1 Tbsp. cornstarch

## Procedure

Gel the above.
Serve with dressing
Cook the above and cool. Add
Juice of one orange and bit of rind. Fold in ½ cup of whipped cream. Dollop on top of jello.
