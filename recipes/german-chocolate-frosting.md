# German Chocolate Frosting

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 can evaporated milk
- 1 cup sugar
- 3 egg yolks
- 1 cube margarine
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1/3 cup coconut
- 1 cup chopped pecans

## Procedure
1. Combine milk, sugar, eggs, margarine and vanilla.  Cook over medium heat and stir until thickened.  About 12 minutes.  Add coconut and pecans.  Beat until thick enough to spread.
