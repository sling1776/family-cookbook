# Banana Bread

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 4 cups featherlight mix
- 1 cup GF mix
- 2 tsp. xanthan gum
- 2 cups sugar
- 7 tsp. baking powder
- 2 tsp. salt
- 6 Tbsp. oil
- 1 1/2 cups milk
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups mashed bananas
- 2 cups chopped nuts (optional)

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 350°. Spray 3 regular loaf pans with Pam (or grease and flour using GF mix). Mix dry ingredients. Mix wet ingredients. Mix dry and wet ingredients together. Beat well. Pour into pans. Bake about 50 minutes (until a knife inserted in the middle comes out clean).
2. Note: The mixture of featherlight and GF mixes gives this banana bread the correct texture. You may use 5 cups of featherlight instead, but the bread will be much spongier.
