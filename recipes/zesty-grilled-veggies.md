# Zesty Grilled Veggies

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 4 zucchini (1-1/2 lb.), cut diagonally into 1/2-inch-thick slices
- 3 each red and yellow peppers (1-3/4 lb.), cut into 1/2-inch-wide strips
- 1/4 cup Kraft Zesty Italian Dressing
- 1/4 cup Kraft Grated Parmesan Cheese

## Procedure
1. Heat grill to medium heat.   Place veggies in grill basket.  Grill 10 minutes or until crisp tender, turning occasionally.  Place in large bowl. Add dressing, toss to coat.  Sprinkle with cheese.
