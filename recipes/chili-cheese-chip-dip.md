# Chili Cheese Chip Dip

Author: Sammi Jo Stewart

## Ingredients
- 1- 8 oz. pkg. cream cheese
- 1 can of chili
- 1-2 cups grated cheese

## Procedure
1. Spread cream cheese in the bottom of an oven or microwave safe dish. Pour 1 can of the chili of your choice on top. Sprinkle grated cheese on top. Bake or microwave until the cheese is melted. Enjoy with tortilla chips!
