# Vanilla Buttermilk Syrup

## Author

Tawna Marsh

## Ingredients

1 ½ cup sugar
¾ cup buttermilk
½ cup butter
2 Tbsp. Karo or corn syrup
1 tsp. baking soda
2 tsp. vanilla extract
Directions:

## Procedure

Combine sugar, buttermilk, butter, Karo and baking soda. Heat on medium heat until hot and melted. DO NOT HEAT TO A BOIL. Remove from heat and add vanilla extract.

## Notes

Excellent on French Toast, pancakes, waffles or as an ice cream or bread pudding sauce.
