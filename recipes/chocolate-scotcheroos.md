# Chocolate Scotcheroos

Author: Great Grandma Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup light corn syrup
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 6 cups Rice Krispies
- 1 cup chocolate chips
- 1 cup butterscotch chips

## Procedure
1. Cook sugar & syrup in 3 qt saucepan.  Cook over moderate heat, stirring frequently until mixture boils.  Remove from heat.  Stir in peanut butter.  Mix well.  Add Rice Krispies.  Stir until well blended.  Press into buttered 13x9 inch pan.  Melt chocolate & butterscotch chips in microwave.  Stir until blended.  Spread over cereal mixture.  Cool.  Cut into bars.
