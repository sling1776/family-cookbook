# Shelly's Spaghetti Sauce

## Author

Sammi Jo Stewart

## Ingredients

Diced small onion
Minced garlic
3 small cans tomato paste (with a little water)
2 small cans tomato sauce (with a little water)
1 large can whole tomatoes (pureed in blender)
Salt
pepper
garlic salt
onion salt
Italian seasoning
oregano 
basil.

## Procedure

Sauté onion and minced garlic in extra virgin olive oil until golden brown.
Add: 3 small cans tomato paste (with a little water) Heat 1 minute
Then add 2 small cans tomato sauce (with a little water), large can of whole (pureed) tomatoes, salt, pepper, garlic salt, onion salt, Italian seasoning, oregano and basil.
Bring to a boil then simmer for 1 ½ hours. Enjoy!

## Notes

I use a lot of the last three seasonings and add browned hamburger that I cook with garlic salt and Italian seasonings.
I got this recipe from a lady I met while living in Virginia. She is Italian and a fabulous and simple cook. I use this recipe for spaghetti and lasagna. Amounts of spices and some ingredients just depend on your preference.
