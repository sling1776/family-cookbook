# Meat Filling:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 lb. hamburger browned and drained
- ½ small onion, chopped
- 1 can cheddar cheese soup
- 1 can drained corn
- 5 Tbsp. salsa

## Procedure
1. Spread a thin layer of cornmeal mixture on a cookie sheet that has sides on it. Cover this with meat sauce and top with rest of cornmeal.  Bake in 350° oven for about 20 minutes. (Actually I am guessing since no time was given.) Cut into squares and serve warm.
