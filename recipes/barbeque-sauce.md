# Barbeque Sauce

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- 3 cups brown sugar
- 3 cups ketchup
- minced onion
- 1 can of crushed pineapple (you can use juice only if desired)
- garlic salt and mustard to taste

## Procedure
1. Optional: 1/4 cup soy sauce can be added for extra zing! Mix all together, marinate chicken, ribs, or anything else your heart desires!
