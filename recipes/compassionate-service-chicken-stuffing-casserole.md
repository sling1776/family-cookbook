# Compassionate Service Chicken & Stuffing Casserole

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 2 boxes of stuffing- cooked as directed.
- 3 lg. cans chicken OR 3 cups cooked chicken
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- ½ cup milk
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 cup shredded cheese

## Procedure
1. Layer chicken in bottom of 9x13” pan.  Mix sour cream, soup & milk together.  Pour over chicken.  Spread cooked stuffing over top.  Bake at 350° for 20 minutes.  Add cheese the last few minutes on top to let it melt.
2. Note: This meal can easily be made in two 8x8 square pans.  One for you and one for a friend or an extra to freeze for later.
