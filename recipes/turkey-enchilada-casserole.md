# Turkey Enchilada Casserole

Author: Sammi Jo Stewart

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground turkey
- 1 med. green pepper (chopped)
- 1 med. onion (chopped)
- 3 garlic cloves (minced)
- 2 (15 oz.) cans black beans (rinsed and drained)
- 1 jar (16 oz.) salsa
- 1 can (15 oz.) tomato sauce
- 1 can (14.5 oz.) Mexican stewed tomatoes
- 1 tsp. onion powder
- 1 tsp. garlic powder
- 1 tsp. ground cumin
- 12 corn tortillas
- 2 cups shredded cheese

## Procedure
1. Cook turkey, green pepper, and onion over medium heat until meat is no longer pink.
2. Add garlic and cook 1 minute longer.  Drain if needed.  Stir in beans, salsa, tomato sauce, tomatoes, onion powder, garlic powder and cumin. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered for 10 minutes.  Spread 1 cup meat sauce in 9x13 pan.  Top with 6 corn tortillas.
3. Spread with half of the remaining sauce and 1 cup of cheese.  Layer the remaining tortillas and meat sauce.  Cover and bake at 350° for 20 minutes.  Uncover and sprinkle remaining cheese.  Bake 5-10 minutes or cheese is melted.  Note: I've only used ground turkey but I think it would be good with chicken or hamburger.
