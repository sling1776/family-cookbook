# Raspberry Slush

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

2 cups sugar (1 ½ cup if raspberries are sweetened)
 3 ½ cups water
 6 oz undiluted orange juice
 1 pkg. frozen raspberries
 1 can crushed pineapple (optional)

## Procedure

Mix Sugar and water. Bring to boil and cool. Add remaining ingredients. Mix together, stirring occasionally while freezing. Take out a few hours before using.
