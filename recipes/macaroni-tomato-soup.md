# Macaroni & Tomato Soup

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- ½ cup chopped onion
- 1/4 cup diced green pepper
- 1 cup diced celery
- 4 oz. macaroni  (cooked)
- 1 can tomato juice (46 oz)
- 1 can tomato soup
- 1 can water
- 1-2 bay leaves
- ½ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. sweet basil
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- Dash of pepper

## Procedure
1. In heavy saucepan, melt butter, cook onion, green pepper, and celery until limp but not brown.  Add remaining ingredients. Heat to boiling.  Serve.
