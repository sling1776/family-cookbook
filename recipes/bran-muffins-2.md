# Bran Muffins

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 3 cups bran flakes (soak in milk first)
- 1 cup milk
- ½ cup applesauce
- 1 ½ cup brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 fat free Yoplait yogurt - French vanilla
- 2 ½ cups whole wheat flour
- 2 ½ tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. Mix together wet ingredients.  In separate bowl, mix together dry ingredients.  Make a well in dry ingredients and add wet ingredients.  Mix just until all ingredients are wet.  Fill muffin tins 2/3 of the way full. Bake at 350° for 20-25 minutes.
