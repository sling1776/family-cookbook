# Pastry

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2 ½ cup flour
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 egg yolk
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 cup shortening
- 2/3 cup milk

## Procedure
1. Roll out one half of the pastry on a cookie sheet.  Add 3/4 cup crushed cornflakes on crust.  Add a layer of sliced apples (6-8 apples), 1 cup sugar and 1 tsp cinnamon.  Roll rest of dough and place on apples.  Beat egg white and brush on top.  Bake 1 hour at 375°.  Glaze with 1 cup powered sugar and a little water.
