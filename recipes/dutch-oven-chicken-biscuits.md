# Dutch Oven Chicken & Biscuits

Author: Gina Olsen

## Ingredients
- 20 chicken tenders
- 1 bag carrots and peas
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 envelope of chicken noodle soup
- 2 cans water
- 1 can of Pillsbury dinner rolls

## Procedure
1. Brown chicken in dutch oven. Add carrots, peas, soups and water. Warm through. Add rolls on top and cook for about 15-20 minutes. Easy. Use about 20 coals on top and 12 on bottom.
