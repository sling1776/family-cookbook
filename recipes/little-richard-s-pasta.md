# Little Richard’s Pasta

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Cook Penne pasta as directed, in meantime heat large jar of your favorite spaghetti sauce with mushrooms, when thoroughly heated add 1 pint of cream.  Mix together with drained pasta and shredded mozzarella cheese and serve immediately.  Top with Parmesan cheese if desired.
