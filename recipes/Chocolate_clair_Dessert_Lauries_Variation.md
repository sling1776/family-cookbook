# Chocolate Éclair Dessert (Laurie's Variation)

## Author

Laurie Fryatt

## Ingredients

24 graham crackers (crumbled)
1 cup sugar
1/3 cup cocoa
1/4 cup evaporated milk
1 stick butter
1 tsp vanilla

## Procedure

Bring to boil and cook 1 minute: sugar, cocoa, evaporated milk.
Add butter, vanilla. Let cool and beat until thick. Spread on top of graham cracker crumbs and refrigerate overnight.

## Notes

Laurie Fryatt uses graham cracker crumbs in place of the whole cracker and the following topping in place of the choc. pudding.
