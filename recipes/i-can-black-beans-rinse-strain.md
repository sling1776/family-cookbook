# I can black beans (rinse & strain)

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 can kidney beans (rinse & strain)
- 1 can pinto beans w/ jalapenos
- 1 chili seasoning packet

## Procedure
1. Brown hamburger. Mix seasoning packet with 1 cup water and pour over browned meat in the crockpot. Add remaining ingredients. Stir. Cook for 1-3 hours on low heat.
