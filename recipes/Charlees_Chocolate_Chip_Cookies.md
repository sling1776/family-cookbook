# Charlee's Chocolate Chip Cookies

## Author

Charlee Olsen

## Ingredients

1 cup butter
1 cup shortening
2 cups brown sugar
1 cup sugar
4 eggs
3 tsp. vanilla, set aside
5 cups flour
2 tsp. baking soda
1 tsp. salt

## Procedure

Beat butter shortening, brown sugar and sugar 'til smooth. Add eggs and vanilla. Separately, mix flour baking soda, and salt together.
Mix dry mixture into moist mixture, adding more flour if necessary. Mix in chocolate chips, bake 375° for 9 minutes.

## Notes

Note: I like to take them out when they look uncooked so they stay soft.
