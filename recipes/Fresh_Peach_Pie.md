# Fresh Peach Pie

## Author

Kadie Allred

## Ingredients

Peaches (several)
1 cup sugar
1/2 cup water
3 Tbsp. cornstarch
3 mashed peaches
2 Tbsp. butter
### Pie Crust
2 cups flour
1 cup shortening
1 tsp. salt.
1 egg
1 Tbsp. vinegar
1/4 cup cold water

## Procedure

Peel and slice several peaches and fill a pre-baked pie crust.
Boil sugar, water, cornstarch, and 3 mashed peaches together for 5 minutes.
Add: 2 Tbsp. butter.
Still until butter is melted and pour mixture over peaches already in crust. Chill.
Top with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.
Making Crust:
Mix flour, shortening, and salt.
Crumble together until fine.
Beat together egg, vinegar, and cold water.
Dump egg mixture into flour mixture.
Roll out and place in pan. Bake at 400° for about 10 minutes.
