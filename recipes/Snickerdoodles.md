# Snickerdoodles

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

1 cup soft shortening or margarine
1 1/2 cup sugar
2 eggs
2 3/4 cup sifted flour
2 tsp. cream of tartar
1 tsp. baking soda
¼ tsp. salt

## Procedure

Cream butter, sugar, eggs until fluffy. Sift dry ingredients together then stir together. Roll mixture into balls the size of walnuts. Roll in sugar-cinnamon mixture. (2 Tbsp. sugar and 2 Tbsp. cinnamon) Bake at 400° for 8-10 minutes. Makes 5 dozen

## Notes

This is one of Jared's favorite cookies. Jared doesn’t care for a conventional birthday cake, so Amy made him a birthday ice cream pie with a crust made from crushed Snickerdoodles.
