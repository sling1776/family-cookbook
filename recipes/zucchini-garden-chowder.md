# Zucchini Garden Chowder

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 2 medium zucchini, diced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 tsp. parsley
- 1 tsp. basil
- 5 Tbsp. + 1 tsp. butter
- 5 Tbsp. + 1 tsp. flour
- 1 tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 3 cups water
- 3 tsp. chicken bouillon
- 4 tomatoes, diced
- 1 can evaporated milk
- 1 can of corn
- 4 Tbsp. parmesan cheese

## Procedure
1. Serves 3-4
2. Stir all ingredients together in a large pot. Cook on low heat until veggies are tender. Enjoy!
