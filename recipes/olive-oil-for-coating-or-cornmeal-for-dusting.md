# Olive oil for coating or cornmeal for dusting

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 4 Tbsp. Olive oil
- Salt
- ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Procedure
1. Punch down the risen dough, sprinkle it with rosemary, and knead it a few times. Divide dough in half.  For each focaccia, flatten one portion into an oval, and then roll it until it is about 10 inches long and 8 inches wide.
2. Place focaccia on a lightly oiled baking sheet or a pizza peel sprinkled with cornmeal.  Let rise for 20 minutes.  Heat the oven to 400°.  After the dough has risen, use your fingers to poke dimples all over it.  Brush half of the olive oil onto the surface of the dough.  Season with a sprinkling of salt.  Sprinkle on half of the Parmesan cheese.
3. Bake about 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden. Serve warm.
