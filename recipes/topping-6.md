# Topping:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1/4 cup white sugar
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 3 Tbsp. cocoa
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1 cup marshmallows for nuts
- 1/3 cup peanut butter and ½ cup nuts
- 1 pkg. Butterscotch chips for nuts

## Procedure
1. Mix ingredients and set aside for topping.
2. Make the chocolate cake.
3. Add:  ½ cups walnuts
4. Pour batter into 9x9 baking dish, sprinkle with the above topping
5. Pour 1 cup boiling water over all.
6. Bake 1 hour (careful not to burn edges) at 350°.  Variations:
