# Easy Chili Rellenos

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

2-7oz. cans whole green chili peppers, drained
Grate and sprinkle on top of chilies:
1 pound cheddar cheese
1 pound Jack cheese
6 eggs, well beaten
1 cup milk
½ cup flour
½ tsp. salt

## Procedure

Remove seeds from green chili peppers, rinse and arrange in pan (13x9)
Grate cheeses and sprinkle on top of peppers.
Combine eggs, milk, flour, and salt.
Pour egg mixture over peppers and cheese.
Bake at 350 for 35 - 40 min.
