# Quick Lasagna

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

1 lb. ground beef, browned and drained 
½ cup Hamburger Helper mix 
1 onion, chopped 
2 cups water 
16 oz. tomato sauce 
3 cups GF lasagna noodles, uncooked, broken in bits OR corn tortillas 
1/4 cup Parmesan cheese 
2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded

## Procedure

Combine all except mozzarella in large skillet. Bring to a boil, let simmer for 15 minutes or until noodles are cooked. (If using corn tortilla, add after 10 minutes and simmer slightly.) Top with mozzarella. Turn off heat and let cheese melt.
