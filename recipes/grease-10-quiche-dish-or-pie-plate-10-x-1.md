# Grease 10” quiche dish or pie plate (10 x 1 ½).

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1/8 tsp. pepper
- 2 cups milk
- ½ cup GF Quick Mix

## Procedure
1. Sprinkle bacon, cheese and onion in plate.
2. Beat remaining ingredients until smooth in a blender (15 second or on high for 1 min. with hand beater.)  Pour into plate.  Bake until knife inserted between center and edge comes out clean.  35 to 40 minutes.  Cool 5 minutes.         Makes 6 servings
