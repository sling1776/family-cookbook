# Creamy Blackberry Pie

Author: Becky Concoby

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup light sour cream
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1/3 cup flour
- dash of salt
- 1 bag of frozen blackberries (Wal-Mart brand is very good)
- 1- 9” pie shell

## Procedure
1. I served this at Thanksgiving at Tawna's one year and everyone seemed to love it.  It is so easy to make & one of my all time favorite pies.
