# Home-Style Syrup

## Author

Becky Concoby

## Ingredients

1 1/2 cups sugar
2/3 cup corn syrup
1/2 cup water
1 tsp. vanilla
5oz. evaporated milk
1/2 tsp. butter flavoring
1/2 tsp. almond extract

## Procedure

Boil first 4 ingredients for 2 minutes, stirring constantly. Remove from heat and add milk and flavorings.
