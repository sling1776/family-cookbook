# Best Gotta

Author: Have

## Ingredients
- 2 cups sugar
- 1/3 cup cocoa
- 4 eggs
- 1 ½ cups flour
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 1 ½ cups chopped nuts (optional)
- 1 2/3 cups powdered sugar
- ½ cup butter
- 1/3 cup cocoa
- 1/3 cup canned evaporated milk

## Procedure
1. Mix butter, sugar and cocoa together.  Stir in 4 eggs.  Add flour, vanilla, salt and nuts.  Stir.  Pour into a greased 1” deep baking sheet.  Bake at 350º for 20 minutes or until done.  Cover with 1 lb. marshmallows; bake for 3 minutes.  Add frosting made of:
