# Chuck Wagon Filled Rolls

Author: Becky Concoby

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1/2 cup chopped green pepper
- 1/2 cup chopped onion
- 1 small can mushrooms
- 1 cup chopped olives
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 2 cup grated cheese

## Procedure
1. French Rolls
2. Brown meat peppers and onions, drain. Stir in remaining ingredients.  Simmer 10 minutes.  Fill rolls with mixture.  Wrap each roll in foil and bake 20 minutes at 350°.
