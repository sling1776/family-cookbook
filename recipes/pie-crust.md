# Pie Crust

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1-1/4 cups flour
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1/3 cup shortening
- 4 to 5 Tbsp. very cold water (add a cube of ice)

## Procedure
1. Makes one 9-inch pie crust
2. Mix flour and salt. Cut in shortening until pieces are very small.  Add water 1 Tbsp. at a time, stirring quickly with fork.  Use as little water as you can to keep dough together.  Roll into a circle 1/4-inch thick.  Roll dough around rolling pin; transfer to pie pan.  Push to fit into pan. Cut about ½ inch larger than pan.  Turn edge under and crimp.  Prick bottom and sides with tines of a fork, if baking before filling.  Bake at 450° for 10-12 minutes.  Or use as unbaked crust.
