# Stroganoff

## Author

Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients

strip of round steak OR hamburger
1 med. onion, chopped
1 can cream of mushroom/cream of chicken
paprika
1 cup sour cream

## Procedure

Mix together & brown meat and onion
Season with: salt, pepper & garlic salt
Add cream of mushroom or cream of chicken soup and paprika
Let simmer
Just before serving add: 1 cup sour cream

## Notes

Serve over egg noodles.
