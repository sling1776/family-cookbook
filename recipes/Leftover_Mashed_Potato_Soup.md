# Leftover Mashed Potato Soup

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

2 Tbsp. butter
1/3 cup chopped onion
2 Tbsp. flour
1 cup mashed potatoes
1 cup water
2 ½ cups milk
½ cup grated cheese

## Procedure

Saute onions in butter. Cook slowly until yellow.
Add remaining ingredients
Heat slowly, but do not boil.
