# Sloppy Joes

## Author

Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients

1 lb. ground beef
1 lg. onion, chopped
1 bay leaf
1 Tbsp. worcestershire sauce
1 tsp. salt
3/4 tsp. curry powder
2/3 cup ketchup
2/3 cup tomato juice

## Procedure

Brown meat and onion. Add other ingredients and simmer 1½ to 2 hrs.

## Notes

serves 6-8
