# Spiced Hot Punch

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 6 quarts water
- 3 ½ cups sugar
- 2 sticks cinnamon
- 1 tsp. cloves
- 1 large can pineapple juice
- 1 small can orange juice
- 3/4 cup lemon juice
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 1 tsp. almond flavoring

## Procedure
1. Boil water, sugar and spices for 5 minutes then add the juices and warm to drinking temperature. Add flavorings. Better made the day before.
