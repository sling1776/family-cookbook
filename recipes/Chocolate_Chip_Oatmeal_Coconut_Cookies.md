# Chocolate Chip, Oatmeal, Coconut Cookies

## Author

Janine Hinkle

## Ingredients

1 cup margarine
½ cup brown sugar
½ cup sugar
2 tsp. baking soda
2 tsp. salt
4 cups flour
4 eggs 
2 tsp. hot water 
2 tsp. vanilla
4 cups quick oats
1 large bag chocolate chips
7oz. coconut

## Procedure

Add together: margarine, sugar, and brown sugar.
Then add baking soda, salt, flour.
Then add eggs, hot water, vanilla.
Then add quick oats chocolate chips and coconut.
Bake 8-10 minutes at 350°. Makes 4 to 5 dozen cookies.
