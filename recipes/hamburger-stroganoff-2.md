# Hamburger Stroganoff

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. margarine
- ½ cup minced onion
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 lb. lean ground beef
- 2 Tbsp. flour
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1/4 tsp. pepper
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 cup sour cream

## Procedure
1. Heat margarine in skillet over medium heat. Sauté onions and garlic until lightly browned. Add meat and brown. Drain fat; stir in flour, salt, and pepper. Cook 5 min. Stir in soup; simmer uncovered about 10 minutes, stirring occasionally. Stir in sour cream. Heat through, but do not boil. Serve over rice or egg noodles.
