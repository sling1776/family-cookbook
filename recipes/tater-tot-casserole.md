# Tater Tot Casserole

Author: Barbara & Bonnie

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. hamburger
- 1 medium onion
- 2 lbs. frozen tater tots
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- ½ cup finely chopped celery
- 1 qt. green beans

## Procedure
1. Brown hamburger with onion.  Drain off fat. Season to taste and spread in oblong casserole.  Spread beans on next, then celery.  Spread cream soup then top with tater tots.  Bake for 30 min at 350°.
