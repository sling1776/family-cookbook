# Diet Milk Shakes

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

1 cup skim milk
 ½ tsp. flavoring
 artificial sweetener
 2 ice cubes

## Procedure

Blend for about 30 seconds or until frothy. Then if desired add unsweetened frozen strawberries or any fresh fruit.

## Notes

“Grandpa Fay told me he thought 1% milk was just white water. I asked him what he thought skim milk was, and he said it wasn’t even worth considering. I guess he didn’t mention that to Grandma Grace.” -Amy Badger.
