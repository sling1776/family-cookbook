# Banana Cake

## Author

Laurie Fryatt

## Ingredients

### Cream: 
2/3 cup shortening
1 ½ cups bananas
½ cup buttermilk
2 eggs
2 ½ cups flour
1 2/3 cups sugar
1 ½ tsp. baking powder
1 tsp. baking soda
1 tsp. salt
2/3 cup chopped walnuts

## Procedure

Mix shortening, bananas, and buttermilk. Sift together dry ingredients. Add dry ingredients to banana mixture then add chopped walnuts. Bake 350° for 35 minutes in 9x13 pan.
