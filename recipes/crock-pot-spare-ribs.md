# Crock Pot Spare Ribs

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 4-5 lbs. pork boneless ribs
- 28 oz. bottle of Kraft Barbecue Sauce

## Procedure
1. Place ribs on a broiler rack and brown in a 400° oven for 15 minutes. Turn and brown on other side 10-15 minutes.  Cover bottom of crock pot with sauce.  Place ribs in pot and cover with remaining sauce. Cover and cook for 6-8 hours.  Serves 5-6.
