# Spudnuts

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- 2 cups mashed potatoes
- 7 Tbsp. yeast dissolved in 1 cup warm water and 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 4 eggs lightly beaten
- ½ cup shortening
- 3 cups scalded milk, cooled
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp. salt
- 12 cups flour

## Procedure
1. Cream shortening, sugar, salt and potatoes together.  Add other ingredients & mix.  Let rise until double in bulk.  Pat out and cut.  Place doughnuts on dish towel to rise the second time.  Fry in hot fat & glaze immediately.
