# Wheat/White Dutch Oven Rolls

Author: FFF Cookbook

## Ingredients
- 2 ½ cups warm water
- 2 Tbsp. sugar or honey
- 2 Tbsp. oil
- 1 1/2 tsp, salt
- 3 cups white flour
- 3 cups wheat flour
- 1 Tbsp. fast acting yeast, OR 2 Tbsp. regular

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients well.  Divide into 24 equal parts and arrange in an oiled 14 inch Dutch oven.  Let rise until double in size, about 25 minutes.  Bake with coals on top and bottom for approximately 12-14 minutes, and then continue baking with coals on top until golden brown.  Rotate the oven 1/4 turn once or twice while baking.  1/3 heat on bottom 2/3 heat on top.  Use 16 briquettes on top and 8 briquettes on bottom. Delicious served with honey- butter or fresh freezer strawberry jam.
