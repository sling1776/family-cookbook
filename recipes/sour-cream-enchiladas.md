# Sour Cream Enchiladas

Author: Sarah Manis

## Ingredients
- 2 cans cream of chicken soup
- 2 Tbsp. chopped green chilies
- 1 doz. tortillas
- 1 cup sour cream or cream cottage cheese
- 1 small onion grated
- 1 1/2 cup grated cheese

## Procedure
1. Heat soup and chilies.  Add sour cream.  Dip tortillas in hot fat and drain.  Put 2 Tbsp. of soup mixture in center of each tortilla.  Put in baking dish.  Pour remaining soup over.  Sprinkle on cheese and onion.  Cover dish and bake 350° for 20-30 min.
