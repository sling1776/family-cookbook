# Apple Pudding

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup shortening
- 1egg
- 1 cup flour
- 4 cups grated apples
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. cinnamon
- ½ tsp. nutmeg
- ½ cup nuts or more
- 2/3 cup Karo syrup
- 2/3 cup canned milk
- 2 cups sugar

## Procedure
1. Bake in a 350° oven about 35 to 45 minutes.  Serve with sauce
2. Bring to boil and add 1/4 cup butter
