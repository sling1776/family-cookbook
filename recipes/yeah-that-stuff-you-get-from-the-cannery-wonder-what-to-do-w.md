# (yeah that stuff you get from the cannery & wonder what to do with it),

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- ½ cup minced fresh parsley
- ½ cup salad oil
- 1 minced onion
- Juice of one lemon

## Procedure
1. Tomatoes, chopped
2. Lettuce, chopped
3. Directions:
4. Blend oil well into cooked wheat.  Add remaining ingredients, except for tomatoes and lettuce.  Preferably, let stand overnight to enhance flavors.  When ready to serve, mix in chopped tomatoes and lettuce of any desired amount.
5. Note: You can cook whole wheat in the crock pot by covering it with water and cooking on low over night or all day.
