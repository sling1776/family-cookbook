# Filling and topping:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 4 Tbsp. cinnamon
- 1 cup chopped walnuts or pecans

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 350°.  Cream sugar and butter.  Mix in sour cream.  Add eggs one at a time.  In a separate bowl mix the flour, baking powder, and baking soda.  Slowly add the flour mixture to the butter/egg mixture.  The dough will be a little stiff.  Spoon half the dough into a well-greased and floured 10-inch tube pan (bundt pan).  Mix together the topping ingredients.  Sprinkle 3/4 of the cinnamon/sugar mixture over the first half of the dough.  Add the remaining dough and sprinkle rest of topping.  Bake 1 hour.  Cool 1 hour, remove from pan.
