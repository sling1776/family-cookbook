# Black Beans

Author: Rachel Mitchell

## Ingredients
- 1 can (15oz.) black beans, rinsed & drained
- 1/3 cup tomato juice
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp. ground cumin
- 2 Tbsp. olive oil
- ½ tsp. salt
- 2 Tbsp. fresh chopped cilantro

## Procedure
1. In a non-stick skillet, cook garlic and cumin in olive oil over medium heat (until you can smell it).  Add beans, tomato juice and salt.  Continually stir until heated through.  Just before serving stir in cilantro.
