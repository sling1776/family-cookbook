# Ranch Chicken

Author: Grace Ann Skousen

## Ingredients
- 4-6 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 1 cup crushed Ritz cracker
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 pkt. dry ranch dressing mix

## Procedure
1. Melted butter
2. Wash chicken, dip in melted butter.  Mix crackers, cheese and ranch mix.  Coat chicken in mixture and bake at 350° for an hour in a glass pan covered with foil.
