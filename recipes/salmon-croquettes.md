# Salmon Croquettes

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 can salmon
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- 5 Tbsp. flour
- dash salt and pepper

## Procedure
1. Drain and clean salmon.  Save liquid and use to make 1 cup with milk.  Make white sauce with flour and liquid.  Cook sauce until thick and stir in fish.  Chill in shallow pan.  Shape into balls, dip balls into a beaten egg, and then roll in cornflake crumbs.  Fry in hot fat in deep fryer or frying pan.
