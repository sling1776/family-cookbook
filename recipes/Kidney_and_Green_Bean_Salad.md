# Kidney and Green Bean Salad

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

2 cans red kidney beans
2 cans whole green beans
2 cans yellow wax beans
2 cans garbanzo
1 large can pimento, diced
1 cup green stuffed olives
2 large green pepper, cut in thin rings
2 large sweet salad onions, cut into thin rings
1/4 cup salad oil
1/4 cup vinegar
1/4 cup sugar
### Dressing
1 cup brown sugar
1 cup catsup
1 cup oil
1 cup wine vinegar
1 tsp. salt
pepper
1 clove garlic crushed
1/4 tsp cloves (optional)
1 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce
several dashes of Tabasco

## Procedure

Drain all canned ingredients. Put in large container and mix above ingredients together.
Then mix in a quart jar the dressing ingredients
Shake and put over bean mixture. Put in jars and store in fridge and just serve.

## Notes
