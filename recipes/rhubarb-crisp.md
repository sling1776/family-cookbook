# Rhubarb Crisp

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 4 cups cut up fresh rhubarb
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 ½ - 2 cups sugar, depending on tartness
- 3/4 cup flour
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1/3 cup butter

## Procedure
1. Heat oven to 350°.  Layer rhubarb in ungreased pan, sprinkle with salt.  Measure sugar, flour and cinnamon.  Add butter and mix thoroughly until crumbly.  Sprinkle over rhubarb.  Bake 40-50 minutes.  Serve with light cream.
