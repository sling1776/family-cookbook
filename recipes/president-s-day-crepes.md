# President’s Day Crepes

Author: Clint Fielding

## Ingredients
- ½ cup flour
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 egg
- 1 1/2 tsp oil
- 1/8 tsp. salt
- 1/3 of a can of cherry pie filling
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar

## Procedure
1. Beat first 5 ingredients with an electric mixer till well blended.  heat a lightly greased small skillet.  Remove skillet from heat and spoon in about 2 Tbsps. batter.  Lift and tilt skillet to spread batter.  Return to heat.  Brown on one side only.  Invert pan over a plate and remove crepe.  Fill crepe with cherries, fold, and sprinkle top with powdered sugar.
