# Chicken Parisienne

## Author

Delsa Laxton

## Ingredients

8 chicken breasts
2 cans cream of mushroom soup
1 tsp. chicken bouillon added to 1 cup water
2 cups sour cream
½ cup flour

## Procedure

Put chicken in 4 quart pot. Pour soup and bouillon mixture over top. Bring to boil. Reduce heat to medium low and simmer for at least 1 hour. Remove chicken. Add sour cream, and flour.
Mix in the soup mixture. Cook 30 minutes or until thickened. Serve over cooked spaghetti and chicken breast.
