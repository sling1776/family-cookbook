# “Pita-Pita-Patter Goes my Heart” Pockets

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

Sausage Patties
Pita Bread (heart shape)
Diced tomato
Diced cucumber
Diced sweet onion
Shredded lettuce
### Sauce:
1/2 cup grated cucumbers, drain in a mesh colander 
1 cup plain yogurt
¼ cup sour cream
1/2 tsp. garlic powder
1 tsp. sugar

## Procedure

Use the recipes in this database to make sausage patties and Pita bread shaped like hearts.
Cut the pitas open and add 2 small heart shaped sausages, diced tomato, diced cucumber, diced sweet onion, and shredded lettuce.
Combine sauce ingredients and add to pitas.

## Notes

Enjoy the loving looks from your loved ones as their hearts go “pita-pita-patter” while they eat their tasty pockets.
