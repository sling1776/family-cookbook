# Carmel Turtles

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1 cup pecan halves
- 36 light carmels
- ½ cup baking milk chocolate, melted

## Procedure
1. Grease cookie sheet. Arrange pecans (flat side down) in clusters of 4. Place 1 carmel on each cluster. Heat in 325° oven until carmel softens (4-8 minutes). Flatten carmel with a buttered spatula. Cool slightly and remove to wax paper. Swirl melted chocolate on top.
