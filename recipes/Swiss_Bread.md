# Swiss Bread

## Author

Chrissy Probst

## Ingredients

### Mix and let work
2 Tbsp. yeast
1 cup lukewarm water
3/4 cup sugar
### Add
1 1/2 cups powdered milk
4 cups warm water
2 Tbsp. salt
4 eggs
1/2 cup butter
about 10 cups flour (can mix wheat/white)

## Procedure

Mix and let raise about 1 hour. Punch down and let rest 5-10 minutes. Mold into loaves or braid it. Bake at 350° for 30 minutes.

## Notes

This is the Swiss Bread recipe that is used for the bread sold at Swiss Days in Midway. We got to be a part of making bread for Swiss Days when we lived in Midway, Utah. I find that if I keep my yeast in the freezer, it lasts longer. This recipe makes wonderful scones as well.
