# Sunshine Chicken

## Author

Grace Ann Skousen

## Ingredients

8 thighs and 8 drumsticks (or other pieces)
1 tsp. salt
1 tsp. basil
1/4 tsp. pepper
½ cup soy sauce
½ cup catsup
1/4 cup honey
1/4 cup oil
garlic to taste

## Procedure

Sprinkle chicken with salt, pepper and basil. Place in baking dish. Mix rest of ingredients in bowl. Pour over chicken, save some for basting. Bake 350° for 1 hour.

## Notes

This was our favorite recipe in Israel. All the meat in Israel is Kosher. We found Kosher beef to taste very different than our beef...so we used a lot of Kosher chicken.
