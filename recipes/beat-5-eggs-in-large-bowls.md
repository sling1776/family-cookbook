# Beat 5 eggs in large bowls

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 8 oz. grated cheese
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- ½ cup milk
- 1 (4oz.) diced green chilies
- 1/4 cup flour
- ½ tsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. Add 1 ½ cups sliced olives
2. Mix. Pour into baking dish.  Sprinkle with paprika.  Bake 325° for 30 min.
