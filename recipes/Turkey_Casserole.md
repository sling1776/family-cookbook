# Turkey Casserole

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

2 cups diced turkey, chicken or ham
2 Tbsp. diced onion
1 tsp. salt
1 cup slivered almonds
2 cups diced celery
2 cups cooked rice
6 hard cooked eggs
2 cans cream of chicken soup
3/4 cup evaporated milk
1 ½ cup mayonnaise

## Procedure

Mix all ingredients (except chicken soup, evaporated milk, and mayonnaise) together.
Combine chicken soup, evaporated milk, and mayonnaise.
Mix with turkey mixture. Put in 9x13”pan. Top casserole with ½ cup bread crumbs and 2 Tbsp. melted margarine. Bake 375° for 30 minutes.
