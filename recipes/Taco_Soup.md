# Taco Soup

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

1 lb. ground beef or sausage
1 onion chopped

1 quart diced tomatoes
1-15oz. can kidney beans with juice
1-15oz. can black beans with juice
1 can whole kernel corn with juice
1 pkg. taco seasoning

## Procedure

Brown and drain ground beef and onion.
Add to crockpot with the rest of the ingredients.
Stir together and cook on high for 2 to 3 hours OR low for 4 to 6 hours.
