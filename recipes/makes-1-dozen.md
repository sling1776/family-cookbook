# Makes 1 dozen

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. yellow summer squash, chopped
- 1/2 cup butter, melted
- 1 egg
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1/2 tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. In a saucepan, bring squash to a boil. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 5 minutes. Drain the squash and mash it in a bowl. Add the butter and egg. In another bowl, sift together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Stir in the squash mixture. Fill greased muffin cups 3/4 full and bake at 375° for 20 minutes.
