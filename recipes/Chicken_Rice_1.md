# Chicken & Rice

## Author

Janine Hinkle

## Ingredients

4 lbs skinned, deboned chicken pieces
1 can cream of chicken soup
2 ½ Tbsp. onion
1 Tbsp. parsley
1 tsp. salt
½ tsp. thyme
½ tsp. celery salt
1 1/3 cup water
1 1/2 cup Minute Rice

## Procedure

Bread about 4 lbs skinned, deboned chicken pieces.
Boil together for 10 minutes the following:
- cream of chicken soup
- onion
- parsley
- salt
- thyme
- celery salt
- water
After boiling add minute rice
Add in layers in casserole dish
½ soup mixture / chicken / ½ soup mixture
Bake at 375° for 30 minutes.
