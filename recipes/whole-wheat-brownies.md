# Whole Wheat Brownies

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp. margarine
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 egg
- 2 squares melted chocolate*
- 1 cup sifted whole wheat flour
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1/2 cup canned milk
- 1 cup chopped nuts
- 6 Tb. cocoa powder + 2 Tb. shortening = 2 squares chocolate

## Procedure
1. Pre-heat oven to 350°. Cream together sugar, butter and vanilla. Add egg and beat until light and fluffy. Add melted chocolate, sifted dry ingredients and canned milk. Mix well and stir in nuts. Pour into a buttered 9 x 13 pan and bake for 30 - 35 minutes.
