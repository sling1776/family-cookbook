# Magic Meatloaf

Author: Grace Ann Skousen

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1 green pepper, chopped
- 1 onion, chopped
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 cup cracker/breadcrumbs
- 1 egg
- 1 pkg. Brown gravy mix
- 1 cup milk
- 4 small potatoes, cut up

## Procedure
1. Mix everything together except potatoes and shape into loaf. Place in crock-pot with potatoes around the sides of the loaf. Cook on low 8-10 hours of high 4-5 hours.
