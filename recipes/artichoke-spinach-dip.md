# Artichoke & Spinach Dip

Author: Kadie Allred

## Ingredients
- 1 can drained artichokes, chopped
- 1 pkg. frozen spinach, chopped
- 8oz. cream cheese
- 8oz. sour cream
- 3/4 cup shredded parmesan cheese
- 8oz. Monterey Jack Cheese
- 1/2 onion finely chopped & sautéed

## Procedure
1. red pepper flakes, to taste
2. mozzarella cheese, to taste
3. Cook spinach according to directions on pkg. and drain.  Mix with artichokes and remaining ingredients.  Put in greased 1 qt. dish and bake at 325° for 20-30 minutes.  Top with  mozzarella cheese for last few minutes.
