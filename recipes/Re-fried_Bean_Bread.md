# Re-fried Bean Bread

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

2 cups lukewarm milk
4 1/2 tsp. yeast OR 2 packages
2 cups re-fried beans
2 Tbsp. sugar
2 Tbsp. salt
2 Tbsp. shortening
4-5 cups flour

## Procedure

Dissolve yeast and sugar in warm milk. Mix beans, salt, shortening and 1 tsp. flour. Add yeast and milk. Mix well. Add flour a little at a time. Knead dough. Place in a greased bowl. Cover with a damp cloth. Let rise until doubled. Punch down, and form into 2 loaves. Bake at 350° for 30-40 min.
