# Sauce: 2/3 cup sugar

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup catsup
- 1/3 cup pineapple juice
- ½ cup vinegar
- 2 Tbsp. soy sauce
- garlic powder
- 1 small green pepper, cut in strips
- ½ medium onion, thinly sliced

## Procedure
1. Cook sauce over medium heat.  Stir in 2 Tbsp. cornstarch mixed well with 1/3 cup water.
2. Add 1 cup well drained pineapple chunks & a few drops of red food colorings.  Mix well and serve immediately over hot cooked rice
