# Poppy Seed Dressing

Author: Grace Ann Skousen

## Ingredients
- ½ cup oil
- ½ cup sugar
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 2 Tbsp. sesame seed
- 1 Tbsp. poppy seed
- 1 ½ tsp. minced onions
- 1/4 tsp. Worcestershire sauce
- 1/4 tsp. paprika

## Procedure
1. Mix well. Pour over spinach leaves, walnuts and mandarin oranges.
