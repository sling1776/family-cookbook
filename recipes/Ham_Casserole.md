# Ham Casserole

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

### Noodles
5 cups noodles
10 cups water
1½ tsp. salt
### Sauce
2 cups diced ham
1 small onion diced
2 Tbsp. margarine
1 ½ cups cheese
1 ½ cups canned milk
2 cans cream of mushroom soup
½ cup water

## Procedure

Cook noodles in water and salt until tender, drain, set aside
Sauté together ham, onions and margarine.
Add cheese, milk cream of mushroom soup and 1/2 cup water.
Top with bread crumbs or potato chips. Bake at 350° until heated through.
