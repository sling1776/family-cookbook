# Vegetable & Meat Dish

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

1 lb. ground beef
1 med. onion, chopped
1 tsp. salt & Pepper to taste
2 cups water
1 med cabbage
2 or 3 potatoes

## Procedure

Brown ground beef and onion in a heavy saucepan and drain
Add salt & pepper and water
Cut cabbage in 8 pieces (add)
Simmer for about 15 minutes. Do not boil.
Add potatoes (cut small)
Boil until tender. Add more water is needed. This depends on how juicy you like this dish. A grated raw carrot may be added if you like for the last 10 minutes. It looks pretty.
