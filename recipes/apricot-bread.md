# Apricot Bread

Author: Angela Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 3-oz orange Jell-O
- 1 lemon Duncan Hines cake mix
- 6 eggs
- 3 Tbsp. flour
- 1 cup apricot nectar
- ½ cup oil

## Procedure
1. Bake at 350° for 30 minutes. While hot from oven pour sauce over bread and cool in pan
2. Sauce:  ½ cup lemon juice, ½ cup orange juice 1 cup powdered sugar. Serve chilled. Makes 4 small loaves.
