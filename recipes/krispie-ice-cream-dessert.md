# Krispie Ice Cream Dessert

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 2 cups Rice Krispies
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup coconut
- ½ cup melted butter

## Procedure
1. Stir together and put ½ mixture in bottom of pan.  Spread ½ gallon softened ice cream oven the mixture.  Sprinkle the rest of the Rice Krispies mixture over the top and freeze.  Top with Danish dessert and fresh raspberries.
