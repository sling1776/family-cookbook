# Crescent Rolls

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. yeast
- 3/4 cup warm water
- 3/4 cup buttermilk or sour milk
- 3/4 cup sour cream
- 5 ½ cups flour
- ½ cup shortening
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 2 tsp. salt
- cups flour and rest of ingredients. Blend on

## Procedure
1. Dissolve yeast in warm water.  Add 2 ½
2. low.  Stir in remaining flour and knead for 5
3. min. Divide dough into 3 parts.  Roll dough
4. into 12 inch circle about ½ inch thick.
5. Spread with soft butter.  Cut into 16 wedges.  Roll up, beginning at rounded edge.  Place rolls with point underneath, on greased baking sheet.  Curve slightly, brush with butter.  Let rise 1 hour before baking.  Heat oven to 375°.  Bake rolls 20-25 min.  Makes 48 rolls.
