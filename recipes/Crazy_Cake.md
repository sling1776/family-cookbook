# Crazy Cake

## Author

Ruby Fielding

## Ingredients

3 cups flour
6 Tbsp. cocoa
1 tsp. salt
2 cups sugar
2 tsp. soda
Mix above ingredients. Place in ungreased 9x13 pan. Make three wells or holes. Pour into the wells:
3/4 cup oil	
2 tsp. vanilla
2 Tbsp. vinegar

## Procedure

Pour over all 2 cups of cold water then mix everything together with a fork. Bake 350° for 30-35 minutes.
