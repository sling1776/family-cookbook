# Baked Macaroni & Cheese

Author: Grandma Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 cup cooked macaroni
- 1 cup soft bread crumbs
- 1 tsp. chopped onion
- 1 cup grated cheese
- 1 1/2 cups milk
- 2 eggs, well beaten
- 1 tsp chopped green pepper
- 1 Tbsp butter
- salt, pepper & paprika (to taste)

## Procedure
1. Combine all ingredients season to taste
2. pour into well-oiled baking dish.
3. Bake at 375 degrees for 45 minutes.
