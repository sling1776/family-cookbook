# Stupid Cookies

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1- 16oz. bag of Fritos
- 1 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1 cup white corn syrup
- 1 cup sugar

## Procedure
1. Heat in pan sugar & syrup until the sugar is dissolved.  Add the peanut butter and mix well.  Pour over Fritos and stir until well covered.  Spoon onto a cookie sheet and allow to cool.
