# Portuguese Rice

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

2 cups of rice, rinsed off
4 cups of water
1/2 cup chopped onions
1/4 cup shredded carrots
2 Tbsp. olive oil

## Procedure

Brown the onions and carrots in the olive oil. Then add the rice and cook until slightly brown. Add the water and bring to a boil. Turn heat to low and let cook 20 minutes. Serve hot with Feijoada.

## Notes

This is the typical side dish for almost every meal in Portugal
