# Sauce:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 8 oz. tomato sauce
- 3/4 cup ketchup
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 2 Tbsp sugar

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 350°.  Cover cookie sheet w/ foil and spray.  Mix sauce. Dip meatballs in sauce and place on foil.  Bake 30 minutes.  Brush balls with sauce.  Bake another 15 minutes.  Serve with white rice.
