# Soft Pretzels

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

1 Tbsp yeast
2 Tbsp oil
1 cup warm water
2 tsp sugar
1 tsp salt
2 ½ cups flour
1 egg white beaten with 1 tsp water
1 Tbsp coarse salt

## Procedure

Combine yeast, warm water, sugar, salt and oil. Let sit for five minutes. Stir in flour and knead for 6-8 minutes until dough is smooth and elastic, adding more flour if dough is sticky. Preheat oven to 400°. Grease baking sheet. Cut dough into 8-10 equal pieces. On a lightly floured surface, roll each piece into a long rope about 15 inches long and twist into pretzel shape. Allow to rise 5-10 minutes. Meanwhile, bring a pan of water to a boil. One at a time place a pretzel in boiling water for 30 seconds. Remove, drain on cooling rack. Brush with egg white and water mixture. Sprinkle with coarse salt, sesame seeds or cinnamon & sugar if desired. Bake on greased baking sheet at 400° for 10-15 minutes.

## Notes

We fell in love with these big pretzels while living in New Jersey. Add a squirt of mustard or melt a slice of cheese on one.
