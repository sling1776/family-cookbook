# Grape Salad

## Author

Kadie Allred

## Ingredients

8oz. cool whip
8oz. cream cheese
8oz. sour cream
½ cup brown sugar
Almonds (sliced or slivered)
Grapes (red)

## Procedure

Mix cream cheese & sour cream first. Mix in cool whip. Add sugar, almonds & grapes. The amount of almonds & grapes is up to you.
