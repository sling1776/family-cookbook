# Creamed Soup Base

## Author

Barbara Olsen - modified from Bette Hagman's recipe

## Ingredients

1 cup dry milk powder
1 cup white rice flour OR 3/4 cup corn starch
2 Tbsp. dehydrated minced onions
½ tsp. pepper
½ tsp. salt
3 Tbsp. GF Chicken or Vegetable Bouillon or Soup Base

## Procedure

Combine all ingredients and mix well. Store in an airtight container. This is the equivalent of 8 or 9 cans of soup.

## Notes

Note: Western Family brand cream soups are GF.
