# Fudge in a Bag

Author: Sarah Manis

## Ingredients
- 4oz. cream cheese, room temperature
- 1/4 cup butter
- ½ cup powdered milk
- ½ cup cocoa
- ½ cup powdered sugar.

## Procedure
1. In a gallon size ziploc bag mix the dry ingredients.  (Make sure the powdered milk has no lumps).  Add the cream cheese and butter. Take turns kneading the baggie until it is well mixed.  Takes a while for this to happen.  When it is all mixed, pass the baggie for everyone to eat.
