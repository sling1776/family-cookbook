# Sauce: 1

Author: 8oz cream cheese, softened

## Ingredients
- 1-can marshmallow crème

## Procedure
1. Blend together and put on cooled, baked brownie crust. Garnish with fresh fruit toppings of your choice: such as strawberries, raspberries, blueberries, bananas, etc.
2. Serve at room temperature.  Refrigerate leftovers if any survive.
