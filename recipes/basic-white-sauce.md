# Basic White Sauce

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 1 stick butter
- ½ cup flour
- 1 tsp. dried parsley
- 1 quart milk
- 1/4 tsp. pepper
- 1 tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. Knowing how to make this sauce can help you make all kinds of creamed soups, scalloped dishes and gravies.  You'll find many ways to use it and vary it.
2. In sauce pan melt butter.  Add flour and seasoning.  Stir into paste.  Heat, stirring constantly to cook flour.  This takes away the floury taste.  Remove from heat to add the milk.  Return to heat and stir constantly
3. while sauce thicken.  DO NOT BOIL.
