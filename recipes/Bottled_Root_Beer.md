# Bottled Root Beer

## Author

Grandma Walker

## Ingredients

1 bottle root beer extract
 4 lbs. sugar
 4 ¾ gallons lukewarm water
 ½ yeast cake

## Procedure

Pour over 4 lbs. sugar. Mix well. Dissolve this mixture in 4 ¾ gallons of lukewarm water. Put ½ yeast cake in a cup of lukewarm water and let stand five minutes. If weather is over 90° use less yeast if under 70° double the quantity of yeast. Add yeast to mixture and stir well. Bottle immediately. Fill bottles to within ½ inch of the top. Place bottles on their side. Cover so draft can’t hit them.

## Notes

Those who had the opportunity to drink some of Grandma’s Root Beer will appreciate the memories.
 Should be ready in two or three days. Keep them in a cool place.
