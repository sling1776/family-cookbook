# Microwave Brownies

## Author

Tiffanie Olsen

## Ingredients

½ cup soft margarine
1 cup sugar
1 tsp. vanilla
3/4 cup flour
1/4 cup cocoa
½ tsp. salt
2 eggs

## Procedure

Mix together margarine, sugar, and vanilla. Then add flour, cocoa, salt, and eggs.
Stir until smooth. Put in greased glass 9x9” pan. Microwave 7 minutes.
