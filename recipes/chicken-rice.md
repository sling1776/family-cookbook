# Chicken & Rice

Author: Barbara & Bonnie

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups raw rice
- 3 cups cold water
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 3-4 lb fryer
- 1 pkg. onion soup mix
- 1 1/2 cup grated cheese

## Procedure
1. Spread rice in bottom of 9x13” pan. Sprinkle 1 cup cheese over rice.  Place chicken pieces over rice, salt and pepper. Sprinkle onion soup mix on chicken.  Beat chicken soup until smooth and spread over chicken. Pour in cold water. Bake uncovered at 350° for 1 hour. Remove from oven sprinkle with remaining cheese. Cover with foil and bake ½ hour more.
