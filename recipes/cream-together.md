# Cream Together:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1/2 cup shortening
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1½ tsp. vanilla
- 2 cups whole wheat flour
- 1½ to 2 cups oatmeal, pulverized in blender
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 24 oz. chocolate chips

## Procedure
1. Add: 2 eggs
2. Mix into above:
3. Bake at 375° for 10-12 minutes. (Make sure you take them out of the oven before they look done or they will be too crispy.)
