# Finger Paint

Author: Becky Concoby

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup cornstarch
- 1 1/3 cup boiling water
- 1/2 cup soap flakes
- 1 Tbsp. glycerin

## Procedure
1. Mix starch with enough cold water to form a smooth paste.  Add boiling water and cook paste until glossy.  While mixture is still warm stir in the soap flakes.  Allow it to cool then add the glycerin.  Stir in desired color.  Pour into jars. It will keep for about a week.
