# Pizza Pasta

## Author

Stephen Badger

## Ingredients

1 pkg. tri-colored noodles, cooked
2 sticks string cheese, sliced
20 slices of pepperoni, diced
1 small can sliced olives

## Procedure

Mix all ingredients and enjoy.

## Notes

I tell my family that I hate salads, but this is one that I like because I don’t put any dressing on it. You can put Italian dressing on it, but then I would think that was yucky!!
Stephen says: If you decided to add onions or other vegetables in this pasta it would be a bad idea. Very yucky!
