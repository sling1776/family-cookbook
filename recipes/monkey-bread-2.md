# Monkey Bread

Author: Suzanne Reber

## Ingredients
- ½ cup butter
- ½ cup brown sugar
- ½ tsp. cinnamon
- 8-10 frozen Rhodes rolls

## Procedure
1. Melt butter, add brown sugar and cinnamon. Arrange rolls in single layer in bread pan.  Pour butter mixture over rolls.  Cover pan with saran wrap sprayed with Pam.  Let rise 3-4 hours.  Bake at 300° for 20-25 minutes. Cool 10 minutes and turn out on waxed paper.
