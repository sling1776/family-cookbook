# Makes 6 qts.

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 6 eggs
- 3 ½ cups sugar
- 3/4 tsp. salt
- 2 Tbsp. vanilla
- 2 pints cream

## Procedure
1. Add 7 ½ cups milk
2. Beat eggs then gradually add sugar.  Continue to beat until mixture is very stiff.  Add remaining ingredients and mix thoroughly.  Freeze - use 25 lbs ice and 3 ½ cups rock salt.  To pack. Drain off water.  Take dasher out and cover top (inside lid) with wax paper.  Push down ice & refill freezer with cracked ice & layer of salt.  Cover w/ newspaper or old quilt.
