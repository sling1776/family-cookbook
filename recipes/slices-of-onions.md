# Slices of onions

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 small tomato, diced
- Salt and pepper to taste
- ¼ to ½ cup shredded cheese

## Procedure
1. Cook zucchini, squash and onions in a little water on low. When the squashes are tender, add tomatoes and sprinkle with grated cheese.  Cover, let cheese melt and serve.
