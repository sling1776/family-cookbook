# Mormon Miracle Pageant Marinade

Author: Trish Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 can sprite
- ½ cup oil
- ½ cup soy sauce
- ½ tsp. horseradish
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed

## Procedure
1. Dump in bag.  Add chicken, steaks or turkey tenders.  Marinate over night.  Grill, broil or bake. Note:  Add ½ cup Worcestershire sauce
