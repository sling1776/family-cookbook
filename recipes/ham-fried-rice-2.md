# Ham Fried Rice

Author: Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients
- 3 cups cooked rice
- 3 Tbsp. margarine
- 3-4 small green onions
- ¼ to ½ cup chopped ham
- 3 eggs

## Procedure
1. soy sauce
2. Melt margarine in frying pan, then add green onions and stir fry until tender.  Add chopped ham and fry until light brown.  Scramble in eggs, cooking until eggs are dry.  Remove from heat and add rice.  Stir together until well blended.  Add soy sauce to taste.  Let stand about 15 minutes to blend flavors.
