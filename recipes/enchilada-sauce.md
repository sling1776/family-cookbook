# Enchilada Sauce

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2 cans tomato sauce
- 1 tsp. basil
- 1 tsp. chili powder
- 1 tsp. oregano

## Procedure
1. Mix and simmer.  Cool completely and can freeze for later.
2. When ready to serve enchiladas, place in 9X13 pan, smother in sauce, and put grated cheese on top.  Bake for 20-35 minutes and enjoy!
