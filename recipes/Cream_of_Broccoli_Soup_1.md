# Cream of Broccoli Soup

## Author

Becky Concoby

## Ingredients

3 cups chopped broccoli
3 Tbsp. butter
1/4 cup chopped onion
1 celery spear
1 cup sliced mushrooms
4 oz. Swiss cheese
1/4 cup Parmesan cheese
### White Sauce
6 Tbsp. butter
6 Tbsp. flour
1 Tbsp. chicken bouillon powder
½ tsp. seasoning salt
1/4 tsp. pepper
1 pt. half & half
2 ½ cups milk

## Procedure

Make white sauce
Sauté: butter, onion and celery spear
Steam Broccoli.
Mix all above together with 1 cup sliced mushrooms,
4 oz. Swiss cheese and 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese.
