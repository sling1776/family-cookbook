# Variation: Carmel Sauce: Bonnie Chantry

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup Karo syrup

## Procedure
1. Boil until thread spins (lift a small amount of sauce, should look like a hair spinning down)
2. Add:  2 Tbsp. butter, dash of salt, ½ tsp. vanilla
3. and 1 cup canned milk
4. Stir & pour over each serving of pudding.
