# Potato Leek Soup

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

1 cup butter
2 lb. potatoes, diced
4 carrots, grated
2 leeks, minced
3 cups flour
1 pt. heated milk
salt & pepper
a few sprigs of parsley

## Procedure

Melt butter. Add leeks, carrots, potatoes and flour and still until all is stuck together. Add heated milk slowly. Add seasonings and simmer until veggies are tender. (If more milk is desired, be sure it is heated first.)

## Notes

via Sister Long. This recipe came from a sister missionary in Greece who was from England and had been to a gourmet cooking school. (It seems like a backwards way to make soup but it was wonderful every time she made it.)
