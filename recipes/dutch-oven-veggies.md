# Dutch Oven Veggies

Author: Gina Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 heads cauliflower
- 2 bunches broccoli
- 1 large bag baby carrots

## Procedure
1. Use 14 inch Dutch oven.  Steam vegetables in 1 1/2 cups water - drain.  Add 2 cans cream of chicken soup.  Spread 1 cup cheddar cheese and 1 pound American cheese over top.  Put lid on and warm until cheese in melted.  Enjoy!
