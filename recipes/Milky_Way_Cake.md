# Milky Way Cake

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

8 Milky War Bars, chopped
1 cup pecans 
1 cup margarine
Cook over low heat, set aside to cool.
Mix the following:
2 cups sugar
4 eggs
2 tsp. vanilla
1 tsp. baking soda
2 ½ cups flour
1¼ cups buttermilk or sour cream
### Milky Way Cake Frosting: 
1 8oz. cream cheese
1 2 lb. box powdered sugar
1 tsp. vanilla
½ cup butter
dash of salt

## Procedure

Beat two minutes, fold in candy mixture. Bake 350° 30-45 minutes.
Frosting:
Mix well.
