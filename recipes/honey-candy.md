# Honey Candy

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Cook honey (any amount you have) to hard ball stage.  Set off stove add a pinch of soda.  Pour onto a buttered platter (don't scrape pan).  Cool enough to handle.  Put butter on hands and pull like taffy.  Cut into pieces.  DO NOT STIR while cooking.
