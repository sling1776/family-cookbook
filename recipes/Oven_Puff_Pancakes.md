# Oven Puff Pancakes

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

4 Tbsp. butter or margarine
2/3 cup GF quick mix
4 eggs
2/3 cup milk

## Procedure

Preheat oven to 450°
Divide the butter between two 9 inch pie plates.
Put in preheated oven to melt butter and heat pans. In a blender, mix for 15 - 20 seconds GF mix, eggs and milk
Pour into pie plates. Bake for 18 minutes, until puffy and browned. Do not open oven while cooking. Serve with syrup or fruit topping.
