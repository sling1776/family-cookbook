# Chocolate Teddy Bears

Author: Tiffanie Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2/3 cup margarine
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 2 eggs
- ½ tsp. baking soda
- ½ cup cocoa
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 2½ cup flour

## Procedure
1. Mix well and refrigerate over night, Dough will be stiff.  Form balls for head, body, arms legs, nose and ears.  Press together and place on a greased cookie sheet. Bake 6-8 minutes at 325°.
