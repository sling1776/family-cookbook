# Pickled Beets

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cups water
- 2 cups vinegar
- 1 tsp cloves
- 1 tsp allspice
- 1 Tbsp. cinnamon

## Procedure
1. Cook beets until tender (amount 30 minutes)
2. Immediately put in cold water.  Cut off tops and bottoms and skins.  Cut beets into bit sized chunks or slices.  Fill bottles (pint size). Bring to a boil.
3. Pour over beets in heated bottles
4. Place lids on that have been in boiling water and screw on bands finger tight. Process pints 10 minutes in a boiling bath canner.
