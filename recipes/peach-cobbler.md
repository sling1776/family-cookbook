# Peach Cobbler

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 qt. bottle peaches
- ½ tsp. cinnamon
- 3 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1 1/4 cups flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. salt
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1/3 cup shortening
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Procedure
1. Heat oven to 375°.  Mix peaches, cinnamon & cornstarch in cake pan.  Put in warming oven.  Measure all ingredients into large mixer bowl.  Blend ½ minute on low speed, scraping bowl occasionally.  Put into pan, over the warmed peaches.  Bake 40-50 minutes or until cake is done.  Serve warm with canned milk or whipped cream.
