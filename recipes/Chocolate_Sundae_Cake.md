# Chocolate Sundae Cake

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

### Cake
2 cups GF Basic Cake mix 
1/4 cup cocoa powder
1 tsp. vanilla
1 egg, slightly beaten
3/4 cup ice cold water
½ cups walnuts
### Topping
1 tsp. vanilla
1/4 cup white sugar
½ cup brown sugar
3 Tbsp. cocoa
1/4 tsp. salt
1 cup boiling water

## Procedure

Mix ingredients for topping (except for boiling water) and set aside.
Stir cake ingredients until well blended.
Pour batter into 9x9 baking dish, sprinkle with the above topping
Pour 1 cup boiling water over all.
Bake 1 hour (careful not to burn edges) at 350°.

## Notes

Variations:
1 cup marshmallows for nuts
1/3 cup peanut butter and ½ cup nuts
1 pkg. Butterscotch chips for nuts
