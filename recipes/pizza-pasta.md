# Pizza Pasta

Author: Stephen Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. tri-colored noodles, cooked
- 2 sticks string cheese, sliced
- 20 slices of pepperoni, diced
- 1 small can sliced olives

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients and enjoy.
2. Stephen says: If you decided to add onions or other vegetables in this pasta it would be a bad idea. Very yucky!
