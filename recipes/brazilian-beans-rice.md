# Brazilian Beans & Rice

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 6 cups pinto beans, washed & soaked overnight
- 1 large onion chopped
- 2 Tbsp. salt
- 1/4 tsp. black pepper
- 2 Tbsp. oil
- 3 cups rice            1 large onion
- 3/4 cup oil             3 tsp salt
- 6 cups boiling water

## Procedure
1. Cook beans in water it has soaked in with other ingredients.  Add water as needed.  When beans are tender mash 5 or 6 times with potato masher and continue to simmer, making a rich bean sauce.  Serve generously over hot fluffy rice.
2. Sauté onion and rice in oil 5 minutes.  Add boiling water and salt.  Cook covered for 20 minutes.
