# Sloppy Joes

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

2 lbs. hamburger
green pepper
celery
onion
1 can tomato soup
1 Tbsp. vinegar
1 ½ cups catsup
1 Tbsp. sugar
salt and pepper to taste

## Procedure

Cook hamburger, green pepper, celery, and onion. Drain and add rest of ingredients.

## Notes

Serve on hamburger buns
