# Ginger’s Ranch Dressing

## Author

Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients

1 cup mayonnaise
1 cup buttermilk
½ tsp. garlic salt
½ tsp. onion salt
1-2 Tbsp. chopped fresh parsley
8 oz. cream cheese (optional)

## Procedure

Put all ingredients in a blender and blend.

## Notes

Note: You can add up to 8 oz. of cream cheese to the thickness you like.
