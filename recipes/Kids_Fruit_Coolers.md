# Kid’s Fruit Coolers

## Author

Alice Chantry

## Ingredients

1 banana
 6 strawberries
 10 grapes
 8 cubes ice
 Sprite

## Procedure

Blend fruit in blender, then add ice a couple at a time, till they are all crushed. Put some in cups then add Sprite and serve.
