# Bisquick and its recipe. For the gravy use:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 3-4 Tbsp. bacon or sausage grease, shortening or butter.
- 1/4 cup flour

## Procedure
1. Stir and cook ‘til a little bit browned
2. Add 2-3 cups milk
3. Cook on medium heat until thickened.  (Start gravy then start biscuits.)  Also good on bread broken in small pieces.
