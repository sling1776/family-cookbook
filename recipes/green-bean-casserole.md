# Green Bean Casserole

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 2 - 16 oz. can green beans, drained
- 1 (10oz.) can cream of mushroom soup
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1 cup French fried onion rings

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 350°.  Mix all ingredients in casserole dish. Microwave for 5 minutes.  Then bake for 15 minutes.  Sprinkle top with French fried onion rings for garnish.  Bake an additional 5 minutes.
