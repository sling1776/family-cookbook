# Best Gotta-Have-It Brownie (Reader’s Digest’s Best Brownie of the year from Katie Barker of Layton, Utah)

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

2 cups sugar
1/3 cup cocoa
4 eggs
1 ½ cups flour
2 tsp. vanilla
¼ tsp. salt
1 ½ cups chopped nuts (optional)
### Frosting
1 2/3 cups powdered sugar 
½ cup butter
1/3 cup cocoa 
1/3 cup canned evaporated milk

## Procedure

Mix butter, sugar and cocoa together. Stir in 4 eggs. Add flour, vanilla, salt and nuts. Stir. Pour into a greased 1” deep baking sheet. Bake at 350º for 20 minutes or until done. Cover with 1 lb. marshmallows; bake for 3 minutes. 
Add frosting: 
Blend frosting ingredients together well. Add to warm marshmallows and mix together for a fudgy frosting.

## Notes

I found this recipe and had to try it. Great brownies, especially if you like marshmallows.
