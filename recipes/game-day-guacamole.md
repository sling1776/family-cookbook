# Game Day Guacamole

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe avocados
- 3 Tbsp. lemon juice
- 2 medium tomatoes, seeded and chopped
- 1- 4 oz. can diced green chilies
- 3 Tbsp. cilantro, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, pressed

## Procedure
1. Peel and pit avocado.  Combine all ingredients until well blended.  Serve with tortilla chips.
