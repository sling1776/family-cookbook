# Bonnie’s Best Baked Beans

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

2 large cans of Pork and Beans
1 bottle of catsup (16 to 20 oz.)
2 Tbsp. prepared mustard
½ cup chopped onion
1/4 cup chopped green pepper
3/4 cup brown sugar
1/4 lb. chopped bacon (raw)
2 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce
½ cup crushed pineapple

## Procedure

Mix all ingredients together in a glass casserole bowl. Bake 3 hours at 300°.

## Notes

I like this recipe because it is so simple to dump it all together without precooking anything.
