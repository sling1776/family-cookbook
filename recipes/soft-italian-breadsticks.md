# Soft Italian Breadsticks

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 cup warm water
- 3 Tbs. butter softened
- 1-1/2 tsp. salt
- 3 cups bread flour
- 2 Tbs. sugar
- 1 tsp. Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp. garlic powder
- 2-1/4 tsp. active dry yeast

## Procedure
1. I love recipes that use my bread machine. I ended up making flat bread a few times when I forgot to add the yeast, so now days I have a sticker on my bread machine that says ADD YEAST so I can’t forget.
2. Add all ingredients to bread machine pan in order.  Select dough setting.  Check after 5 minutes of mixing: add 1 to 2 Tbs. of flour or water if needed. When cycle is completed turn dough onto a lightly floured surface: divide in half.  Cut each portion into 12 pieces; roll each into a 4-6 in. rope.  Place 2 in. apart on greased baking sheets. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled, about 20 minutes.   Bake at 350 for 15-18 minutes . Immediately brush with butter and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese.
