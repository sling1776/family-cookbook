# Chicken Salad

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 head lettuce
- 2-3 cups cut up chicken or turkey
- 1 large Bermuda onion, diced
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 1 can sliced water chestnuts
- ½ cup diced green pepper
- 8 oz grated cheese
- 3 hard cooked eggs, grated
- 5-6 slices bacon, crumbled

## Procedure
1. Mix well in large Tupperware bowl.  Cover with 2 cups (or more) mayonnaise
2. Garnish with parsley
