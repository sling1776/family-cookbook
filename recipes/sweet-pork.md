# Sweet Pork

Author: Rachel Mitchell

## Ingredients
- 1 (3 to 5lb.) boneless pork roast
- 1 cup salsa
- 1 cup brown sugar, packed

## Procedure
1. Place ingredients in a greased 3 ½ to 4 ½ qt. slow cooker.  Cover and cook on low 8 to 10 hours.  Shred pork with a for or potato masher before serving.  Use meat in burritos, tacos or salads.  Freezes well.
