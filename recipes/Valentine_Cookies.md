# Valentine Cookies

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

1 cup sugar 
½ cup shortening (can use half margarine)
2 eggs
½ cup milk
3 cups flour
½ tsp. salt
2 tsp. baking powder
½ tsp. nutmeg
½ tsp. lemon flavoring

## Procedure

Cream together the sugar and shortening. Add rest of ingredients. Roll on floured board and cut. Bake at 350° until very light tan. Frost with pink frosting.
