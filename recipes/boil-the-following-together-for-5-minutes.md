# Boil the following together for 5 minutes:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup water
- 3 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 3 mashed peaches

## Procedure
1. Add: 2 Tbsp. butter
2. Still until butter is melted and pour mixture over peaches already in crust.  Chill.
3. Top with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.
