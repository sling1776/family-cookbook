# Meatballs in BBQ Sauce

Author: Tawna Marsh

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs. hamburger
- ½ cup saltine cracker crumbs
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 2 tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 1 Tbsp. chopped onion

## Procedure
1. Mix well and form into balls.  Brown lightly in a skillet over medium heat.  Place in a baking dish and cover with sauce.  Bake covered at 350° for 1 hour.
