# Rice and Sausage Casserole

Author: Becky Concoby

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground sausage
- 1 med. onion
- 1 pkg. dry chicken noodle soup mix
- 1 cup uncooked rice
- 2 cups water

## Procedure
1. Brown sausage and onion.  Add chicken soup mix, rice and water. Place in a 9x13” pan, cover and bake at 350° for 1 hour.
