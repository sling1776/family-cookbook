# Dressing

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- 3-4 qts. dry bread cut into ½ inch squares
- 2 large onions, chopped
- 3 cups celery, chopped
- 2 sticks of  margarine
- 1 Tbsp. sage
- 1 Tbsp. poultry seasoning
- Salt
- Pepper

## Procedure
1. Cook onions, celery in water to cover. (Start onion first, as they take longer. Don't overcook celery.)
2. Chicken bouillon
3. Cream of chicken soup
4. Pour over bread.  Put into buttered baking dish and top with strips of bacon.  Bake at 350° for 30 minutes or more.
