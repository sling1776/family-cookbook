# Alfredo Sauce

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- ½ cup soup base mix
- 2½ cup milk
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 tsp. Italian seasoning

## Procedure
1. Blend the mix in ½ cup of the milk until dissolves and then add the rest of the milk and cook over medium heat until it thicken. Add cheeses and stir well.
2. For variety add either:
3. OR 1½ cups chopped chicken
4. Serve over bean thread noodles
5. Serve over G F Noodles.
