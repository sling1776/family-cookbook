# Wheat Thin Crackers

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 3 cups wheat mix
- 5 Tbsp. vegetable oil
- 1 cup water

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 350°. Combine wheat mix, oil and water. Stir with a fork until blended. Knead dough only until smooth, about 3 min. Divide dough into 4 pieces. Roll out dough 1 piece at a time to a 16 x 12 rectangle, 1/16 thick. Place dough on an un-greased baking sheet. Use a pastry cutter or a tailors tracing wheel to mark cutting lines on dough, 2 " apart. Prick each cracker several times with a fork. Sprinkle lightly with salt. Bake 10-20 min. Cool and break apart.
