# Beef roast, any kind

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. Hidden Valley Ranch Dressing Mix
- 1 pkg. Brown gravy mix
- 1 pkg. Italian dressing mix
- ½ cup warm water

## Procedure
1. Place roast in crock-pot. Sprinkle dry mixes over meat and then pour water in the bottom of the crock pot. Cook on low 6-7 hours
