# Hot Apple Cider Nog

## Author

Clint Fielding

## Ingredients

2 eggs, beaten
 ½ cup sugar
 1 cup apple juice or cider
 ½ tsp. salt
 ½ tsp. cinnamon
 1/8 tsp. nutmeg
 3 cups milk, scalded
 ½ cup whipping cream, whipped

## Procedure

Combine eggs, sugar, cider, salt and spices. Add scalded milk gradually; heat, stirring constantly. Serve hot or chilled. Pour into mugs, top with whipped cream.

## Notes

Makes 6 servings.
