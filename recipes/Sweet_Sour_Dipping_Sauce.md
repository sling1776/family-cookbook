# Sweet & Sour Dipping Sauce

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

1 cup pineapple juice
½ cup brown sugar
½ diced green pepper
1/3 cup cider vinegar
1 1/2 Tbsp. cornstarch
2 Tbsp. soy sauce

## Procedure

Combine ingredients and cook, stirring constantly, until thickened and translucent.
