# Sift together:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- 3/4 tsp. soda
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup melted margarine

## Procedure
1. Add:  2 cups buttermilk (or regular milk with a little lemon juice)
2. Mix and cook on heated griddle
3. Note: For a healthier version try it with 1 cup whole wheat flour & 1 cup white flour & 1 cup applesauce in place of the melted butter. They taste great either way.
