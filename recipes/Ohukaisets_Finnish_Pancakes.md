# Ohukaisets (Finnish Pancakes)

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

2 1/2 cups milk
1/2 cup sugar
1/2 tsp. salt
1/4 cup oil
1 3/4 cups featherlight mix
2 eggs

## Procedure

Beat ingredients together Let the batter sit in the fridge for 20-30 minutes. Pour about a 1/4 cup of batter into pan. It will be thin. Fry until slightly brown, flip and cook about 1 minute more. Top with whip cream and jam- roll and eat.

## Notes

Note: Most of GF the recipes here are from Betsy Thomas and Kristi Kirkland on www.eatingglutenfree.com
The pancakes are very similar to crepes only sweeter.
