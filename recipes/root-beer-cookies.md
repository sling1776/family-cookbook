# Root Beer Cookies

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup softened butter
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup buttermilk or water
- 1 tsp. Root beer extract
- 1 3/4 cup flour
- ½ tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. Mix together all ingredients well spoon onto cookie sheet and bake at 400° for 8 minutes. Let cool and glaze with Root Beer Glaze. Makes about 30 cookies.
