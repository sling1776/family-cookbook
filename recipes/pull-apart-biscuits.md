# Pull Apart Biscuits

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 can refrigerator biscuits
- 1/3 cup brown sugar
- ½ tsp. cinnamon
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- 1 Tbsp. water

## Procedure
1. In a round microwave dish, mix together sugar, cinnamon, butter and water.  Cook 1 minute on high in the microwave. Cut biscuits into fourths. Put on top of the brown sugar mixture and microwave 3 more minutes on high.  Turn upside down on a plate and serve.
