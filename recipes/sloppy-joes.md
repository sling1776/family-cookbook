# Sloppy Joes

Author: Chrissy Probst

## Ingredients
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 lb. hamburger
- 4 Tbsp. brown sugar
- 3 tsp. lemon juice
- ½ tsp. mustard
- 3 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce
- 1 cup ketchup
- ¼ cup water

## Procedure
1. Brown hamburger with onions. Mix all ingredients together .  Cool completely and freeze for later.  When ready to serve, simmer for 15 minutes and enjoy on hamburger buns!!
