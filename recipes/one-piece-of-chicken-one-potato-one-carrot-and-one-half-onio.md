# One piece of chicken, one potato, one carrot and one half onion for each person.

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 green pepper, if desired
- salt and pepper
- 1 pound of bacon
- 1 lb. of your favorite grated cheese

## Procedure
1. choice of seasoning
2. Warm Dutch oven.  Slice bacon into squares and line bottom of oven.  Cool until thoroughly done.  Remove bacon onto paper towel.  Coat chicken pieces with flour which has been salted, peppered and seasoned.  Brown chicken.  Slice vegetables into bite size pieces approximately ½ inch thick and add on top of chicken.  Salt and season as desired.  Put lid on and cook at moderate heat until the chicken is tender and carrots and potato slices easily.  Remove oven from heat, sprinkle bacon bits over top and then sprinkle on cheese.  Put lid back on until cheese melts (about 5 min) 12 inch oven use 9 briquettes on top and 15 on bottom.
