# Raspberry Slush

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 cups sugar (1 ½ cup if raspberries are sweetened)
- 3 ½ cups water
- 6 oz undiluted orange juice
- 1 pkg. of frozen raspberries
- 1 can of crushed pineapple -optional

## Procedure
1. Bring to boil and cool.  Add:
2. Mix together, stirring occasionally while freezing.  Take out a few hours before using.
