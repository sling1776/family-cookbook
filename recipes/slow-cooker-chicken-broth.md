# Slow Cooker Chicken Broth

Author: Grace Ann Skousen

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken breasts with bones
- 2 onions quartered
- 2 carrots, quartered
- 2 celery stalks with leaves, quartered
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp. salt
- 12 whole coriander seeds
- 8 whole black peppercorns
- 8 cups water

## Procedure
1. Handful of flat-leaf parsley
2. Put everything in cock pot, cover and cook on low 8-10 hours of high 4-5 hours.  Take chicken out and let cool. Strain the broth and discard vegetables and herbs. De-bone chicken and put back into broth if desired for soup. Add fresh vegetables and noodles. Or use chicken in casseroles or salads & freeze broth for later use.
