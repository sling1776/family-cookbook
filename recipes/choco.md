# Choco

Author: Orange Slices:

## Ingredients
- 1 ½ tsp. grated orange peel
- 1/3 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 1 Tbsp. orange juice
- ½ tsp. orange extract (optional)

## Procedure
1. Add orange peel to 1 ½ cups of dough; mix well.  Shape into a 8x1½” log. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.  Cut log into ¼” slices.  Bake at 325º for 15 to 18 minutes or until edges begin to brown.  Remove from cookies sheets.  Cool on wire rack.  Store in airtight container.  Melt 1/3 cup chocolate chips, orange juice and orange extract on low heat, stirring until smooth.   Dip ½ of cookie into chocolate mixture; let excess drip off.
