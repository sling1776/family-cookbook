# Watermelon Rind Relish

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 2 cups diced red or orange bell peppers
- 1 cup diced onion
- 2 ¼ cups (1 lb.) brown sugar
- 1 cup cider vinegar
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. fennel seeds
- 1 tsp. mustard seeds
- ½ tsp. red pepper flakes
- 10 whole cloves
- 1-(3-inch) cinnamon stick

## Procedure
1. 4 quarts (16 cups) watermelon rind, peeled and grated (NOTE: To prepare watermelon rind, trim dark skin and pink flesh from thick watermelon rind.)
2. Directions:
3. In a large pot combine all ingredients; mix well.  Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until thick, stirring occasionally.  Remove cloves & cinnamon stick.  Pour into sterilized jars and seal, or chill and serve.  Makes 10 pints.
4. Note: Processing time is 5 min. sea level and 15 min. 3,000 to 6,000 feet.
