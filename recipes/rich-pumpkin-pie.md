# Rich Pumpkin Pie

Author: Christine Fielding

## Ingredients
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 Tbsp. flour
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. ginger
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp. cloves
- 1/4 tsp. nutmeg
- 3 cups pumpkin
- ½ cup molasses
- 1 cup corn syrup
- 3-4 eggs, slightly beaten
- 2 cups milk

## Procedure
1. Combine and mix together
2. Mix and add to above mixture
3. Mix together and add to above mixture.
4. Pour filling into unbaked pastry shell.  Makes enough for 2 ten inch pie shells.  Cover crust with foil and bake at 325° for an hour or until knife comes out clean.
