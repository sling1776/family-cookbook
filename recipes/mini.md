# Mini

Author: Pumpkin Cheesecakes

## Ingredients
- 18 baking cups
- 18 gingersnap cookies
- 12 ounces cream cheese, softened
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1 tsp. pumpkin pie spice
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup canned pumpkin
- 1/3 cup corn syrup

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 325°.  Line 18 muffin cups with paper baking cups.  Place 1 gingersnap in each cup.  Beat cream cheese, sugar, cornstarch and pumpkin pie spice with mixer.  Add eggs and mix well.  Add pumpkin and corn syrup.  Beat 1 minute.  Pour filling into liners, dividing evenly.  Bake for 30-35 minutes, until just set.  Chill 1 hour.  Serve with whipped topping
