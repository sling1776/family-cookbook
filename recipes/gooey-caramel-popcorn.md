# Gooey Caramel Popcorn

Author: Kadie Allred

## Ingredients
- 1 can sweetened-condensed milk
- 1 cube margarine
- 1 cup corn syrup
- 2 cups brown sugar

## Procedure
1. Boil to soft-ball stage (about 5 mins.) stirring constantly. Pour over 8 quarts of popcorn.
2. Optional: Add 1 tsp. vanilla to mixture before pouring over popcorn.
