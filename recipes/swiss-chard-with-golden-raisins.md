# Swiss Chard with Golden Raisins

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 2 ½ pounds (the average weight of 2 bundles) Red Swiss chard
- 1 ½ Tbsp. extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 slices, pancetta OR bacon, chopped
- 1 small yellow skinned onion, chopped
- 1/4 cup (2 handfuls) golden raisins
- 14 oz. chicken stock or broth
- 1/8 tsp. nutmeg

## Procedure
1. Coarse salt
2. Heat a large skillet over medium high heat. Coarsely chop the greens of clean red chard. Add oil, pancetta, and chopped onion to the pan and cook 2 or 3 minutes until onions begin to soften and pancetta is lightly browned. Add chopped chard to pan in large bunches, adding remaining chard as the greens wilt. Sprinkle in raisins, broth & season with salt and nutmeg. Bring liquid to a boil, reduce heat and simmer greens 10 to 15 minutes until greens are no longer bitter. Raisins will plump as it cooks.
