# Manicotti with Cheese

Author: Emily Hammerstad

## Ingredients
- 1 ¾ cup Ricotta Cheese
- 2 cup Mozzarella Cheese
- ¼ cup Parmesan Cheese
- 2Tbsp. Parsley
- ½ tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. pepper
- 3 cups Spaghetti Sauce

## Procedure
1. Cooked Gluten-Free Lasagna Noodles
2. Preheat oven to 350°.  Mix cheese and seasoning together in a bowl.
3. Pour some spaghetti sauce on the bottom of a baking pan.  Place a dollop of cheese mixture onto a lasagna noodle and wrap into a tight roll.  Place these rolls into the baking pan and pour the rest of the spaghetti sauce on top.  Bake for 40 minutes.
