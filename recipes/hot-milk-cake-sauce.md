# Hot Milk Cake Sauce:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 6 Tbsp. real butter
- 1/4 cup milk
- 6 Tbsp. brown sugar
- 3/4 cup coconut

## Procedure
1. Cook butter, milk and sugar until it spins a thread when a small amount is dropped from a spoon.  It may look burned or curdled but don't worry about what it looks like.  Pour sauce over the hot baked cake, top with the coconut. It makes a crusty frosting that keeps the cake moist for as long as it takes us to eat it.
