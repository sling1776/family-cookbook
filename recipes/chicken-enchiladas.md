# Chicken Enchiladas

Author: Lisa Olsen

## Ingredients
- 8 chicken breasts cooked and shredded, (I use skinless, boneless breasts)
- 1 pint sour cream
- 2 cans cream of chicken soup
- 2 small cans or 1 large can mushroom pieces
- 1 cup Monterey or mild cheese
- 8 soft tortilla shells

## Procedure
1. Mix chicken, sour cream, soup and mushrooms.  Fill tortilla shells.  Roll and place in baking dish.  Bake in 350° oven until heated through.  Top with grated cheese & bake until melted.
