# Microwave Peanut Brittle

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

1 cup sugar
½ cup light corn syrup in bowl
½ tsp. salt (if using unsalted peanuts.)
1 cup Spanish Peanuts.
1 tsp. butter
	1 tsp. vanilla

## Procedure

Use a 3 quart glass casserole dish with lid.
Put in sugar, corn syrup, and salt.
Microwave 4 minutes on high.
Add peanuts
Microwave 3-5 minutes on high, just until it turns light brown.	
Add butter and vanilla
Microwave 1 minute. 
Add 1 tsp. baking soda.
Pour onto lightly greased cookie sheet and cool completely. About 30 minutes. Lift and break into bite size pieces.

## Notes

This comes from our bishop’s wife, Donna Huff, from Park City area. I like this recipe because the cooking times are so exact. It takes out much of the guess work. Makes 1 lb.
