# Apple Crisp

Author: Ruby Fielding

## Ingredients
- 6 cups sliced apples
- 1 - 1 1/3 cups brown sugar
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup oats
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1 tsp. nutmeg
- 2/3 cup margarine

## Procedure
1. Heat oven to 375°.  Grease 9x13 pan.  Place sliced apples in pan.  Mix the rest of the ingredients thoroughly.  Sprinkle over apples.  Bake 30 minutes.
