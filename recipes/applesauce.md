# Applesauce

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1.  Wash apples.  Peel, core and cut.  Place in fruit fresh.
- 3.  Mash with a potato masher and then beat with a beater.  Sugar can be added to sweeten to your taste.
- 4.  Ladle applesauce into hot jars leaving ½ inch head space.  Remove air bubbles with rubber spatula.

## Procedure
1. 2.  Cook, covered until tender in just enough water to prevent sticking. (I fill a six quart pot 2/3 full with 2 cups water.)
2. 5.  Place lids that have been in boiling water and screw on bands finger tight. Process 20-25 minutes in boiling water bath.
