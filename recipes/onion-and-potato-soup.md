# Onion and Potato Soup

Author: Grandma Myrl Olsen

## Ingredients
- 6 cups diced potatoes
- 1 good size onion

## Procedure
1. Cook all together until tender.  Drain and mash potatoes and onions together and return to water.  Add 1 can evaporated milk, a piece of butter and 1 cup of grated nippy cheese.
