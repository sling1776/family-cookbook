# Cream of Veggie Soup

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 cup onion
- 1 cup celery
- 1 cup carrots
- 1 cup broccoli
- 1 cup cauliflower
- 1 cup potatoes
- 5 cups water
- 5 chicken bouillon cubes

## Procedure
1. Cook vegetables in the chicken broth.
2. Make roux with 4 Tbsp. margarine and 4 Tbsp. flour.  Add 2 cups half and half.  Melt 2 cups cheese.  Add 1 Tbsp. vinegar.
