# Hawaiian Teriyaki Chicken Marinade

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup soy sauce
- 1 tsp. ginger powder
- 1 tsp. garlic powder
- 2 Tbsp. sesame seeds (optional)

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients together except sesame seeds. Add chicken. Freeze in labeled gallon freezer bag.. Freeze sesame seeds separately. Attach to gallon bag. To serve: Thaw. (As it thaws it marinates) Grill, bake or broil. Serve with rice and steamed vegetables.
