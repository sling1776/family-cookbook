# Molasses Gingerbread Cake

## Author

Tiffanie Olsen

## Ingredients

Cream: 3 Tbsp. shortening
½ cup sugar
1 egg
Add: 1 1/2 cup flour
½ tsp. salt
1 tsp. ginger
1 tsp. cinnamon
1 tsp. baking soda
½ cup canned milk
½ cup molasses 
### HARD SAUCE:
4 Tbsp. butter
3/4 cup powdered sugar
2 Tbsp. canned milk
½ tsp. vanilla
¼ tsp. lemon extract

## Procedure

Use 9x13” pan. Bake 350° 30-45 minutes.
