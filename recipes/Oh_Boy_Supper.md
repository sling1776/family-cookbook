# Oh Boy Supper

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

1 lb. ground beef, browned
1 small onion, diced
¼ cup diced green pepper, optional
1 1/2 cups rice cooked
1 cup celery diced
1 can cream of mushroom soup
1 can cream of chicken soup
1 Tbsp. soy sauce

## Procedure

Brown ground beef
Add remainder of ingredients
Simmer ten minutes, pour over Chinese noodles. 
Bake covered at 350° for 30 minutes.

## Notes

One of Nathan's favorite's
Variation: Add ½ cup of uncooked rice & 1½ cups water in place of cooked rice.
