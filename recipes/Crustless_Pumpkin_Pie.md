# Crustless Pumpkin Pie

## Author

Grandpa Fay

## Ingredients

1 (16 oz.) can solid pack pumpkin
1 (12 oz.) evaporated skim milk
2 whole eggs
2 egg whites
3/4 cup sugar
1 tsp. cinnamon
1/4 tsp. allspice
1/4 tsp. ginger
1/8 tsp. salt
½ cup graham cracker crumbs

## Procedure

Combine milk, eggs and whites. Beat until smooth. Add sugar, spices, and salt. Blend well. Stir in crumbs. Spray a 9 inch pie pan with Pam. Bake in preheated 325 degree oven for 45-55 minutes. Cool then refrigerate overnight. Top with Cool Whip.

## Notes

"This is a recipe Grandpa Fay had on his bulletin board. He thought he might be able to make it since he could skip the crust." Bonnie
