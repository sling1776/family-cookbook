# Topping:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- ½ cup grated cheddar cheese
- 2 Tbsp. grated Parmesan cheese
- ¼ tsp. paprika

## Procedure
1. Put all ingredients, except topping, into 8 inch round glass baking dish.  Cover with plastic wrap.  Cook in microwave oven 4 minutes: remove.  Stir, cover and cook 4 minutes more. Combine topping ingredients and sprinkle over potatoes.  Cook uncovered 2 minutes more.  Let stand 5 minutes. Serve hot.  Makes 4 to 6 servings.
