# No Bake Cookies

## Author

Ruby Fielding

## Ingredients

2 cups sugar
½ cup butter
½ cup milk
1/4 cup cocoa

## Procedure

Bring to boil, boil 1 minute. Remove from heat, add 1 tsp vanilla add 3 cups quick oats. Spoon onto waxed paper. Chill 20 minutes.

## Notes

Variations: Add 2/3 cup peanut butter, coconut or peanuts if desired.
