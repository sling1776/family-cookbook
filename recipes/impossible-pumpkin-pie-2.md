# Impossible Pumpkin Pie

Author: Alecia Bales

## Ingredients
- 2 eggs
- 1 can evaporated milk
- 1/4 tsp. cloves
- ½ tsp. ginger
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp. nutmeg
- 3/4 cup sugar
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 1/2 cup cooked pumpkin
- ½ cup Bisquick

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 350°.  Pour all ingredients into blender.  Blend well at medium speed or mix with electric mixer on high for 4 minutes.  Pour into 10 inch greased pan.  Bake for 45 minutes.
