# Caramel Fruit Dip

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

1 (8 oz) cream cheese softened
½ cup brown sugar
¼ cup caramel ice cream topping

## Procedure

In a small bowl, beat cream cheese and brown sugar until smooth. Add caramel topping; beat until blended. Serve with fruit.

## Notes

Refrigerate leftovers. Makes 1½ cup.
