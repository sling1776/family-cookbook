# Jell

Author: O Salad

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Cook one large pkg. vanilla pudding using water instead of milk (do not use instant pudding). Add any favor of jello. Refrigerate to set up, beat in a large cool whip when cooled.
2. Note: This makes a great dessert when made with lemon jello and served with a vanilla wafers on the side!
