# Zucchini

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

zucchini
onion
water
salt
pepper
butter
milk

## Procedure

Slice zucchini and onion. Cook in a little water on low. Watch so it does not scorch. When the zucchini becomes translucent, add salt and pepper, butter and enough milk to cover. Warm and serve.
