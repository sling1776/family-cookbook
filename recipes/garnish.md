# Garnish:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp minced dried cranberries
- 1/3 cup white chocolate chips- melted
- Cut into bars immediately because the chips harden quickly and makes cake cutting messy.

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 350° and lightly grease a 9x13 pan.  Beat butter and sugar together and add eggs and vanilla, beating until fluffy (use electric mixer).  Sift together flour, ground ginger and salt, then add to butter/sugar mixture, beating well. Fold in cranberries, chips and candied ginger. Spread thick batter in pan and bake for about 20-25 minutes or until light golden. When cake is cool, mix all frosting ingredients together using electric mixer until smooth and fluffy.  Spread on cooled cake, then immediately sprinkle cranberry pieces on top of frosting.  Melt white choc chips in baggie and cut the tip off corner and drizzle over the cake.
