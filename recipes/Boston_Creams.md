# Boston Creams

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

½ cup sugar
2 cups milk
4 cups sugar (added separately)
2 tsp. vanilla
1/4 cup butter

## Procedure

Caramelize 1/2 cup sugar in heavy pan, heating until melted and browned. Add milk. Dissolve sugar that turns hard when milk is added.
Add: 4 cups of sugar and cook to soft ball stage
Add: 2 tsp. vanilla and butter.
Let cook, then beat by hand until smooth and stiff (like fudge). Spread in buttered pan, cool and cut into squares.

## Notes

This is a recipe I first made in High School Home Ec. class. Since then it has become a Christmas treat to share with friends and neighbors.
