# GLAZE:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup water
- 2 Tbsp. margarine
- 1 tsp. coconut flavoring

## Procedure
1. Beat eggs.  Add sugar, coconut flavoring and oil.  Sift dry ingredients and add to egg mixture.  Fold in buttermilk and shredded coconut.  Pour into 2 greased loaf pans.  Bake at 325° for 75 minutes.  Or smaller loaf pans for 35 to 45 minutes.  In saucepan, combine sugar, water, and margarine for glaze.  Boil for 5 minutes.  Remove from heat and add coconut flavoring.  Pour over bread as soon as it comes from the oven.  Note: For a simple glaze, you can microwave ½ cup of canned cream cheese frosting and add ½ tsp. of coconut extract.
