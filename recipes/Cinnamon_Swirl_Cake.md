# Cinnamon Swirl Cake

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

2 cups GF Basic Cake mix 
1 tsp. vanilla
1 egg, slightly beaten
½ cup milk
Dash of nutmeg, optional
1/4 cup cinnamon and sugar mixture

## Procedure

Stir ingredients (besides cinnamon and sugar mixture) until well blended. Pour into an 8 inch square pan. Before baking swirl gently through the batter your favorite cinnamon and sugar mixture. Bake at 350° for 25-30 minutes.
