# Zucchini Supreme

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

Slices of zucchini
Slice crooked neck squash
Slices of onions
1 small tomato, diced
Salt and pepper to taste
¼ to ½ cup shredded cheese

## Procedure

Cook zucchini, squash and onions in a little water on low. When the squashes are tender, add tomatoes and sprinkle with grated cheese. Cover, let cheese melt and serve.

## Notes

This is the recipe that helped my children learn to love zucchini. Our neighbor Gordon Waasdorp always planted lots of zucchini so we never had to living in Salt Lake City.
