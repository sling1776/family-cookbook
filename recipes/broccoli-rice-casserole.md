# Broccoli Rice Casserole

Author: Scott & Donna Chantry

## Ingredients
- 2 boxes frozen chopped broccoli
- 2 cups Minute rice
- 2 cans Cream of Mushroom soup
- 1 8 oz. jar Cheese whiz
- 1 chopped onion
- 1 stick melted butter

## Procedure
1. Thaw broccoli. Sauté onion in butter.  Add Cheese whiz to melt cheese. Combine rest of ingredients. Bake 1 hr in casserole dish at 350°.
