# Olive oil for coating or cornmeal for dusting.

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup ricotta cheese
- 2 cloves garlic, pressed or minced
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 3 cups grated Mozzarella cheese

## Procedure
1. Follow the first 3 steps on Perfect Pizza Recipe.
2. Combine the ricotta, garlic, and Parmesan.  Spread half of the mixture on the dough, leaving a 1-inch border.  Sprinkle half of the Mozzarella on top.  Bake 12 - 15 minutes or until crust is golden brown underneath.
