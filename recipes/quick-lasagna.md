# Quick Lasagna

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground beef, browned and drained
- ½ cup Hamburger Helper mix
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cups water
- 16 oz. tomato sauce
- 3 cups GF lasagna noodles, uncooked, broken in bits OR corn tortillas
- 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese
- 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded

## Procedure
1. Combine all except mozzarella in large
2. skillet. Bring to a boil, let simmer for
3. 15 minutes or until noodles are cooked. (If using corn tortilla, add after 10 minutes and simmer slightly.) Top with mozzarella. Turn off heat and let cheese melt.
