# Banana Boat S’mores

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. banana
2. mini marshmallows
3. chocolate chips
4. Leave banana in peel. Slice it horizontally, NOT in half, and NOT fully through, only through top of banana and peel. Open it up like a hot dog bun and put in chocolate and marshmallows. Wrap in tinfoil and heat over fire.
