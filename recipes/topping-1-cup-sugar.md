# Topping: 1 cup sugar

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. unsweetened Kool Aid (raspberry)
- 4 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 2 cups water

## Procedure
1. Fruit (raspberries)
2. Bring water to boil in pan.  Add dry ingredients and cook about 3 minutes.  Add fruit.  Cool and then spread on top of cheese cake.  Refrigerate.
