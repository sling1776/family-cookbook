# Caramel Apple Fruit Salad

Author: Stephen Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 small container (8oz) Cool Whip
- 1 small box (4oz)Butterscotch Instant Pudding
- 1 (20oz) can crushed pineapple (with juice)
- 4 cups chopped apples

## Procedure
1. crushed cashews (optional)
2. First combine pineapple (with juice) and instant pudding mix, stir well. Fold in Cool Whip.  Add apples.  Stir. Sprinkle with chopped nuts/cashews if desired.
