# Melt butter and sauté onions for 3 min.

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2 cups celery, sliced diagonally
- 1 ½ cups broth or chicken bouillon
- 2 1/4 cups diced cooked pork roast or chicken
- 1 (16oz.) can bean spouts
- 1 (5oz.) can water chestnuts
- 1 (4oz.) can sliced mushrooms
- 2 Tbsp. soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1 tsp. sugar

## Procedure
1. Add: celery and broth and simmer for 5 min.
2. Stir in and simmer 5 more min.
3. Combine remaining ingredients and add to chicken mixture.  Serve over rice or Chinese noodle.
