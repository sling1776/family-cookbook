# Green Grits

## Author

Aunt Gladys Reber Olson

## Ingredients

6 cups water
1 1/2 cup regular hominy grits
3 beaten eggs
1 stick butter
2 tsp. Savory salt
1 lb. cut up american cheese
2 cans green chilies

## Procedure

Add hominy grits to water.
Cook until consistency of Cream of Wheat

Add: eggs, butter and savory salt.
Remove from stove.
Add: cut up American cheese and green chilies. Put in 9x13” pan. Bake 250° oven. 2½ - 3 hours.
