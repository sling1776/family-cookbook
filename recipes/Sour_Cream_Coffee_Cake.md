# Sour Cream Coffee Cake

## Author

Angela Olsen

## Ingredients

2 cups sugar
1 cup butter
2 eggs
1 cup sour cream
1 tsp. vanilla
½ tsp. salt
2 cups flour
1 tsp. baking powder
### Topping
1 tsp. cinnamon
1 cup chopped nuts
4 Tbsp. brown sugar

## Procedure

Cream butter, sugar and eggs until smooth. Carefully add sour cream, vanilla, and salt. Sift flour and baking powder together and add to mixture.
Pour in half batter to pan; sprinkle more than half of topping. Add remainder of batter and rest of topping.
Bake at 350° for 60 minutes
