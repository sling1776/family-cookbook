# Aunt Priscilla’s Carrot Pudding

## Author



## Ingredients

1 cup grated carrots		
1 cup grated apple
1 cup raisins			
½ cup shortening (they used lard)
3/4 cup sugar			
2 eggs
1 tsp. cinnamon		
1 tsp. nutmeg
½ tsp. cloves			
2 tsp. baking powder
½ tsp. baking soda			
1/4 cup milk
1½ cup flour

## Procedure

Mix ingredients well and bake at 350° for 30 minutes in 9x13” pan. Serve with Alecia's Best Ever Sauce from her Apple Pudding.

## Notes

Priscilla is Mother Olsen's sister-in-law. Grandma Grace used to make this during the holidays.
