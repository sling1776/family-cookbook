# Hearty Tomato Soup

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

1 clove garlic, minced
1 onion, chopped
1 stalk celery, chopped
1 carrot, chopped
2 Tbsp. flour
3/4 cup raw rice, brown preferred
1 (28 oz) can tomatoes
2 tsp. salt
½ tsp. pepper
1 Tbsp. sugar
1 tsp. each: oregano, and basil

## Procedure

In heavy sauce pan add 1 Tbsp. oil. Sauté garlic, onion, celery and carrot.
Add 2 tbsp flour and 3/4 cup raw rice.
Sauté until rice is toasty
add remaining ingredients.
Cook till rice is done, about 30-45 min. Remove from heat. If desired puree soup in blender. Add 2 cups hot milk and 4 Tbsp. butter. Warm, but do not boil.
