# Peppermint Dessert

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 cup candy cane, crushed
- 1 cup finely chopped nuts
- 1 cup finely crushed vanilla wafers
- 1 pint whipping cream, whipped
- 24 large marshmallows, cut up

## Procedure
1. Put 2/3 of wafers in 9x13” pan.
2. Combine whipped cream, marshmallows, candy canes, nuts and remaining wafers.  Pour into pan and let set.
