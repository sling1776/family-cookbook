# Becky’s Salsa

Author: Becky Concoby

## Ingredients
- ½ bushel tomatoes, scalded, peeled and chunked
- 8 medium onions, chopped
- 8 large green pepper chopped
- 15 jalapeño peppers, remove seeds and chopped
- 15 yellow pepper chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, chopped
- 5 Tbsp. sugar
- 5 Tbsp. (not iodized) salt
- ½ cup white vinegar

## Procedure
1. Cook in large kettle, bring to boil.  Simmer 2-3 hours.  Process in jars for 20 minutes.
