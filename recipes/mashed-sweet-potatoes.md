# Mashed Sweet Potatoes

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs. sweet potatoes (3 medium)
- 4 Tbsp. butter, cut into 4 pieces
- 2 Tbsp. whipping cream
- 1 tsp. sugar
- ½ tsp. salt
- Pinch of pepper
- ½ tsp. grated orange zest
- 2 Tbsp. maple syrup

## Procedure
1. Microwave the sweet potatoes 10-15 minutes until soft to the touch.  When done, peel off skin and slice into chunks.  In a large saucepan combine the butter, cream, sugar, salt, and pepper.  Add potato chunks & mash all together with potato masher until smooth.
