# Shirley Temple

Author: Jared Reber

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Blend a bunch of ice in a blender, or if you like just take a big bag of ice and beat it with a kitchen mallet on the porch for two or three minutes.  Fill glasses full of the shaved/cracked ice stuff. Pour in about two fingers worth of the grenadine syrup over the ice.  Of course you won't be able to measure two fingers worth because it will be dripping over the ice you just put in there.  Just approximate.  Fill the rest of the glass with 7-Up or sparkling water.  The drink has a bit of a watermelon and pomegranate flavor.  Note: By leaving out the liquor you can remember how good it tastes the next morning.
