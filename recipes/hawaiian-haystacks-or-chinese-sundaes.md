# Hawaiian Haystacks Or Chinese Sundaes

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 1 (9 oz) can chow mein noodles
- 4 cups cooked long grain rice
- 2 cups cream of chicken soup diluted with 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup chopped celery
- 3 medium tomato wedges
- ½ cup chopped green peppers
- ½ cup chopped green onions
- 1 cup grated cheddar cheese
- 1 (20 oz) can pineapple chunks, drained
- ½ cup grated coconut
- ½ cup slivered almonds

## Procedure
1. maraschino cherries
2. Stack the 13 ingredients on plate in order given, topping with cherry as a sundae.  Variation:  Ham chunks and mushroom soup can be used instead of the chicken.
