# Creamed Peas & Potatoes

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

5 to 8 medium sized red potatoes
1 to 2 cups frozen peas
GF White Sauce recipe

## Procedure

Wash potatoes. Cut into 1 inch pieces. Cover potatoes with water in a saucepan. Bring water to a boil. Boil for 8 minutes. Add frozen peas. Continue boiling for another 8 minutes. Remove from heat. Drain water. Top with one recipe of GF White Sauce.
