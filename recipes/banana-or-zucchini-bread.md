# Banana or Zucchini Bread

Author: Derlene Olsen

## Ingredients
- ½ cup shortening
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 Tbsp. milk
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup nuts

## Procedure
1. Mix well: 1 cup sugar
2. Add: 3 mashed bananas OR grated zucchini
3. Add:  2 cups flour
4. Mix well and bake at 350° for 45 minutes.
