# Applesauce

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 6 large granny apples, cored and sliced
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ tsp. cinnamon

## Procedure
1. Little water (less than half a cup)
2. Prepare apples and place in large kettle with water.  Simmer and stir until apples turn into sauce.  Add sugar and cinnamon.
3. Makes 4 cups.
