# Chicken Pot Pie

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Cut up boneless skinless chicken and brown or use already cooked chicken. Cut potato into bite size chucks, chopped onion, diced carrots and a little celery chopped up. Boil until vegetables are crispy tender. Add cream of GF chicken soup recipe.  Top with GF biscuit dough rolled thin and cut slits in the top to let steam out.  Bake at 400° for 20 - 25 minutes.
2. (These freeze very well. If frozen bake for 30 minutes or until filling is bubbly.)
