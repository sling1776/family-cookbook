# Sloppy Joes

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

1 lb hamburger
½ cup chopped onion
1 8-oz tomato sauce
1 1/2 tsp. Worcestershire sauce
1/4 cup chopped green peppers
1 tsp. salt
1/8 tsp. pepper
1/4 cup catsup
1 Tbsp. sugar
1 Tbsp. vinegar

## Procedure

Brown meat, add vegetables & cook ‘til tender. Add remaining ingredients & simmer 20 minutes. Serve on hamburger buns. Serves 6.

## Notes

This is a recipe our family has used for at least 50+ years.
