# Then add:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2/3 cup oil
- 2/3 cup honey or sugar (honey tastes best)
- 5 cups hot tap water

## Procedure
1. Mix and knead only slightly.  Let rise.  Punch down and divide into 4 loaves and place into bread pans.  Let rise until double in bulk.  Bake at 350°.  When you smell the bread baking set the timer for 5 more minutes.  (Don't laugh, it works for me)
