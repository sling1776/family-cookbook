# Slow

Author: Cooker Vegetable Broth

## Ingredients
- 2 parsnips
- 2 celery stalks
- 2 carrots
- 2 onions
- 1 small fennel bulb
- 2 fresh thyme sprigs
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 garlic cloves, bruised but not peeled
- 1 tsp. salt
- 8 whole black peppercorns
- 8 cups water

## Procedure
1. Handful of flat-leaf parsley
2. Cut all vegetables into chunks. Put everything in the crock-pot and cook on low for 8-10 hours or high for 4-5 hours. Strain broth and discard veggies and herbs. Great for lentil soup or use instead of water when cooking rice.
