# Sopapillas

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

4 cups flour
1 1/2 tsp. salt
1 tsp. baking powder
1 Tbsp. sugar
1 Tbsp. shortening
1 Tbsp. yeast in 1/4 cup warm water
1 1/4 cup scalded milk, cooled

## Procedure

Mix ingredients and knead 15-20 times. Let rest 10 minutes. Roll out 1/4 inch thick. Cut into 2” squares. Fry in deep fat 425°.
