# (I add only 2 but can add the other can if you like it hotter)

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 (10oz.) can diced tomatoes & chilies (Ro-Tel)
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 1 large can evaporated milk
- 2 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1 lb. Velveeta cheese (cubed)
- Shredded sharp cheddar cheese for garnish

## Procedure
1. Add to the chicken broth mixture and bring to a boil.  Reduce heat and simmer 30 minutes until potatoes and carrots are done.
2. Mix cornstarch with canned milk and stir into soup mixture.  Cook until almost boiling.  Add Velveeta cheese and stir until melted (but don't boil).  Remove from heat and sprinkle with shredded sharp cheese for garnish.
