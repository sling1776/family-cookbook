# Pasta In Alfredo Sauce

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 Tbsp. margarine
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 Tbsp. flour
- 1  cup milk
- 2 Tbsp. cream cheese
- 2 Tbsp. Parmesan cheese
- 1-2 cups pasta, cooked according to package

## Procedure
1. In sauce pan, melt margarine over low heat.  Add minced garlic and sauté 1 min.   Stir in flour.  Gradually add milk and whisk until smooth.  Cook stirring constantly until thick about 8 minutes on medium heat.  (Don’t scorch).  Stir in cream cheese.  Cook 2 minutes.  Add Parmesan cheese and cook until melted.  Serve over hot cooked pasta. (Can add Italian seasoning, parsley, garlic powder if desired.)
