# Clam Chowder

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

3 to 4 cups diced potatoes
1 lg. onion diced
4-5 celery stalks, finely chopped
3 cans minced or chopped clams
1 quart of half and half 
3/4 cup butter
½ tsp. sugar
1 tsp. salt
Pepper to taste
Milk (as needed)
1/2 - 3/4 cup Ultra Gel
OR White Sauce Recipe

## Procedure

Mix potatos, onion and celery. Add water to cover and juice from 3 cans of clams (clams added later). Cook until vegetables are tender and add half & half, butter, sugar, salt, and pepper.
Add enough milk to make enough for your family. Thicken with ½ - 3/4 cup Ultra Gel or use the medium white sauce recipe. Add clams.
