# Chocolate Peanut Butter Chip Cookies

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1 cup butter
- 1 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 2 cup flour
- 2/3 cup cocoa
- 3/4 tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. salt
- 2 cups Reese’s peanut butter chips

## Procedure
1. Cream butter, sugar, eggs and vanilla in large bowl.  Mix in flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt.  Blend together.  Add chips and blend more.  Bake at 350° for 8-10 minutes on an ungreased cookie sheet.  NOTE: For soft cookies mix the longest amount of time and cook for the least amount of time.
