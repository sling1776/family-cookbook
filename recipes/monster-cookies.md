# Monster Cookies

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 lb. brown sugar
- 1 cup margarine
- 1 1/2 lb peanut butter
- 6 eggs
- ½ Tbsp. vanilla
- ½ lb. chocolate chips
- ½ lb. M&Ms
- 1 cup chopped nuts

## Procedure
1. Mix well:
2. Add 9 cups quick oatmeal
3. Mix and Chill ½ hour.  Make large cookies on greased cookie sheet. Bake 10 min. at 350°
