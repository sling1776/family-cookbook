# Slush for 50

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 2 pkg. lime Jell-O
- 3 cups sugar
- 1- 46oz. pineapple juice
- 1 can crushed pineapple
- 4 mashed bananas
- 3 bottles of Sprite (2 liter size)

## Procedure
1. Dissolve Jell-O with 1 cup boiling water.  Add sugar and 7 cups warm water.  Stir.  Add pineapple juice and 5 cups cold water.  Add pineapple and bananas.  Freeze 2 days before needed (or longer). Set out of freezer for several hours before serving.  Serve slush mixture covered with Sprite.
