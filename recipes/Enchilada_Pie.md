# Enchilada Pie

## Author

Gina Olsen

## Ingredients

1 lb. hamburger, browned
1 onion, chopped
1 lrg. can enchilada sauce
sliced olives
flour tortillas
grated cheese

## Procedure

In a Dutch oven, layer flour tortillas, sauce mixture, grated cheese. Repeat. Cook in 350° oven for 20-25 minutes.
