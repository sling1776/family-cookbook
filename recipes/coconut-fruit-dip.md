# Coconut Fruit Dip

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 can (8 oz.) crushed pineapple, undrained
- 3/4 cup fat-free milk
- 1/2 cup (4 oz.) fat-free sour cream
- 1 tsp. coconut extract
- 1 pkg. (3.4 oz.) instant vanilla pudding mix

## Procedure
1. In a blender, combine the first five ingredients; cover and process for 1 minute or until smooth. Serve with fruit. Store in the refrigerator.
