# Linz’s Yummy Rolls

Author: Kadie Allred

## Ingredients
- 1 dozen frozen rolls
- 1/3 c margarine (melted)
- 2 tsp parsley
- ¾ cup parmesan cheese
- 1 tsp. garlic powder
- 1 tsp. salad supreme seasoning

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients together except rolls. Spread on top of frozen rolls, then let rise & bake according to directions on bag.
