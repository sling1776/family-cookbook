# Vanilla Ice Cream

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 quarts half and half
- ½ pint heavy cream
- 1 ½ cups sugar
- 4 tsp. vanilla
- Pinch of salt
- 1 small pkg. instant vanilla pudding mix

## Procedure
1. Combine ingredients and freeze according to manufacturer’s instructions.
2. Note: You may use any flavor pudding mix you want. Crushed fruit may be added also.
