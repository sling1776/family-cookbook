# No Fail Fudge

## Author

Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients

4 ½ cups sugar
1 can evaporated milk
3 cups chocolate chips
2 cups chopped nuts
1 Tbsp. vanilla
26 large marshmallows OR 3 cups mini
½ lb. butter or margarine

## Procedure

Combine sugar and milk. Put on low heat and bring to boil. Boil 12 minutes stirring occasionally. In a big bowl mix chocolate chips, chopped nuts, vanilla, marshmallows and butter.
Pour hot mixture on top and stir until dissolved. Pour into lightly greased jelly roll pan. Chill.
