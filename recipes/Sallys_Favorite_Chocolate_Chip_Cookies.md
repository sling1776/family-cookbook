# Sally’s Favorite Chocolate Chip Cookies

## Author

Sally Marsh

## Ingredients

1 cup shortening
1 cup sugar
½ cup brown sugar
1 tsp. vanilla
½ tsp. water
2 eggs
2 ½ cups flour
1 tsp. baking soda
1 tsp. salt
½ cup chocolate chips

## Procedure

Beat shortening, sugar, bown sugar, vanilla and water together until creamy, then add eggs. Combine flour, baking soda, salt, and chocolate chips.
Add to creamed mixture. Do not over mix. Shape into balls. Bake at 375° for 7 to 8 min.
