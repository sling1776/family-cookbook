# Valentine’s Day Dessert

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 8oz. cream cheese, softened
- 2 cups mini marshmallows
- ½ cup sugar
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 angel food cake mix
- 1 cup whipped cream
- 1 can cherry pie filling

## Procedure
1. Make angel food cake according to directions on box.  Bake and cool.  In a bowl beat cream cheese, sugar and vanilla until fluffy.  Fold in whipped cream and marshmallows.  Break cake into bit size pieces.  Layer cake, cream mixture & pie filling in a glass bowl. Refrigerate.
