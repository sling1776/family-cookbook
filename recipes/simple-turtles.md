# Simple Turtles

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 20 mini pretzels
- 20 Rolos
- 20 pecan halves

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 300°. Arrange the pretzels in a single layer on a parchment lined cookie sheet. Place one chocolate covered caramel candy on each pretzel. Bake for 4 minutes. While the candy is warm, press a pecan half onto each candy covered pretzel.
