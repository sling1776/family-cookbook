# Chicken Enchilada’s

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken breasts cooked and shredded
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1 recipe cream of chicken soup (from mix)
- 1 small can mushrooms pieces
- 1 cup Monterey or mild cheese
- 8-9 soft corn tortilla

## Procedure
1. Mix chicken, sour cream, soup and mushrooms. Fill tortillas. Roll and place in baking dish. Bake at 350° until heated through about 25-30 minutes. Top with grated cheese and return to oven to melt cheese.
2. Easier version: Put a small amount of chicken mixture in bottom of pan. Layer with Tortillas, chicken mixture, cheese, repeat layers and top with more cheese. Bake as normal.
