# Rolly Pancakes

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- 4 c. flour & milk, 6 eggs, salt =13 pancakes
- 2 2/3c. flour & milk, 4 eggs, salt = 10 pancakes
- 2 c. flour and milk, 3 eggs, salt = 6 pancakes
- 2/3 c. flour and milk, 1 egg, salt = 2 pancakes

## Procedure
1. Mix the flour and milk first, then the eggs. Pour onto the center of a hot griddle and then tip the pan around to thin out the batter for a crepe type pancake. Top with butter, powdered sugar, syrup, or jam, roll up and enjoy.
