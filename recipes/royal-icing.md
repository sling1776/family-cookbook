# Royal Icing

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 3 egg whites (room temperature)
- 4 cups sifted powdered sugar
- ½ tsp cream of tartar

## Procedure
1. Beat thoroughly in mixing bowl until the icing stands in peaks, then keep the bowl covered at all times with a damp cloth, as the icing dries quickly and becomes very hard.  Use icing to cement house pieces together and to place candies on for decoration.  Place on cardboard covered with foil.
