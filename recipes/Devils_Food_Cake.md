# Devil’s Food Cake

## Author

Derlene Olsen

## Ingredients

½ cup shortening
3 Tbsp. cocoa
2 eggs
3/4 cup canned milk
1 tsp. vanilla
1 ½ cups sugar
2 cups flour
3 tsp. baking soda
1 cup boiling water
dash of salt

## Procedure

Cream shortening and sugar. Add eggs and cocoa. Sift dry ingredients. Add alternately with milk. Add vanilla, water and salt. Mix well. Bake at 375° for 30 minutes.
