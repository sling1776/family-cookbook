# Onion and Potato Soup

## Author

Grandma Myrl Olsen

## Ingredients

6 cups diced potatoes
1 good size onion

## Procedure

Cook all together with water until tender. Drain and mash potatoes and onions together and return to water. Add 1 can evaporated milk, a piece of butter and 1 cup of grated nippy cheese.
