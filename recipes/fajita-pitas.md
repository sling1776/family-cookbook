# Fajita Pitas

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. oil
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 1 small green pepper, sliced
- 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts, thinly slices
- 1 (15 oz) chunky salsa

## Procedure
1. Pita bread or warmed tortillas
2. In large pan stir fry first 4 ingredients.  When chicken is cooked, stir in salsa.  Heat through and spoon into pita or tortillas.  Makes 4-6 servings
