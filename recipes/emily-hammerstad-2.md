# Emily Hammerstad

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 4-5 eggs
- ½ cup olive oil
- 5 Tbsp. cocoa
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1/8 tsp. salt
- ¼ cup walnuts (slightly crushed)

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 350°.  Combine all ingredients.  Pour into a greased pan and bake for 30-45 minutes, depending on elevation.
2. Note: I prefer to only use a glass, or specially made brownie pan when making this recipe.
