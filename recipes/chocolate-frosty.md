# Chocolate Frosty

Author: Landon Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 cup of milk
- 1/2 cup of Nesquik Cocoa Mix, the kind with the bunny on the container
- 2 really, really large scoops of vanilla ice cream

## Procedure
1. Blend them all up in the blender until thick but pretty smooth. Note: You can use Hershey's syrup if you don't have Nesquik Cocoa Mix.
