# Sloppy Joe

## Author

Suzanne Reber

## Ingredients

1 lb. browned hamburger, drained
¼ cup chopped onions
1/3 cup chopped celery
1/3 cup water
2 tsp. brown sugar
½ Tbsp. vinegar
1 ½ tsp. lemon juice
½ tsp. worcestershire sauce
½ tsp. salt
1/8 tsp. pepper

## Procedure

brown hamburger and drain.
Add chopped onions, celery and cook with hamburger until soft
Stir in flour
Add remainder of ingredients.
Bring to a boil and simmer for 1 hour.

## Notes

This is my Mom’s tried and true recipe.
