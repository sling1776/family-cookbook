# No Bake Cookies

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

1 cup sugar
2 Tbsp cocoa
1/4 cup milk
1/4 cup butter
1 tsp vanilla
1 1/2 cups quick oats
1/4 cup peanut butter

## Procedure

Combine sugar, cocoa, milk, butter and boil 1 minute. Remove from heat. Add vanilla, oats, and peanut butter. Mix well and drop on waxed paper. Chill until firm.
