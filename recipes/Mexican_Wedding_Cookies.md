# Mexican Wedding Cookies

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

1 cup butter
½ cup powdered sugar
½ tsp. vanilla
1 3/4 cup sifted flour
½ cup chopped nuts

## Procedure

Cream butter and sugar. Blend in vanilla, flour and nuts. Chill. Shape into 1 inch balls. Place on ungreased cookie sheet and bake 350° for 20 minutes. When cool sprinkle with powdered sugar.

## Notes

This recipe comes from Mexico by way of Grandpa Joe Chantry's wife, Irene. She made these many times.
