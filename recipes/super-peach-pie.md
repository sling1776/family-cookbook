# Super Peach Pie

Author: Diane Anderson

## Ingredients
- Slice 3 large peaches into a cooked pie shell. Mash 2 more peaches and add:
- 1 cup sugar
- 3 Tbsp. corn starch
- ½ cup water
- ½ tsp. almond flavoring
- 2 Tbsp. butter

## Procedure
1. Cook 5 minutes, so that it's boiling then add:
2. Pour over sliced peaches.  Chill for about an hour.  Serve with whipped cream.
