# Cheese Mixture

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- ¾ lb. cottage cheese
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 lb. shredded mozzarella cheese

## Procedure
1. Mix all cheeses in a bowl.
2. Layer crock pot with meat sauce, uncooked lasagna noodles (or any pasta of your choice) and cheeses. Repeat layers. Cover, cook on low 6-8 hours. Watch so it doesn’t dry out, can add a little water.
