# Bottled Root Beer

Author: Grandma Walker

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle root beer extract.

## Procedure
1. Pour over 4 lbs. sugar.
2. Mix well.  Dissolve this mixture in 4 ¾ gallons of lukewarm water.  Put ½ yeast cake in a cup of lukewarm water and let stand five minutes.  If weather is over 90° use less yeast if under 70° double the quantity of yeast.  Add yeast to mixture and stir well.  Bottle immediately.  Fill bottles to within ½ inch of the top.  Place bottles on their side.  Cover so draft can’t hit them.  Should be ready in two or three days.  Keep them in a cool place.
