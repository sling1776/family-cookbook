# Pumpkin Cake Pie

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 cup cake mix
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 cup pumpkin
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1 egg
- 1 ½ tsp. pumpkin pie spice
- 1 cup cake mix and:
- 1/4 cup water

## Procedure
1. Sprinkle in 8 x 8 pan:
2. Sprinkle on cake mix:
3. Mix together and pour over cake mix & water:
4. Sprinkle over the top of pumpkin mix
5. Bake at 350 for 25 minutes.
6. Serve with whipped cream or substitute.
