# Bread in 2 Hours

## Author

Diane Anderson

## Ingredients

4 cups warm water
4 Tbsp. yeast in ½ cup warm water
½ cup honey
1 Tbsp. salt
2 Tbsp. oil
12 cups flour

## Procedure

Mix together and let rise half hour. Form into loaves and put in pans. Let rise half hour. Bake at 400° for half hour.

## Notes

4 loaves.
