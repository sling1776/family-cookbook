# Peanut Cookies

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- 2 cups brown sugar
- 3/4 cup shortening
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 3 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1/8 tsp. soda
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- pinch of salt
- 1 cup salted peanuts

## Procedure
1. Cream sugar and shortening.  Add eggs, milk and dry ingredients,  Can  add more than 1 cup nuts if want.  Bake at 350° for 10-12 minutes.
