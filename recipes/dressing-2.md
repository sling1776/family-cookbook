# Dressing:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tsp pepper
- 1 cup red wine vinegar
- 1/2 cup soy sauce

## Procedure
1. Cook and cool pasta, place in a large bowl. Cook and cut up chicken and add to pasta. Combine dressing ingredients and pour over, stir well.  Chill at least an hour.  Add remaining ingredients prior to serving.
