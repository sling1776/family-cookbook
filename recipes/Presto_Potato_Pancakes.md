# Presto Potato Pancakes

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

### Mix
1 cup leftover mashed potatoes
2 eggs (mixture will be mushy)
Bread crumbs (plain or flavored)

## Procedure

Add eggs to the potates until the consistency is such that you can shape into cakes. Coat with:
Bread crumbs (plain or flavored)
Cook over medium heat in a greased skillet until lightly browned and heated through.
