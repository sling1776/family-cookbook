# Oven Stew

Author: Angela Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs. stew meat, browned
- 1 pkg. onion soup mix
- carrots, potatoes, and onions diced up
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 cup ketchup
- 1 can (14 ½ oz) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 cup water
- 3 Tbsp. quick cooking tapioca
- 2 tsp. sugar
- 1 ½ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. pepper
- 2 pounds lean beef stew meat, cut in one inch cubes
- 4 medium carrots, cut into 1 inch chunks
- 3 medium potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 2 celery ribs, cut in 3/4 in. chunks
- 1 medium onion, cut into chunks
- 1 slice bread, cubed

## Procedure
1. Mix together and bake at 300° for about 3 hours.
2. Baked Beef Stew - Barbara Olsen
3. In a large bowl, combine the tomatoes, water, tapioca, sugar, salt and pepper.  Add remaining ingredients; mix well.  Pour into a greased 13 x 9 inch baking dish.  Cover and bake at 375° for 1 ¾ - 2 hours or until the meat and vegetables are tender.  Yield: 6-8 servings.
