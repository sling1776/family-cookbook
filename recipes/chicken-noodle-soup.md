# Chicken Noodle Soup

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Use chicken broth.  Cube cooked chicken.  Use old fashioned noodles OR make your own using Grandma Grace's recipe.  Add sliced celery, diced carrots, diced onion while cooking noodles.  Can add chicken bouillon if more flavor is needed.  Salt and pepper to taste.
