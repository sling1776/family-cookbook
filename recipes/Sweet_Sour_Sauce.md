# Sweet & Sour Sauce

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

2/3 cup sugar
1/4 cup catsup
1/3 cup pineapple juice
½ cup vinegar
2 Tbsp. soy sauce
garlic powder

## Procedure

Mix ingredients together and cook sauce over medium heat. Stir in 2 Tbsp. cornstarch mixed well with 1/3 cup water. Add 1 cup well drained pineapple chunks. Add a few drops of red colorings.

## Notes

Add some sautéed onions, peppers and cooked chicken nuggets to this sauce for an easy dish, best served over rice.
