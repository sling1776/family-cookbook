# Crunchy Pecan Topping (Flaming Thanksgiving Pumpkin Pie)

## Author

Pam Reber Grange

## Ingredients

Pumpkin pie (whatever recipe you prefer)
TOPPING:
1 cup coarsely chopped pecans
2/3 firmly packed brown sugar
3 Tbsp. melted butter

## Procedure

Make pumpkin pie. 
In a small bowl, mix 1 cup coarsely chopped pecans with 2/3 cup firmly packed brown sugar. Drizzle with 3 Tbsp. melted butter and stir until uniformly moistened. Sprinkle over completely cooked pumpkin pie. 
Broil about 5 inches from heat 1-2 minutes or until topping is bubbly. Let cool, then garish with whipped cream and extra pecan halves if desired.
OR put under broiler & being chatting with the family who have come for Thanksgiving dinner. In 10 minutes you will have created flaming Thanksgiving pumpkin pie. As black smoke billows from oven, quickly remove pie & rush it out the front door, ignoring hysterical laughter from the rest of the family.
