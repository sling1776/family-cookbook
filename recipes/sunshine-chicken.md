# Sunshine Chicken

Author: Grace Ann Skousen

## Ingredients
- 8 thighs and 8 drumsticks (or other pieces)
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. basil
- 1/4 tsp. pepper
- ½ cup soy sauce
- ½ cup catsup
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup oil
- garlic to taste

## Procedure
1. Sprinkle chicken with salt, pepper and basil.  Place in baking dish.  Mix rest of ingredients in bowl.  Pour over chicken, save some for basting.  Bake 350° for 1 hour.
