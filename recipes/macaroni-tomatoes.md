# Macaroni & Tomatoes

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Cook 1 pkg. macaroni with some onion.  Season 1 lb. hamburger with small diced onion, onion powder, celery salt, & Greek seasoning.  Be careful not to get too much salt.  Add 1 qt. bottled tomatoes & heat through.
