# Infallible Rice

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- 1 medium onion minced
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- 1 cup long grain white rice
- 2 cups chicken broth

## Procedure
1. Sauté onion in butter until transparent.  Combine onion, rice and hot broth.  Bring to a boil on top of the stove.  Cover and place in oven for 20 minutes at 325°.  Serve.
