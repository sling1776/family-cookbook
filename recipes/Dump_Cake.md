# Dump Cake

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

1-20 oz. can crushed pineapple
1 large can pie filling
1 yellow cake mix (dry)
1 1/2 c. walnuts
1 1/2 cubes of butter

## Procedure

Put crushed pineapple in a 9x13 cake pan OR Dutch oven
Spoon on top pie filling.
Crumble on top cake mix (dry).
Sprinkle 1 1/2 c. walnuts on top.
Cut up butter into slices and drop on evenly. Bake 350° for 1 hour.
Top with whipped topping.

## Notes

This works well in the Dutch oven also.
