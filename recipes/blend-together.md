# Blend together:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. cherry chip cake mix
- 1/4 cup unsifted flour
- 1/8 cup cooking oil
- 1 egg
- 1/4 cup water
- 8 drops red food coloring
- 1 cup mini chocolate chips

## Procedure
1. Stir in:
2. Roll into 3/4 inch balls and put on a lightly greased cookie sheet. Bake at 350° for 8-10 minutes, or until edges are lightly brown.
3. Note: The dough is very thick and sticky so I just spray my hands
