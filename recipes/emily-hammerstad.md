# Emily Hammerstad

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ stick butter
- ½ cup milk
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 3 cups oats
- ¾ cup peanut butter

## Procedure
1. Clear a large area for laying out your cookies and put wax paper down.  Then boil the sugar, butter, milk and cocoa for 1 minute.  Remove from heat and add the vanilla, oats and peanut butter.  Stir for about a minute and then spoon the cookies onto the wax paper with an ice cream scoop.
