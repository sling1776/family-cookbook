# Dutch Oven Chicken & Rice

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Cook chicken in 1 quart water until tender.  Cut meat from the bones and return to water.  Add chopped onion, celery, shredded carrot and 1 Lipton chicken rice Soup mix.  Add equal amount of minute rice as you have water left and cover let stand for 5 minutes
