# Preserve Thumbprint Cookies:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- ½ cup finely chopped Pecans
- ½ tsp. vanilla
- ¾ cup raspberry, or your favorite preserves

## Procedure
1. Add pecans and vanilla to 1 ½ cup of dough.  Mix just until blended. Cover. Refrigerate 30 min.  Preheat oven to 350°F. Shape dough into 1-inch balls. Place, 2” apart, on ungreased baking sheets. Indent centers. Bake 10 min.  Fill each cookie with about 1 tsp. preserves. Continue baking 8 to 10 minutes.
2. Snowmen:
3. Powdered sugar
4. Icing
5. Small round candies
