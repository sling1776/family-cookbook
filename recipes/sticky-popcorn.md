# Sticky Popcorn

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1½ cups brown sugar
- ½ cup corn syrup
- 6 Tbsp. butter
- 8 large marshmallows
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 6 qts. popped popcorn

## Procedure
1. Cook brown sugar, syrup and butter until it reaches soft ball stage. Add vanilla and marshmallows. Pour mixture over popcorn as soon as marshmallows have melted.
2. Optional: Add 1 cup roasted peanuts
