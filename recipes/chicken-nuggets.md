# Chicken Nuggets

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 2 small whole skinless, boneless chicken breasts (about 1 lb) cut into 1 inch pieces
- 1 Tbsp. oil
- ½ cup Bisquick
- ½ cup cornflake crumbs
- 3/4 tsp. paprika
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1/4 tsp. pepper

## Procedure
1. Toss chicken in oil.  Mix remaining ingredients in a plastic bag.  Shake chicken in mix a few pieces at a time until coated.  Shake off excess.  Arrange on cooked sheet in a single layer.  Bake at 350° for a few minutes until done.
