# Playdough

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

1½ cups flour
1 cup salt
1 Tbsp. powdered alum
1 Tbsp. oil
1 cup boiling water
Food coloring OR Kool-aid

## Procedure

Stir flour, salt, powdered alum, oil, and boiling water until cool, then knead in food coloring. Keep dough in an airtight container when not in use. Add food coloring for different colors OR Kool-aid for a fun color and scented dough.
