# Chocolate Mint Cutout

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

1 pkg. (8oz) cream cheese, softened
3/4 cup (1-1/2 sticks) butter, softened
1 cup sugar
2 ¼ cups flour
½ tsp. baking soda
1/2 tsp. mint extract
Green food coloring
1/2 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips

## Procedure

Beat cream cheese and butter in large bowl with electric mixer on medium speed until well blended. Add sugar; beat until light and fluffy. Gradually add flour, beating until well blended after each addition. Add baking soda; mix well. Makes 3 cups of dough.
Add mint extract and a few drops of green food coloring to dough; mix well. Refrigerate 30 minutes. Roll dough to 1/8” thickness on lightly floured surface; cut with assorted 3 inch cookie cutters. Bake at 325º for 10 to 12 minutes or until edges begin to brown. Remove from cookie sheets. Melt chocolate chips on low heat, stirring until smooth. Drizzle over cookies. Makes about 3 dozen.
