# Tostaditas

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- Oil

## Procedure
1. Corn or flour tortillas
2. Heat oil, enough to cover pan about 1/4 inch.
3. Add a little salt to the oil.  It will give the tortilla a salty taste.  Cut tortillas into pie shape, or strips.  Fry for about a minute until crisp.  When all fried sprinkle lightly with salt.  Serve as a snack or as accompaniment to chile con queso or guacamole or refried beans.
