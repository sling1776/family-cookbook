# Butterscotch Brownies

Author: Janine Hinkle

## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- ½ cup butter
- 2 cups packed brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 350°. Grease 9 X 13-inch pan. Mix dry ingredients (flour, baking powder and salt); set aside. Melt butter. Add sugar, eggs and vanilla; blend. Add dry ingredients slowly. Spread in greased pan. Bake 20 to 25 minutes. Cool completely before cutting.
