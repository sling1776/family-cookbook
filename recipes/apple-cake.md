# Apple Cake

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 cups sugar
- ½ cup butter
- 2 eggs
- 2 cups shredded apples
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup chopped nuts
- ½ cup butter
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup whipping cream - not whipped.
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Procedure
1. Cream sugar, butter, eggs, then add the rest. Bake in greased 9x13 at 400° for 10 minutes.  Then 300° for 30 minutes. Serve warm with the following sauce.

3. DO NOT BOIL!! Pour over warm cake.
