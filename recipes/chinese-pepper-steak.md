# Chinese Pepper Steak

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 lbs. sirloin steak
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1 clove garlic crushed
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. ground ginger
- ½ tsp pepper
- 3 large green peppers, seeded and sliced
- 2 large onions, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- ½ tsp. sugar
- 1 cup beef bouillon
- 1 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 1/4 cup water
- 4 green onions, cut in 1 inch pieces

## Procedure
1. Freeze steak 1 hour to make it easier to slice,  then cut into 1/8 inch slices.  Heat oil in skillet; add garlic until golden.  Add steak slices brown lightly 2 minutes.  Remove meat.  Add green pepper and onions.  Cook 3 minutes.  Return beef to pan.  Add soy sauce, sugar, bouillon, chestnuts, cornstarch dissolved in water and green onion.  Simmer 2 minutes or until sauce thickens.  Serve over hot rice.
