# Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Cookies

## Author

Laurie Fryatt

## Ingredients

2 cup sugar
1 cup brown sugar
4 eggs
4 tsp. vanilla
2 cups shortening 
3 cups flour
2 tsp. soda 
2 tsp. salt
4 cups oatmeal
12 oz. chocolate chips

## Procedure

Mix sugar, brown sugar, eggs, vanilla, and shortening together and add flour, soda, salt, oatmeal and chocolate chips.
Mix with other and drop on cookie sheet. Bake at 375° for 8 to 10 minutes.

## Notes

These are the best chewy oatmeal cookies in the world. Even professed Oatmeal Cookie haters loves these cookies! Mom says you need to add these ingredients in order for the best taste.
