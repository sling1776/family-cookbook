# Tortilla chips

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- ½ lb. bulk sausage (browned)
- 1 can sliced olives
- ½ can diced green chilies
- ½ lb. med. cheddar cheese (grated)
- ½ lb. jack cheese (grated)

## Procedure
1. Mild Salsa
2. Cover microwaveable plate with a layer of chips and sprinkle half of the sausage, olives, chilies and cheeses evenly over the chips.  Make another layer with the other half. Microwave 2-3 minutes.  Serve with salsa (or guacamole) as desired.
