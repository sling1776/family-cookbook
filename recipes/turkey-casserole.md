# Turkey Casserole

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 cups diced turkey, chicken or ham
- 2 Tbsp. diced onion
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 cup slivered almonds
- 2 cups diced celery
- 2 cups cooked rice
- 6 hard cooked eggs
- 2 cans cream of chicken soup
- 3/4 cup evaporated milk
- 1 ½ cup mayonnaise

## Procedure
1. Mix above together.
2. Combine:
3. Mix with turkey mixture. Put in 9x13”pan. Top casserole with ½ cup bread crumbs and 2 Tbsp. melted margarine.  Bake 375° for 30 minutes.
