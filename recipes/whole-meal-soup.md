# Whole Meal Soup

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs. ground beef, browned
- 2 qts. hot water
- 2 cups potatoes, diced
- 1 cup celery, sliced
- ½ cup rice
- 2 cups corn
- 1 cup carrots, sliced
- 2 cups shredded cabbage
- 2 cups tomatoes, cut up
- 2 onions, chopped

## Procedure
1. Add ingredients and boil until tender and done.
