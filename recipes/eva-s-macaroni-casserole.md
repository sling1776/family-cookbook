# Eva’s Macaroni Casserole

Author: Lisa & Trisha Olsen's Mom

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Cook macaroni as directed.  Brown ½ lb. hamburger with onions. Mix macaroni and hamburger in casserole dish: add 1 can Niblets corn, drained.  Mix in 1 can tomato soup and top with cheese.  Bake at 350° until bubbly and the cheese is melted.
