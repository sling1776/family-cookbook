# Gluten Free Cheese Bread

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1/2 cup oil
- 3 eggs
- 3/4 tsp. salt
- 3 cups tapioca flour
- 1/3 cup Parmesan cheese
- 2 cups grated cheddar cheese (I use a blend of Colby Jack and Cheddar or just Colby Jack)

## Procedure
1. Mix mine in a bread maker, by hand, or in a Bosch like machine.  Drop by spoonful on a baking sheet and bake at 350 for 12 minutes.
2. (I use a soup spoon size)  Either way they taste the same and are delicious!
