# Dill Ranch Dressing

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

2 cups mayonnaise
2 cups buttermilk
2 Tbsp. onion flakes
1 tsp. parsley flakes
1 tsp. dill weed (OR add 1 tsp. more parsley)
1 tsp. salt
1 tsp. Accent (msg)
¼ tsp. garlic salt

## Procedure

Blend. Refrigerate. Shake well before use

## Notes

This dressing is one that Justin’s mom makes often for her delicious green salad. She adds tomatoes, cucumbers, hard cooked eggs, bacon and her secret ingredient “garbanzo beans” to romaine lettuce for a delicious salad we love.
