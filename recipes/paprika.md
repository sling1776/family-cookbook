# paprika

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1/3 cup milk
- 1 tsp fresh parsley

## Procedure
1. Mix stuffing mix.  Spoon stuffing across center of a 2 qt shallow baking dish.  Place chicken on each side of the stuffing.  Sprinkle chicken with paprika.  Mix soup, milk and parsley.  Pour over chicken.  Bake covered at 400° for 15 minutes or uncovered 15 minutes or until chicken is no longer pink.
