# Chocolate Zucchini Cake

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 cups cake mix
- 1/4 tsp. cloves
- 1/4 tsp. cinnamon
- 1/4 cup cocoa
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 egg, slightly beaten
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 ½ cups grated zucchini
- 6-7 Tbsp. water
- ½ cup chopped nuts

## Procedure
1. Mix dry ingredients together well and add wet ingredients. Mix in nuts.
