# Turkey Dressing

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 8-10 cups bread, cubed and dried
- 4 eggs beaten
- 1 medium onion, run through meat grinder
- 3 stalks of celery, run through meat grinder
- 1 can cream of chicken
- 1 cube of melted butter
- 1 Tbsp. poultry season
- 1 tsp. sage
- salt and pepper to taste.

## Procedure
1. Cooked giblets, run through meat grinder
2. Mix together. Add broth or hot water to slightly moisten dry bread.  Stuff turkey.  Add extra liquid to remaining dressing.  Spread in baking dish and bake at 350° for 30-40 minutes.
