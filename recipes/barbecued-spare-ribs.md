# Barbecued Spare Ribs

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 4 lbs. country style pork ribs
- 1 can condensed tomato soup
- ½ cup cider vinegar
- ¾ cup packed brown sugar
- 1 Tbsp. soy sauce
- 1 tsp. celery seed
- 1 tsp. chili powder

## Procedure
1. Trim fat on ribs and put in slow cooker. Combine ingredients and pour over ribs. Cook on low 7 -8 hours or high 4 to 5 hours.
