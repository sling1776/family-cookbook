## Procedure
1. Wash several potatoes of about the same size. Do not peel.  Put in large pot and cover with water.  Add 2 tsp. salt and cook until tender.  Be careful not to overcook.  Drain and use in any dish you desire.
2. Potato salad
3. Canyon potatoes
4. Scalloped potatoes
5. Clam chowder
6. Hashed Browns
7. Au Gratin
8. Yummy Potatoes, etc.
