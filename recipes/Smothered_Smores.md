# Smothered S’mores

## Author

Joy of Camping

## Ingredients

bag of semi-sweet chocolate morsals
bag of marshmallows
sprinkles of all kinds

## Procedure

Melt chocolate in aluminum pie plate or disposable bread tin. Place marshmallow on skewer and roll in melted chocolate. Then roll in your favorite topping.

## Notes

Topping ideas: crushed graham crackers, nuts, or colorful jimmies, shredded coconut, etc.
