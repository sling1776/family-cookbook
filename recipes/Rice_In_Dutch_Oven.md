# Rice In Dutch Oven

## Author

Joe Olsen

## Ingredients

1 quart rice
2 quarts water
1 Tbsp. oil
1 tsp. salt

## Procedure

Boil water with oil and salt. Add rice, cover tightly, return to boil then reduce heat to very low and cook 25 minutes without peeking. Fluff with fork and serve.
