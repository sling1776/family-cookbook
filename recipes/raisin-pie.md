# Raisin Pie

Author: Grandma Walker

## Ingredients
- 1 qt. water
- 1 pkg. seeded raisins
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. Bring to a boil, let simmer 5 minutes.  Thicken with 4 Tbsp. cornstarch with a little water added to it.  Remove from heat and add the juice from half a lemon and 1 Tbsp. butter.  Cook again.  Bake in two crust pie
