# Old Fashioned Fudge

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

1 Tbsp. butter
3 Tbsp. cocoa
2 cups sugar
1 cup milk
½ cup nuts

## Procedure

1. Melt in sauce pan -	 1 Tbsp. butter
2. Add and stir together - 3 Tbsp. cocoa & 2 cups of Sugar
3. Add 1 cup milk
4. Cook slowly; stir occasionally, until candy will form a soft ball when a few drops are put in a dish of cold water.
5. COOL COMPLETELY
6. Stir until no longer glossy.
7. Add ½ cup nuts
8. Pour into a buttered plate or dish

## Notes

Good recipe with good basic instructions & hints.
