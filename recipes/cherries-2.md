# Cherries

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1. Wash, stem and pit.
- 2. Place in fruit fresh mix
- 4.  Put in a freezer baggie or jars with lids and freeze completely.

## Procedure
1. 3.  Put a single layer of cherries on a cookie sheet and put in freezer just long enough to harden the outside.  This helps them not freeze together so they can be eaten one by one.
