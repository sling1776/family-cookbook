# Regular Biscuits

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 cups GF Quick Mix
- ½ cup water or milk or buttermilk

## Procedure
1. Add enough liquid to make dough form a ball. You don't have to worry about overworking the dough. Cover the board with mix and pat dough into shape by hand.  Cut into shapes and bake on a greased pan. Bake at 375° for 12-15 min.
2. Variations:
3. Shortcake: Add 4 Tbsp. sugar to mix, proceed as directed.
4. Drop Biscuits: Add more liquid and drop on grease pan.  For variety add herbs or seasoning or cheese to the dough before dropping onto pan.
