# Topping:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 12 oz. Cool Whip
- 8 oz. cream cheese
- ½ cup powdered sugar

## Procedure
1. Whip together powdered sugar and cream cheese.  Then carefully blend in cool whip.  Spread this on top of crust.  Decorate with favorite fruit; bananas, kiwi, cherries, mandarin oranges, pineapple, raisins, nuts, apples, peaches, strawberries, etc. Slice and eat.
