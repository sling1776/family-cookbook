# Chili Mac

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

1 lb. ground beef, browned and drained
1 cup water
½ cup GF macaroni noodles (uncooked)
2 cans chopped tomatoes
1 Tbsp. chili powder
1/2 cup Hamburger Helper mix

## Procedure

Combine all and simmer 20 minutes or until macaroni is cooked.
