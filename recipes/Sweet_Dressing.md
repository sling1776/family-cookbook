# Sweet Dressing

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

¼ of a white onion
1/3 cup sugar
1 tsp. dry mustard
1 tsp. salt
1 tsp. celery salt
½ cup cider vinegar
1 cup salad oil

## Procedure

In a food processor, finely chop white onion and then add sugar, mustard, salt, celery salt and vinegar. Blend. With food processor running, slowly drizzle in oil and blend to emulsify. Store in fridge until use. Shake well.

## Notes

This is great on a salad of romaine, mandarin oranges, slivered almonds & purple onion.
