# Bottling Tips

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Place filled jars in water at an angle as to not break bottles.  Not straight in.
2. Heat jars before pouring hot syrup into them as to not break bottles
3. To heat bottles place in a cake pan with 1 inch water on stove
4. Boil lids to soften the gum on them for a better seal, especially if you have had the lids for a long time.  Leave them in hot water until ready to use.  Fruit can be placed in a prepared Fruit-Fresh mixture to prevent browning, but it is optional.  Syrup should be boiling when poured over fruit.  Use only bottling jars (Kerr, Ball and Mason) to pressure cook anything.  Mayo and Miracle whip jars CAN ONLY be used in a boiling water bath.  Clean bottles and rinse well before using.  Store empty bottles either with a lid or upside down.
