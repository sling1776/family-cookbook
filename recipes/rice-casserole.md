# Rice Casserole

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 3 cups cooked rice
- 1 can (10-3/4 oz.) cream of mushroom soup
- 1 cup milk
- 2 cups cooked vegetables
- 1-1/2 tsp. instant minced onion
- 1 tsp. Worcestershire sauce
- Dash Tabasco sauce

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven 375°. Put rice in 1-1/2 qt casserole dish. Mix remaining ingredients; pour over rice. Bake for 40 minutes until hot and bubbly. Variation: Add 2-1/2 cups cut up cooked meat or poultry
