# Filling:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- 1/4 cup GF All-purpose baking flour
- 2 Tbsp lemon juice
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 tsp. baking powder

## Procedure
1. Combine flour and sugar.  Mix in beaten eggs and lemon juice.  Pour over slightly cooked crust.  Bake at 350° for 25 minutes or until set.  Cool and sprinkle with powdered sugar.
