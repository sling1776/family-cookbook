# Corn tortillas

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- Sliced olives

## Procedure
1. Sauté onions.  Add green chilies and chicken. Can add sliced olives to chicken mix.  Layer chicken mixture.  Add layer of torn up tortillas.  Layer back and forth then pour can of cream of chicken soup diluted with ½ cup water over top of all.  Cover with cheese & bake at 350° until bubbly.
