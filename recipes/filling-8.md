# Filling:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2 ½ cups milk
- 8oz. softened cream cheese
- 2 small pkg. instant vanilla pudding

## Procedure
1. Mix milk and cream cheese and beat until smooth.  Add pudding gradually.  Spread on cooled pastry.  Top with 8 oz. Cool Whip and drizzle Magic Shell (Smucker's Chocolate Fudge) over Cool Whip.
