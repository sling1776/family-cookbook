# Vanilla Buttermilk Syrup

Author: Tawna Marsh

## Ingredients
- 1 ½ cup sugar
- ¾ cup buttermilk
- ½ cup butter
- 2 Tbsp. Karo or corn syrup
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 2 tsp. vanilla extract

## Procedure
1. Directions:
2. Combine sugar, buttermilk, butter, Karo and baking soda.  Heat on medium heat until hot and melted. DO NOT HEAT TO A BOIL. Remove from heat and add vanilla extract.
