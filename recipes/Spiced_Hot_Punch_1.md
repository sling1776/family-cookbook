# Spiced Hot Punch

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

6 quarts water
 3 ½ cups sugar
 2 sticks cinnamon
 1 tsp. cloves
 1 large can pineapple juice
 1 small can orange juice
 3/4 cup lemon juice
 2 tsp. vanilla
 1 tsp. almond flavoring

## Procedure

Boil water, sugar and spices for 5 minutes then add the juices and warm to drinking temperature. Add flavorings.

## Notes

An Avon Open House at our home wouldn’t be the same without this simmering on the stove. Better made the day before.
