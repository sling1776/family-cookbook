# Pumpkin Bars

## Author

Devan Chantry

## Ingredients

2 cups sugar
1 cup oil
1 cup pumpkin
2 tsp. baking powder
1 cup chopped nuts
4 eggs, beaten
1 tsp. soda
½ tsp. salt
1 tsp. cinnamon
1 7/8 cup flour
### Cream cheese frosting
1 8-oz cream cheese	
1 cube margarine
1 lb powdered sugar
2 tsp. vanilla
