# Pizza

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

### Crust
1 pkg. yeast dissolved in 1/4 cup water
2 cups flour
2 Tbsp. shortening
1 tsp. salt
water to make dough
### Sauce:
1 can tomato sauce
1/4 can water
diced onions
good shake of parsley
½ tsp. Italian seasoning
### Toppings
Mozzarella or Monterey Jack cheese
Any other you prefer

## Procedure

Crust:
Knead and let rise once. Roll out onto 2 pizza pans. Bake 5 minutes at 375°.
Mix sauce ingredients.
Topping ideas:
 - green pepper
 - mushroom
 - sausage
 - pepperoni
Top with Mozzarella or Monterey Jack. Bake until cheese melts.
