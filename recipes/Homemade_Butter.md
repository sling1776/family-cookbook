# Homemade Butter

## Author

Catarina Badger

## Ingredients

whipping cream

## Procedure

Fill a baby jar or container with a screw on lid half way full of whipping cream. Shake the container for 6 to 8 minutes. Taking turns with other kids is a good idea. A ball of butter will form in the middle of the buttermilk liquid that is left over. Enjoy on crackers.

## Notes

My Mommy comes into my classroom each Friday to teach about a famous artist, composer or scientist and to do fun activities with us. One day for Scientist of the Month we learned how to turn a liquid into a solid & made this butter.
