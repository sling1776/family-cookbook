# Microwave Pizza Nachos

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1- 8 oz. corn tortillas chips
- 1 cup pizza sauce
- 1 1/3 cup mozzarella or Jack cheese
- 1/4 cup pepperoni
- 1/4 cup diced green chilies
- 1 Tbsp. thinly sliced green onions
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- ½ cup guacamole

## Procedure
1. On large microwave safe platter, arrange chips.  Pour pizza sauce over chips.  Sprinkle with cheese, pepperoni, olives and chilies.  Microwave on high for 2 ½-3 minutes or until cheese is melted.  Sprinkle with green onions.  Garnish with sour cream and guacamole. 4 servings
