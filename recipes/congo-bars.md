# Congo Bars

Author: Grandma Reber

## Ingredients
- 2/3 cup melted shortening
- 2 1/4 cups light brown sugar, packed
- 3 eggs
- 2 3/4 cups sifted flour
- ½ tsp. salt
- 2½ tsp. baking powder
- 1 cups chopped nuts
- 1 cup semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Procedure
1. Add shortening to sugar and mix well.  Add eggs, one at a time, beating thoroughly after each.  Add sifted dry ingredients, nuts, chocolate chips and vanilla.  Spread in greased 15x10 inch pan.  Bake at 350° for 25 minutes.  Cut into bars while warm.
