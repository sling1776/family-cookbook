# Grape Salad

Author: Kadie Allred

## Ingredients
- 8oz. cool whip
- 8oz. cream cheese
- 8oz. sour cream
- ½ cup brown sugar
- Almonds (sliced or slivered)

## Procedure
1. Grapes (red)
2. Mix cream cheese & sour cream first. Mix in cool whip. Add sugar, almonds & grapes. The amount of almonds & grapes is up to you.
