# Chicken Pot Pie

Author: Trisha Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 or 2 chicken breasts
- 1 cup cooked and cubed potatoes
- ½ cup cooked and sliced carrots
- 1/4 cup frozen peas
- 1/4 cup frozen corn
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- ½ cup milk
- 1 ready made crust (I like Pillsbury brand.  It has a flaky crust.)

## Procedure
1. Mix chicken, vegetables, soup and milk together.  Pour into prepared pie shell.  Follow pie shell direction for baking.  I usually bake at 425° for about 45 minutes. Tin foil edges after the first 20 minutes to prevent burning.
