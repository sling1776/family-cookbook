# Chex Mix with Glaze

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

9 cups Rice Chex.
1 cup coconut
9 cups Golden Graham
2 cups slivered almonds
1 cup Karo
1 cup sugar
1 cup margarine
1 tsp baking soda

## Procedure

Mix together and set aside Rice Chex, coconut, Golden Graham, and slivered almonds
Mix in heavy pan Karo, sugar and margarine
Boil for two minutes, remove from heat.
Add baking soda. Pour over cereal.
