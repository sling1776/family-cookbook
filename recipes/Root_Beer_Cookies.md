# Root Beer Cookies

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

1 cup brown sugar
½ cup softened butter
1 egg
1/4 cup buttermilk or water
1 tsp. Root beer extract
1 3/4 cup flour
½ tsp. baking soda
½ tsp. salt
### ROOT BEER GLAZE
2 cups powdered sugar
1/3 cup butter (softened)
1 ½ tsp. Root Beer extract
2 Tbsp. water

## Procedure

Mix together all ingredients well spoon onto cookie sheet and bake at 400° for 8 minutes. Let cool and glaze with Root Beer Glaze. Makes about 30 cookies.
Mix together and frost cookies when slightly cooled.

## Notes

Put two together with vanilla ice cream in the middle for a Root beer float you can eat out of your hand.
