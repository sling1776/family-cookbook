# Cup-A-Cup-A-Cup-A

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

1 cup sugar
1 cup corn syrup
1 cup peanut butter

## Procedure

Heat sugar & corn syrup ‘till boiling. Add peanut butter.
Pour over 6-8 cups of your favorite cereal. Spread on a greased cookie sheet and sprinkle chocolate chips on top for a great frosting. Melt in a warm oven & spread. Works with Rice Krispies, but Cocoa Puffs are the best.

## Notes

This is a recipe from my Snow college roommate Ruthanne. The name of the recipe is basically an easy way to memorize the recipe.
