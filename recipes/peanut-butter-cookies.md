# Peanut Butter Cookies

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 1 cup shortening
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 beaten eggs
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 3 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. Cream shortening, peanut butter, then add sugars, egg and vanilla.  Sift dry ingredients then add to previous mixture.  Form dough into small balls the size of walnuts.  Place on ungreased cookie sheet about 1 inch apart.  Press crosswise with fork.  Bake 375° for 12-15 minutes.  Makes 7 dozen cookies.
