# Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Cookies

Author: Laurie Fryatt

## Ingredients
- 2 cup sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 4 eggs
- 4 tsp. vanilla
- 2 cups shortening
- 3 cups flour
- 2 tsp. soda
- 2 tsp. salt
- 4 cups oatmeal
- 12 oz. chocolate chips

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients together and add...
2. Mix with other and drop on cookie sheet.  Bake at 375° for 8 to 10 minutes.
