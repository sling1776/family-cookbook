# Spicy Cucumbers

## Author

Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients

1 cucumber
2 Tbsp. white vinegar
2 Tbsp. sugar
1 tsp. salt
1/4 tsp. pepper
1 medium onion

## Procedure

Peel and slice cucumbers. Slice onions, and mix with cucumbers. Add all other ingredients, toss lightly and chill for 2 hours.
