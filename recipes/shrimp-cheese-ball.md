# Shrimp Cheese Ball

Author: Marjean Spencer "I didn't like cheese balls until this recipe came along" ... Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 2 large pkgs. cream cheese
- 1 jar Kraft Old English cheese
- ½ cup grated cheddar cheese
- 1 tsp. lemon juice
- ¼ - ½ tsp. onion juice
- ¼ - ½ tsp. dried chives
- 1 tsp. dry parsley
- 1 Tbsp. mayonnaise
- 1 can (4.25oz) shrimp

## Procedure
1. Mix together well and form into ball, roll in chopped nuts.  Freezes well.
