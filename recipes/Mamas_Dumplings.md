# Mama’s Dumplings

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

1 egg beaten
2/3 cup milk
1 1/2 cup flour
1 1/2 tsp. baking powder
½ tsp. salt

## Procedure

Mix together. Drop dough by spoonfuls onto hot meat or vegetables in boiling stew or soup stock. Cover. Cook for 8-10 min. Serve at once.
