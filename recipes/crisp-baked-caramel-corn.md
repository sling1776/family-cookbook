# Crisp Baked Caramel Corn

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 6 qts. popped popcorn
- ½ cup margarine
- 1 cups brown sugar
- 1/4 cup light corn syrup
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1/4 tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. vanilla

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 250°. Put popcorn in large, oven-proof bowl (such as aluminum). Melt margarine; stir in sugar, corn syrup, and salt. Bring to boil, stirring constantly. Boil without stirring for 5 minutes. Remove from heat; add soda and vanilla. Pour over popped corn; mix well. Bake for 1 hour, stirring every 15 minutes. Store in air-tight container.
