# Monster Cookies

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

2 cups sugar
1 lb. brown sugar
1 cup margarine
1 1/2 lb peanut butter
6 eggs 
½ Tbsp. vanilla
9 cups quick oatmeal
½ lb. chocolate chips
½ lb. M&Ms
1 cup chopped nuts

## Procedure

Mix well, sugar, brown sugar, margarine, peanut butter, eggs, and vanilla.
Add 9 cups quick oatmeal, chocolate chips m&Ms and chopped nuts.
Mix and Chill ½ hour. Make large cookies on greased cookie sheet. Bake 10 min. at 350°
