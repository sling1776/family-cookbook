# Bread Sticks

Author: Garth & Ruby Fielding

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Use the above basic roll recipe.  Put dough on greased cookie sheet. Press or roll out into the pan like pizza dough.  With sharp knife cut across the dough into desired size of bread stick without cutting all the way through the dough.  Score for sticks then cover with a layer of melted butter.  Top with Parmesan cheese, Mrs. Dash sprinkles, garlic salt or powder, corn meal, Italian seasoning or any desired topping.  Let rise then bake at 400° for 18 minutes.
