# Spanish Casserole

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 16 oz. Fritos corn chips
- 2 small cans pinto beans
- 1 can tomato sauce (8 oz.)
- 1 can enchilada sauce
- 2 cups grated cheese
- 1 lb. hamburger

## Procedure
1. The Chantry's first tasted this recipe while on a trip to Nebraska to visit Gene's sister Wanda Jordan.  We enjoyed it then and still do.
2. Salad made of lettuce, tomato, onion, etc.
3. Heat chips in a baking pan in 375 degree oven for 10 minutes.  Heat beans, enchilada sauce and tomato sauce in a pan on stove.  On top of the chips layer hamburger then cheese, salad, heated sauce/beans, and more cheese. Heat until cheese melts.  Serve immediately.
