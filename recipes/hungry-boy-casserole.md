# Hungry Boy Casserole

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 lb. hamburger
- 1 cup sliced celery
- ½ cup chopped green pepper
- ½ cup chopped onion
- 6 oz. can tomato paste
- 3/4 cup water
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. paprika
- ½ tsp. accent
- 1 large can pork and beans

## Procedure
1. Refrigerator biscuits or Bisquick drop biscuits
2. Sauté beef, celery, green pepper, and onion until tender.  Add tomato paste, water, salt, paprika, Accent & beans.  Pour into 9x13 baking pan. Top with biscuits.  Bake 25 to 30 minutes at 425°.
