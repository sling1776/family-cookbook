# Muffins

Author: Julie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 1 3/4 cups flour
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1 egg -beaten
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1/4 cup cooking oil
- 1/4 tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. In a mixing bowl combine flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Make a well in the center. Combine egg, milk, and oil; add all at once to flour mixture. Stir just till moistened- batter should be lumpy. Lightly grease muffin cups or line with paper baking cups; fill 2/3 full.  Bake at 400° about 20 min. or until golden.  Makes 10 to 12 muffins.
2. Variations:
3. BLUEBERRY : Prepare as above except fold 3/4 cup fresh or frozen blueberries, if desired, 1 tsp of finely shredded lemon peel.
4. CRANBERRY: Prepare as above, except combine 1 cup coarsely chopped cranberries and 2 tbsp of additional sugar.
5. JELLY:  Prepare as above, except do not use baking cups.  Fill muffin 1/3 full with batter, top with 1 tsp. jelly, jam, or preserves, top with enough batter to fill cup 2/3 way full.
6. BANANA: Prepare as above, except reduce milk to ½ cup: Add 2/3 cup mashed banana and ½ cup chopped nuts to flour mixture with the egg mixture.  Do not use baking cups.
7. PUMPKIN: Prepare as above, except add
8. 1 tsp  of ground nutmeg, and  1/8 tsp ground cloves to flour mixture.  Add ½ cup canned pumpkin to egg mixture.  Stir 1/4 cup chopped nuts to batter. No baking cups.
9. OATMEAL:  Prepare as above, except reduce flour to 1 1/3 cups and add 3/4 cup rolled oats.
