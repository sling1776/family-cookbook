# Zucchini Chocolate Cake

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 2 ½ cups flour
- ½ cup cocoa
- 2½ tsp. baking powder
- 1½ tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 3/4 cup margarine
- 2 cups sugar
- 3 eggs
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 2 tsp. grated orange peel
- 2 cups coarsely shredded zucchini
- ½ cup milk
- 1 cup chopped pecans

## Procedure
1. Combine first 6 ingredients and set aside. Cream margarine and sugar.  Add eggs one at a time. Stir in with a spoon - vanilla, orange peel and zucchini.  Add dry ingredients alternately with milk.  Stir in pecans. Pour into greased and floured 10 inch tube pan.  Bake at 350° for about 1 hour.  Drizzle with glaze after cooled on wire wrack.
