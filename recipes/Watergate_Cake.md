# Watergate Cake

## Author

Tiffanie Olsen

## Ingredients

1 white cake mix
1 small pkg. pistachio pudding
1 cup oil
3 eggs
1 can club soda
½ cup chopped nuts, optional
### Frosting
1 envelope Dream Whip
1 small pkg. pistachio pudding
1 1/2 cup cold milk.

## Procedure

Mix and bake at 350° for 45 minutes. 
Frosting:
Whip together and frost
