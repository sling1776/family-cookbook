# Three Wise Men Christmas Salad

Author: Grandma Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 large pkg. each lime and raspberry Jell-O
- 3/4 pkg. miniature marshmallows
- 1 small lemon Jell-O
- 1 20oz. can crushed pineapple, drained, reserve juice
- ½ cup mayo
- 1 cup whipping cream
- 4oz. cream cheese

## Procedure
1. Dissolve lime Jell-O in 1 cup boiling water and add 2 cups cold water.  Pour into 9x13 pan and set.  Dissolve lemon Jell-O in pineapple juice and just enough water to make 2 cups.  Bring to a boil and pour over marshmallow.  Stir to keep creamy.  Mix cream cheese and mayonnaise until smooth and add to lemon/pineapple mixture. Whip cream and drained pineapple then add this to lemon Jell-O mixture.  The mixture will melt down a little.  Spread entire mixture as second layer on the set lime Jell-O.  Set.  Prepare raspberry Jell-O the same way the lime Jell-O was prepared.  Pour on top of set lemon mixture.  Set.
