# Oatmeal Muffins

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

1 cup quick Quaker oats
1 cup buttermilk OR 1 cup milk mixed with 1 Tbsp. vinegar
1 egg, well beaten
½ cup brown sugar
1 cup flour
1 tsp. baking powder
1 tsp. salt
½ tsp. baking soda
1/3 cup oil

## Procedure

Combine wet ingredients and oats. Sift together dry ingredients and add to wet ingredients; stir. DO NOT BEAT! Add oil and stir. Fill muffin tins ½ to 2/3 full. Bake 20 minutes at 400°.
