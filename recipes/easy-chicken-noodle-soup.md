# Easy Chicken Noodle Soup

Author: Becky Concoby

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. 2 chicken breasts cooked in 4 cups chicken broth. You can add carrots or celery and bay leaf to flavor the broth if you want.
2. Remove chicken and let cool, then shred.
3. Meanwhile, add egg noodles and let cook.  If you want it to be hard chicken noodle soup, make Grandma Grace's Egg Noodles at this point, instead of adding regular noodles.  When the noodles are done add 1 can of cream of chicken soup, and shredded chicken.
4. Add salt and pepper to taste
