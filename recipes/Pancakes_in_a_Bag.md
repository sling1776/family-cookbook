# Pancakes in a Bag

## Author

Rachel Mitchell

## Ingredients

2 cup flour
2 tsp baking powder
1 tsp soda
½ tsp salt
2 cup buttermilk
2 eggs

## Procedure

Mix dry ingredients and put in a sandwich baggie to store. When ready to make pancakes add buttermilk and eggs.

Will need a little more regular milk to thin.

## Notes

A perfect way to make pancakes when you are camping.
