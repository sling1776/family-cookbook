# Olive oil for coating or cornmeal for dusting

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup pizza sauce
- 1 ½ cup grated Mozzarella cheese
- 1 ½ cup grated Muenster cheese
- 1. Prepare Perfect Pizza Dough as directed.
- Thinly sliced sautéed mushrooms

## Procedure
1. Heat oven to 450°.  Punch down the risen dough, knead a few times to remove all the air, and divide it in two.  On a lightly floured work surface, use a rolling pin to roll one portion into a 12-inch round.  Place on a lightly oiled baking sheet.  If you are using a pizza peel (wooden paddle) sprinkle it with cornmeal and then place dough on the peel.  Spread a thin layer of sauce on the crust  -- not too much or the crust will go soggy. Top your pizza with half of the Mozzarella and Muenster cheese and garnish with toppings of your choice. Bake 12-15 minutes.  When done, the crust will be golden underneath.
2. Topping Ideas:
3. Sautéed, diced, or thinly sliced green peppers
