# Chex Mix with Glaze

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- 9 cups Rice Chex.
- 1 cup coconut
- 9 cups Golden Graham
- 2 cups slivered almonds
- 1 cup Karo
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup margarine

## Procedure
1. Mix together and set aside
2. Mix in heavy pan:
3. Boil for two minutes, remove from heat,
4. Add: 1 tsp baking soda.  Pour over cereal.
