# Texas Sheet Cake

Author: Lisa Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour + 2 Tbsp. flour
- 2 cups sugar
- ½ cup buttermilk
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 4 Tbsp. cocoa
- 1 stick margarine
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 cup water
- ½ cup oil
- 2 eggs

## Procedure
1. Sift flour, sugar and cocoa.  Combine margarine, water, oil in pan and bring to a boil.  Pour over flour mixture.  Combine buttermilk with soda, pour in with mixture.  Add eggs and vanilla.  Bake at 400° for 20 minutes in jelly roll pan.
