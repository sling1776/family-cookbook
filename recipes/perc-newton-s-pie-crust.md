# Perc Newton’s Pie Crust

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 2 ½ cups flour
- 1 cup shortening
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 Tbsp. vinegar
- 1/4 cup water

## Procedure
1. "Perc Newton was the host of a radio show with helpful hints for women.  He was asked time and time again for his pie crust recipe."
2. Sift dry ingredients together into bowl.  Mix egg, water and vinegar together and add to flour mixture.  Mix until dry ingredients are moist.  Roll out on a floured board.  Makes a double crust or two single crusts.
