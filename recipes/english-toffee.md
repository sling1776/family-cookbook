# English Toffee

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- ½ lb. butter or margarine
- 2 cups sugar
- 3 Tbsp. water
- ½ tsp. cream of tartar
- 6oz. chocolate chips
- 1 cup chopped nuts

## Procedure
1. Boil the butter, sugar, water and cream of tarter in a heavy fry pan on low to the hard ball stage.  Pour onto a buttered cookie sheet.  Let stand a few minutes.  Sprinkle on chocolate chips while still warm. Spread chocolate when soft.  Sprinkle with chopped nuts.  When cooled, break into bite size pieces.
