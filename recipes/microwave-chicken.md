# Microwave Chicken

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- 1 frying chicken
- 1 Tbsp. dried onion flakes
- 1/3 cup catsup
- 1 tsp. prepared mustard
- 1/3 tsp. orange juice
- 2 Tbsp. soy sauce
- ½ garlic bud
- 2 Tbsp. flour
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- pinch of pepper

## Procedure
1. Place chicken in glass 9x13” cake pan. Mix rest of the ingredients with wire whisk until well blended. Pour over the chicken. Cook 10 minutes on Hi in microwave.  Turn over.  Put plastic wrap on & microwave for 10 more min.
