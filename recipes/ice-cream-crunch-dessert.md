# Ice Cream Crunch Dessert

Author: Diane Anderson

## Ingredients
- 1 half gallon ice cream, soft enough to spread, divided into two portions
- 1 pkg. cake mix
- 1 cup chopped nuts (optional)
- 3/4 cup softened margarine
- ½ cup brown sugar

## Procedure
1. Mix together.  Spread on cookie sheet.  Bake for 12 min. at 375°. Crumble mixture with fork while still hot, let cool, divide into thirds. In 9x13 pan, layer 1/3 of the crumbs, spread ½ of the ice cream over it, layer the next 1/3 crumbs, the remainder of the ice cream, and finish with crumbs on top.  Freeze until hard. (This will take some time.  Freeze the day before!)  Use any combination of ice cream and cake that is compatible.
