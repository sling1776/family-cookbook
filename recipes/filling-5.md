# Filling:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups chopped raisins
- 3/4 cup water
- 3/4 cup sugar

## Procedure
1. Sprinkle of salt
2. A little vanilla, A little butter, A little flour.  Simmer filling on medium heat until raisins are plump. Crumble remainder of dough on top.  Bake 20 minutes at 350 degrees.  Allow to cool 15 minutes before cutting
