# Filling:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup chocolate chips
- 1/2 cup butter
- 1-14oz. can sweetened condensed milk
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Procedure
1. Mix first four ingredients together until well blended.  Add flour, soda and oatmeal and mix well.  Press down 2/3 of the dough mixture into a greased 9x13 inch pan.  Mix the filling ingredients together in a small sauce pan and heat on medium low until blended or microwave in a microwave safe bowl for 2 to 3 minutes on low power or until blended. Pour chocolate filling over cookie dough mixture in pan.  Allow to cool slightly.  Flatten the remaining dough in your palm and place on top of chocolate mixture until mainly covered.  Bake at 350° for 30 to 35 minutes
