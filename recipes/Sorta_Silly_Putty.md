# Sorta Silly Putty

## Author

Tiffanie Olsen

## Ingredients

1 3/4 cup water
2 cups white glue
1/3 cup warm water
1 tsp. Borax

## Procedure

Mix 1 3/4 cup water and glue till smooth. Separately combine 1/3 cup warm water and borax and stir.
Pour borax mix into glue mix. Pull out globs. If needed, mix another borax solution and add to remaining glue mix. Color if desired with food coloring. Keep in Ziploc baggie.
