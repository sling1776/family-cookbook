# Baking on a Rack:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Set any Dutch oven up to bake like a regular oven.  Preheat the oven with a 9 inch round cooking rack set inside. Arrange the food in another pan that fits into the Dutch oven.  A 9 inch square pan or most round cake pans fit into a 12 inch oven.  Although this method limits the amount of food cooking at one time, the oven recycles easier for baking several batches instead of one.
