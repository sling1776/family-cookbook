# Rachael Ray's Roast Potatoes with Rosemary

Author: Kadie Allred

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs small potatoes, baby yukon gold or red
- 6 cloves garlic
- 2-3 Tbsp. extra virgin olive oil
- 2 Tbsp. fresh rosemary leaves, chopped
- salt and freshly ground pepper, to taste

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 500° or highest setting.  Halve potatoes and place on cookie sheet. Combine with garlic.  Coat potatoes and garlic with oil and season.  Place on lower rack and roast 20-25 min. turning potatoes after 12 minutes.
