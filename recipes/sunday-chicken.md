# Sunday Chicken

Author: Grandma Reber

## Ingredients
- 2/3 cup rice
- 1 envelope dry onion soup mix
- 6-8 large pieces of chicken
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 2 cans water

## Procedure
1. Layer rice, onion soup, & chicken.  Cover with mixture of soups and water.  Pour over chicken.  Cover with foil.  Bake 300° for 2 hours.
