# Bran Muffins

## Author

Chrissy Probst

## Ingredients

6 cups All-Bran Cereal
2 cups boiling water 
2 ½ cups sugar
1 cup oil
4 beaten eggs
2 cups milk plus 2 Tbsp. lemon juice
5 cups flour
2 tsp. soda
2 tsp. baking powder
1 tsp. salt

## Procedure

Pour water over cereal and let sit for 5-10 minutes. Then add remaining ingredients. Bake at 375° for 20 minutes. Can freeze mixture or refrigerate for up to 3 weeks.
