# Gingerbread Man Cookies

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

1 cup shortening
½ cup dark molasses
1 tsp. vanilla
2/3 cups brown sugar
2 egg whites
3 cups flour
1 tsp. salt
2 tsp. cinnamon
1 tsp. baking soda
½ tsp. baking powder
2 tsp. ground ginger

## Procedure

Beat shortening, molasses vanilla, brown sugar, and egg whites on high until smooth;
Combine flour, salt, cinnamon baking soda, baking powder, and ginger; then mix well with the creamed mixture;
Refrigerate overnight. Grease cookie sheets. Flour counter, roll out to 1/8” thick. Bake 350° for 6-8 minutes. Yield 2 dozen.

## Notes

These are a soft texture, similar to a sugar cookie, if not over baked.
