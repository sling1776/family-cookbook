# Flourless Thunder Brownie

## Author

Emily Hammerstad

## Ingredients

1 cup brown sugar
4-5 eggs
½ cup olive oil
5 Tbsp. cocoa
1 tsp. vanilla
1/8 tsp. salt
¼ cup walnuts (slightly crushed)

## Procedure

Preheat oven to 350°. Combine all ingredients. Pour into a greased pan and bake for 30-45 minutes, depending on elevation.

## Notes

I prefer to only use a glass, or specially made brownie pan when making this recipe.
