# Three Bean Salad

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 16 oz. can green beans
- 1 16 oz can dark red kidney beans
- 1 16 oz can garbanzo beans
- 1 medium onion chopped
- ½ cup chopped green pepper
- ½ cup sugar
- 2/3 cup vinegar
- 1/3 cup salad oil
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1/4 tsp. pepper

## Procedure
1. Directions:
2. Drain beans.  Combine beans with green pepper and onions.  Combine the sugar, oil and vinegar then pour over the vegetables.  Add salt and pepper, toss.  Chill overnight.  To serve, toss to coat beans, then drain.  Serves 6-8.
