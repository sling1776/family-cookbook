# Creamy Orange Pie

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1-8oz light cream cheese, softened
- 1-14oz sweetened condensed milk
- ¾ cup frozen orange juice concentrate, thawed
- 1-8oz carton lite whipped topping, defrosted
- 1-9 inch chocolate cookie wafer pie crust

## Procedure
1. Chocolate syrup
2. In a large bow with electric mixer, beat cream cheese until fluffy.  Add condensed milk and mix until incorporated.  Mix in orange juice until smooth and creamy.  Gently fold in whipped topping.  Pile into crust.  Refrigerate at least 1 hour or overnight.  Drizzle with chocolate syrup before serving.
