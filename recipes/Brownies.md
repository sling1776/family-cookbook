# Brownies

## Author

Diane Anderson

## Ingredients

2 cups sugar
4 eggs
1 cup margarine
1 cup flour
½ cup chopped nuts
1 tsp. vanilla
8 Tbsp. cocoa
pinch of salt

## Procedure

Cream together sugar eggs and margarine. Add Flour, nuts, vanilla cocoa and salt.
Grease and flour a 9x13 pan. Bake at 350° for 30-40 minutes.
