# Topping:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/2 cup sugar

## Procedure
1. Mix first 6 ingredients. Put berries in pie shell.  Pour sour cream mixture over the top.  Combine topping ingredients until crumbly and sprinkle on top.  Bake at 350° for 50 to 60 minutes.
