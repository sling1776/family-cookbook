# Rich Pumpkin Pie

## Author

Christine Fielding

## Ingredients

3 cups pumpkin
½ cup molasses
1 cup corn syrup
3-4 eggs, slightly beaten
2 cups milk
### Dry Ingredients
½ cup sugar
2 Tbsp. flour
1 tsp. salt
1 tsp. ginger
1 tsp. cinnamon
1/4 tsp. cloves
1/4 tsp. nutmeg

## Procedure

Combine and mix together dry ingredients.
Mix eggs and milk. Beat together. Mix pumpkin, molasses, and corn syrup. Add to the egg mixture.
Add mixture to dry ingredients.
Pour filling into an unbaked pastry shell. Makes enough for 2 ten inch pie shells. Cover crust with foil and bake at 325° for an hour or until knife comes out clean.
