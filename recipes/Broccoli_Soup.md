# Broccoli Soup

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

3-4 cups broccoli
1 (8 oz.) cream cheese
1 can evaporated milk
1 can cream of chicken soup
2 cups milk

## Procedure

Steam broccoli. Add rest of ingredients and process in blender for a few seconds, so you can still see chunks of broccoli, Heat but do not boil. Add a sprinkle of Italian seasoning.

## Notes

“This soup is one that we traditionally serve for dinner along with hoagie sandwiches on Christmas Eve” - Sue
