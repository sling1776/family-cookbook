# Daniel’s Brownies

Author: Daniel Hinkle

## Ingredients
- 2 cups sugar
- 4 eggs
- 2 Tbsp. shy of two squares of margarine
- dash of salt
- 1 Tbsp. vanilla
- 3/4 cups coconut
- 1 cup nuts
- 1 3/4 cup flour (cake flour is best)

## Procedure
1. Optional:  zest of 1 orange or ½ cup crushed raspberries - if raspberries add more flour)  Mix all together and bake in a greased cake pan at 350° for 35 minutes.
