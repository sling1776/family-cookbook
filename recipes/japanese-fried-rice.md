# Japanese Fried Rice

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Cook 2 cups rice. Add salt and soy sauce to taste.   Cook ½ head cabbage, finely shredded and 3 diagonally cut stalks celery, just until tender.  In another pan, fry 1 lb. hamburger.  Add 3 large grated carrots and 1 finely shredded onion. Cook.  SAVE ALL JUICES. Combine all ingredients.  Feeds a bunch of people
