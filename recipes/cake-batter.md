# Cake Batter

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 Tbsp. butter
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. vanilla

## Procedure
1. Beat the eggs, add the sugar, butter and vanilla and continue beating until smooth.  Sift salt, flour and baking powder together and add to batter.  Spoon mixture over hot milk/butter.  Bake at 350° about 30 minutes.  When cake is almost done, start sauce.
