# pickle brine, to taste

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- onion salt, to taste
- garlic salt, to taste

## Procedure
1. miracle whip
2. paprika
3. Ahead of time:
4. Bring potatoes to a boil and cook for 8 minutes
5. Cook bacon until crispy and drain on a paper towel.  Boil eggs for 10 minutes, remove shells, and slice. Put potatoes, bacon, and eggs in fridge to cool
6. When ready to serve:
7. In a large bowl add: potatoes, 3/4 of the egg slices, bacon, onion, mustard, pickle brine, onion and garlic salt.  Gently mix together.  Coat with miracle whip and top with remaining egg slices.  Sprinkle with paprika.
