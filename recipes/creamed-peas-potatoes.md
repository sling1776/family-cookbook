# Creamed Peas & Potatoes

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 5 to 8 medium sized red potatoes
- 1 to 2 cups frozen peas

## Procedure
1. Wash potatoes. Cut into 1 inch pieces. Cover potatoes with water in a saucepan. Bring water to a boil. Boil for 8 minutes. Add frozen peas. Continue boiling for another 8 minutes. Remove from heat. Drain water. Top with one recipe of GF White Sauce. (Medium or Thick)
