# Swiss Bread

Author: Chrissy Probst

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. yeast
- 1 cup lukewarm water
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 1/2 cups powdered milk
- 4 cups warm water
- 2 Tbsp. salt
- 4 eggs
- 1/2 cup butter

## Procedure
1. Mix and let work:
2. Add:
3. about 10 cups flour (can mix wheat/white)
4. Mix and let raise about 1 hour.  Punch down and let rest 5-10 minutes.  Mold into loaves or braid it.  Bake at 350° for 30 minutes.
