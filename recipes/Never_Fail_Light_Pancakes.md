# Never Fail Light Pancakes

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

Sift together:
2 cups flour
2 tsp. baking powder
1 tsp. salt
1 Tbsp. sugar
3/4 tsp. baking soda
2 cups buttermilk (or regular milk with a little lemon juice)
2 eggs
1 cup melted butter/margarine

## Procedure

Sift flour baking powder, salt, sugar, baking soda. Add Buttermilk, eggs and butter. Mix and cook on heated griddle
## Note
For a healthier version try it with 1 cup whole wheat flour & 1 cup white flour & 1 cup applesauce in place of the melted butter. They taste great either way.
