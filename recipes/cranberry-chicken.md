# Cranberry Chicken

Author: Grace Ann Skousen

## Ingredients
- 1 whole chicken or 4 breasts (or turkey breast)
- 1 can whole cranberry sauce
- 1 envelope dry onion soup mix
- ½ cup water.

## Procedure
1. Place Chicken and water in pot. Sprinkle soup mix over chicken. Pour cranberry sauce on top. Cover and cook on low 6-7 hours.
