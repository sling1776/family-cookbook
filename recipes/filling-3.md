# Filling:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- ½ pound pork sausage
- 3 strips cooked bacon, crumbled
- 2 Tbsp. finely chopped onion
- 2 Tbsp. fresh chopped chives
- 4 eggs, lightly beaten
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 1 cup cheddar cheese
- 1 cup mozzarella cheese
- 1/3 cup grated parmesan cheese

## Procedure
1. Brown sausage and onion.  Stir in chives and bacon.  Fill each muffin cup with about 1/2 tsp.  sausage mixture. Combine eggs and cheeses.  Spoon over sausage mixture. Bake at 375° for 20-25 minutes.  Cool for 5 minutes.  Serve warm.
