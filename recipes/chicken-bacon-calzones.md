# Chicken Bacon Calzones

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 2 cups cooked chicken, cubed
- ½ jar Alfredo sauce
- 6 pieces bacon, cooked & crumbled
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- ½ cup cheese, (any kind you like)

## Procedure
1. Your favorite bread dough
2. Mix together all ingredients, except for bread dough.  Roll out bread dough into 12 inch circle.  Place chicken mixture on one side of circle.  Fold over other side of circle onto chicken mixture and pinch edges shut.  Cut slits into dough.  Let rise for about 20 to 30 minutes.  Bake at 375º for 35 to 45 minutes or until golden brown.
