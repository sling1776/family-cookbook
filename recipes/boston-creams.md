# Boston Creams

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 cups milk
- 1/4 cup butter

## Procedure
1. Caramelize sugar in heavy pan, heating until melted and browned.  Add milk.  Dissolve sugar that turns hard when milk is added.
2. Add: 4 cups sugar and cook to soft ball stage
3. Add:  2 tsp. vanilla
4. Let cook, then beat by hand until smooth and stiff (like fudge).  Spread in buttered pan, cool and cut into squares.
