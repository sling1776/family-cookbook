# Dutch Oven Beef Stew

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Brown stew meat in small amount of oil.
2. Add chopped onion and sauté.  Add 1 qt. water and simmer until meat is tender.  Add potatoes, carrots, celery and cook until vegetables are tender. (More water may be added, as needed.)  Beef bouillon can be added for flavor.  Thicken with a 3/4 cup water and 1/4 cup flour mixed.
