# Honey Pan Rolls

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

1 cup warm milk (70° to 80°)
1 egg & 1 egg yolk (save white for glaze)
1/2 cup vegetable oil
2 Tbsp. honey
1-1/2 tsp. salt
3-1/2 cups bread flour
2-1/4 tsp. active dry yeast
### Glaze
1/3 cup sugar
2 Tbsp. butter, melted
1 Tbsp. honey
1 egg white

## Procedure

In bread machine pan, place the first eight ingredients in order. Select dough setting (after 5 minutes of mixing; add water or flour if needed.) When cycle is completed, divide dough into 24 balls. Place 12 balls each in two greased 8-in. baking pans. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled, about 30 minutes. For glaze, combine sugar, butter, honey and egg white; drizzle over dough. Bake at 350° for 20-25 minutes or until golden brown.
