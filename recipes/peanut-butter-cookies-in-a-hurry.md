# Peanut Butter Cookies (In a Hurry)

Author: Ruby Fielding

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. Duncan Hines yellow cake mix
- 1 cup peanut butter, plain or chunky
- ½ cup oil
- 2 Tbsp water
- 2 eggs

## Procedure
1. Combine all ingredients and mix well.  Drop on ungreased cookie sheet.  Press criss- cross on each cookie with fork.  Bake at 350° for 10-12 minutes or until golden then cool 1 minute on sheet before removing.  Makes 4-5 dozen 2 ½ inch cookie.
