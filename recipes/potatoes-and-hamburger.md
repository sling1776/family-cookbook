# Potatoes and Hamburger

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Cook potatoes in their jackets with salted water.  Brown 1 lb. ground beef with lots of onion.  Slow cook until ground beef is browned.  (The onions make the mixture a bit watery so cook until moisture is mostly gone.)  Chop potatoes with a chopping can or cut up potatoes.  Mix well and steam to mix flavors.
