# Creamed Soup Base

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 cup dry milk powder
- 1 cup white rice flour OR 3/4 cup corn starch
- 2 Tbsp. dehydrated minced onions
- ½ tsp. pepper
- ½ tsp. salt
- 3 Tbsp. GF Chicken or Vegetable Bouillon or Soup Base

## Procedure
1. Combine all ingredients and mix well. Store in an airtight container. This is the equivalent of 8 or 9 cans of soup. Note: Western Family brand cream soups are GF.
