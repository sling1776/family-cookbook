# Hamburger Soup

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1 can pork & beans (large)
- 1 large onion
- 2 cans minestrone soup
- 1 quart tomatoes
- 2 cans water
- 1 tsp. Italian Seasoning
- 1 can string beans
- 1 tsp. oregano
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- garlic salt

## Procedure
1. Brown hamburger and onion together. Add tomatoes and seasonings. Simmer 10-15 minutes. Add rest of ingredients. Simmer & serve.
