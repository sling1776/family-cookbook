# English Toffee

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

½ lb. butter or margarine
2 cups sugar
3 Tbsp. water
½ tsp. cream of tartar
6oz. chocolate chips
1 cup chopped nuts

## Procedure

Boil the butter, sugar, water and cream of tarter in a heavy fry pan on low to the hard ball stage. Pour onto a buttered cookie sheet. Let stand a few minutes. Sprinkle on chocolate chips while still warm. Spread chocolate when soft. Sprinkle with chopped nuts. When cooled, break into bite size pieces.

## Notes

This is Caroly Trost’s recipe. She was in my ward in Granger when I was RS President. She moved away, but I met her again in Ontario, CA. That is when I got this recipe.
