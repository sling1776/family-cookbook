# Lemonade Apples

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

6 Granny Smith apples, peeled, cored, and sliced
2 Tbsp. sugar
3 Tbsp. lemon juice, bottled or fresh

## Procedure

In a medium bowl, mix sliced apples, sugar, and lemon or lime juice. Chill until ready to serve.

## Notes

This is also makes a great kid-friendly snack. Put prepared apples in Ziploc bags and refrigerate for a healthy, but sweet snack.
Serves 6.
