# Canyon Potatoes

## Author

Grace Fielding

## Ingredients

2-3 pre-cooked potatoes, peeled
2-3 slices bacon
½ cup chopped onion

## Procedure

Cook bacon on griddle, add onions and cook. Add cut potatoes and heat thoroughly. Season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

This recipe was a staple item when we lived up Payson Canyon and made this side dish for breakfasts while camping in the canyon.
