# Soft Sugar Cookies

Author: Angela Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 cup butter
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 tsp. soda
- 2 tsp. cream of tartar
- 1 cup milk
- 5 cups flour

## Procedure
1. Mix together.  Roll the size of a lime or use a cookie scoop and then roll in sugar.  Place on greased cookie sheet. Do not flatten.  Bake at 375° for 12 minutes.  Cool and frost.
