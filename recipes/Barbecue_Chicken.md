# Barbecue Chicken

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

2 lemons thickly sliced OR 4 tsp. lemon juice
1/4 cup vinegar
1 tsp. salt
1 Tbsp. prepared mustard
½ tsp. pepper
2 Tbsp. sugar
1/4 tsp. cayenne pepper
1/4 cup margarine
½ cup catsup
2 tsp. Worcestershire sauce
1 ½ tsp. liquid smoke

## Procedure

Simmer all ingredients (except catsup, Worcestershire sauce and liquid smoke) for 20 minutes.
Add remaining ingredients.
Bring to boil. Pour over skinned chicken pieces. Bake slowly at 350° for 1½ hours or 325° for 2 hours, or 300° for 3 hours.
