# Salmon Patties

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. (6 oz.) chicken stuffing mix
- 3/4 cup water
- 1/3 cup mayonnaise
- 1 Tbsp. lemon juice,
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- chopped green onions

## Procedure
1. 2 cans (6 oz. each) salmon, drained, skin discarded. Smash the bones fine between your fingers and mixed in (A trick from my Grandma Reber to get more calcium)
2. Mix all together. Shape into 12 patties, using about 1/3 cup of the stuffing mixture for each patty; cover. Refrigerate 1 hour. Cook 6 min. on heated skillet or until golden brown on both sides, turning carefully after 3 min.
