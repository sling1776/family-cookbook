# Cookie S’mores

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 package Keebler Fudge Stripes cookies
- 1 package large marshmallows

## Procedure
1. OR chocolate covered graham crackers
2. Roast your marshmallow over the campfire on your fire-stick. When marshmallow is done, place it between two cookies and gently pull the marshmallow off the stick. The cookies seem to melt in your mouth.
