# Jell-O Salad

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

1 pkg. vanilla pudding (use water)
1 jello pkg.

## Procedure

Cook one large pkg. vanilla pudding using water instead of milk (do not use instant pudding). Add any flavor of jello. Refrigerate to set up, beat in a large cool whip when cooled.

## Notes

Note: This makes a great dessert when made with lemon jello and served with a vanilla wafers on the side!
