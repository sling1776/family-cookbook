# Ramen Noodle Salad

Author: Tawna Marsh

## Ingredients
- ½ cabbage, chopped
- 1 pkg. chicken ramen noodles
- ½ cup sliced almonds
- 4-5 green onions, chopped
- 3 Tbsp. sesame seeds
- ½ cup oil
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 3 Tbsp. vinegar
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1/4 tsp. pepper

## Procedure
1. Mix together and break up noodles into bite size pieces.  Add dressing:
2. seasoning packets from noodles.
3. Mix well, pour over salad and refrigerate.
