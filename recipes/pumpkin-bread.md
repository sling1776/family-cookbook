# Pumpkin Bread

Author: Alecia Bales

## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. ginger
- 1/4 tsp. baking soda
- 1/4 tsp. cloves
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/3 cup shortening
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup pumpkin
- 1/4 cup milk

## Procedure
1. Beat sugar, shortening, and eggs.  Add pumpkin and mix well.  Add remaining ingredients.  Pour batter into greased loaf pan.  Bake 350° for 55-60 minutes.  Cool in pan 10 minutes.  Remove from pan.  Cool.
