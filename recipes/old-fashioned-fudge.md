# Old Fashioned Fudge

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 1.  Melt in sauce pan -	 1 Tbsp. butter
- 2.  Add and stir together - 3 Tbsp. cocoa
- 2 cups sugar
- 3.  Add 1 cup milk
- 5.  COOL COMPLETELY
- 6.  Stir until no longer glossy.
- 7.  Add ½ cup nuts
- 8.  Pour into a buttered plate or dish

## Procedure
1. 4.  Cook slowly; stir occasionally, until        candy will form a soft ball when a few       drops are put in a dish of cold water.
