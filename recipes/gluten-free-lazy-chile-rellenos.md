# Gluten Free Lazy Chile Rellenos

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 8 whole roasted green chilies
- 1 1/2 cups Monterey jack cheese shredded
- 5 whole eggs
- 2 cups whole milk
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 325°.  Mix together eggs, milk, salt, pepper, paprika and cayenne.  Cut chilies in half and add a single layer of chilies on the bottom of a 9 x 13 pan. Top chilies with half of the grated cheese.  Repeat with another layer of chilies and another layer of cheese.  Pour egg mixture over all.  Put 9 x 13 in a sheet pan with 1/2 inch of water in the bottom.  Bake for 35 to 40 minutes or until done.
