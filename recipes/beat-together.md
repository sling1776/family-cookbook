# Beat together:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 1/2 tsp. salt
- 2 eggs
- 6 Tbsp. shortening

## Procedure
1. Add scalded milk.  Slowly add 6-7 cups flour.  Add yeast.  Let raise.  Roll out with butter, brown sugar, nuts and cinnamon.  Roll up like jelly roll.  Let rise 1 hour.  Bake at 375°  for 15 min. Note: I have even frozen the cinnamon rolls before letting them raise 1 hour, then when I want fresh rolls I thaw, raise, & bake.
