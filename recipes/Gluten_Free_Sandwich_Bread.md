# Gluten Free Sandwich Bread

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

1 ½ cups blanched almond flour
¾ cup arrowroot powder
¼ cup flax seed meal
½ tsp. celtic sea salt
½ tsp. baking soda
4 eggs
1 tsp. agave nectar
1 tsp. apple cider vinegar

## Procedure

In a medium bowl, combine almond flour, arrowroot, flax meal, salt and baking soda.
In a larger bowl, blend eggs 3-5 minutes until frothy. Stir agave and vinegar into eggs.
Mix dry ingredients into wet.
Pour batter into a well greased 7.5" x 3.5" loaf pan. Bake at 350º for 30-35 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into center of loaf comes out clean. Cool and serve.

## Notes

Blanched almond flour has become one of my family’s favorite for baking. Don’t make the mistake of using UNblanched almond flour. All the skins from the almonds change the taste & texture of all the recipes.
