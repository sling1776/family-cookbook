# Impossible Pie

## Author

Quinn Mitchell

## Ingredients

4 eggs
1 stick (1/2 cup) butter
½ cup flour
2 cups milk
¾ cup sugar
1 cup shredded coconut
½ tsp. vanilla

## Procedure

Blend all ingredients thoroughly. Pour into a 10” pie plate. Bake at 350° for one hour or until a knife comes out clean. Chill and serve.
