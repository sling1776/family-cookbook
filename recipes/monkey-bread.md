# Monkey Bread

Author: Sammi Jo Stewart

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup butter
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup marshmallows

## Procedure
1. Melt together. (You can add 1/2 cup whipping cream, I never do.)  Grease Bundt pan and arrange 16 frozen rolls.  Pour syrup evenly over rolls. Rise overnight in fridge.  Bake at 325° for 25-30 minutes. Turn upside down on platter; syrup will run over rolls. Cool.
