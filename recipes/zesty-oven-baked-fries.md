# Zesty Oven Baked Fries

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 3 medium or 2 large potatoes
- 1 Tbsp. grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 Tbsp. oil
- ¼ tsp. salt
- ¼ tsp. garlic powder
- ¼ tsp. paprika
- ¼ tsp. pepper

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 450°.  Spray pan with cooking spray or use parchment paper.  Peel and cut potatoes into julienne fry shape. Combine all ingredients in a plastic bag.  Shake to coat the potatoes.  Pour potatoes in a single layer on baking sheet and bake for 20 minutes or until golden brown.
