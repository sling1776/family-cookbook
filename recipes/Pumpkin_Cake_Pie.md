# Pumpkin Cake Pie

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

2 cup cake mix (divided)
1/2 cup water (divided)
1 cup pumpkin
1/3 cup sugar
1/4 tsp. salt
1 egg
1 ½ tsp. pumpkin pie spice

## Procedure

Sprinkle 1 cup cake mix in 8 x 8 pan. Sprinkle 1/4 cup water on cake mix. Mix pumpkin, sugar, salt, egg and pumpkin pie spice together and pour over cake mix & water:
Sprinkle over the top of pumpkin mix 1 cup cake mix and 1/4 cup of water.
Bake at 350 for 25 minutes.
Serve with whipped cream or substitute.
