# Dill Ranch Dressing

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 2 cups mayonnaise
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 2 Tbsp. onion flakes
- 1 tsp. parsley flakes
- 1 tsp. dill weed (OR add 1 tsp. more parsley)
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. Accent (msg)
- ¼ tsp. garlic salt

## Procedure
1. Blend.  Refrigerate. Shake well before use
