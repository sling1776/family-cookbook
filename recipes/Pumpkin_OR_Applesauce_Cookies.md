# Pumpkin OR Applesauce Cookies

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

2 cups GF cookie mix
½ tsp. cinnamon OR pumpkin pie spice
1 egg
½ tsp. vanilla
1 cup applesauce OR pumpkin

## Procedure

Mix well and add:
Nuts, chocolate chips, or raisins as desires
Bake at 350° for 12 - 15 minutes.
