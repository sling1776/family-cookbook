# Mini Pizza

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Refrigerated biscuits.
2. pizza sauce
3. toppings of choice
4. Monterey jack cheese
5. Separate biscuits and flatten out.  Spread pizza sauce (canned or home made.) Put on slices of pepperoni, green pepper chopped, chopped olives, sausage or hamburger browned or anything that would taste good.  Sprinkle grated Monterey Jack and bake until cheese melts at 400° for 8-10 minutes.
