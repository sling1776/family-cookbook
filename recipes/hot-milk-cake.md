# Hot Milk Cake

Author: Bonnie Chantry via Teresa Smith, a friend in the Jefferson Branch where we are serving a stake mission. This is a small, old fashioned cake that always leaves us wanting more. Every time we have it I have memories of Grandma Walker come to mind for it is like having lovin' from the oven.

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Prepare 8" square pan by buttering it and putting ½ c. milk and 1 T. butter in it. Place in the oven while it is heating to 350°.  Leave until the butter melts.  Meanwhile make the cake batter.
