# Banana Fruit Cocktail

## Author

Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients

### Syrup
 4 cups sugar
 7 cups water
 pinch of salt
 ### Fruit
 2 small cans undiluted orange juice
 6 Tbsp. lemon juice
 6 bananas sliced
 2 cans (20 oz) crushed pineapple with juice
 7-Up

## Procedure

Heat syrup ingredients to a boil. Cool. Mix with fruit and freeze. Serve with 7-Up.
