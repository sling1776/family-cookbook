# Peanut Butter Pie

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1 (9”) graham cracker crust
- 1 (8 oz.) cream cheese softened
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- ½ cup peanut butter
- ½ cup milk
- 1 8 oz container of cool whip, thawed
- 1/4 cup chopped peanuts

## Procedure
1. Beat cream cheese until fluffy.  Mix in powdered sugar and peanut butter.  Slowly add milk, mixing well.   Fold in whipped topping.  Pour into crust.  Sprinkle with nuts.  Freeze until firm.  6-8 servings
