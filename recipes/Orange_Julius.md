# Orange Julius

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

1- 6 oz. can frozen orange juice concentrate
 1 cup cold water
 1 cup cold milk
 ½ cup sugar
 1 tsp. vanilla
 14 ice cubes (1 tray)

## Procedure

Put all but ice in blender container. Blend, adding ice cubes a few at a time, until there are no more chunks of ice.

## Notes

Makes 1 quart.
