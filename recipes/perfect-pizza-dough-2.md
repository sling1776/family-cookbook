# Perfect Pizza Dough

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- Butter for coating
- 1/3 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1 tsp. dried oregano
- ½ tsp. dried basil
- ½ tsp. dried dill
- ½ tsp. garlic salt
- 2 Tbsp. Olive oil

## Procedure
1. Punch down the risen dough and knead it a few times.  Butter a baking sheet and set it aside.
2. Shape the dough into a log and cut it into 24 pieces.  Roll each piece into a ball, then roll the ball into a rope 8 inches long.  With the heel of your hand, flatten the rope so it is 1 inch wide.  Leaving ½ inch at the top, cut the dough into three lengthwise strips (the strips will remain attached at the top).  Braid the strips, then press the ends together.  On a plate, combine the cheese, herbs, and garlic salt.  With a pastry brush, coat the top and sides of the breadsticks with olive oil.  One at a time, roll the breadsticks in the cheese-herb mixture and gently press down to help it adhere.
3. Heat the oven to 400°.  Place the breadsticks on the baking sheet and let them rise for 20 minutes, then bake them for 8 minutes or until the breadsticks are golden.  Cool on a wire rack.  Serve warm.
