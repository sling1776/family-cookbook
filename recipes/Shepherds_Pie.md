# Shepherd’s Pie

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

1 lb. Hamburger
1 med. onion
1 can green beans, drained
1 recipe cream of tomato soup, seasoned to taste
3 cups of mashed potatoes (fresh or flaked)
lots of grated cheese.

## Procedure

Brown hamburger and onion
Place in 8 x 8 baking pan. Add: green beans
Cover with tomato soup
Top with mashed potatoes (fresh or flaked) & lots of grated cheese. Bake 350° for 30 min.
