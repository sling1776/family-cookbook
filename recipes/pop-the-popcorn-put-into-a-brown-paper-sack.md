# Pop the popcorn & put into a brown paper sack.

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup margarine
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 cup light corn syrup
- 1 1/2 tsp. water

## Procedure
1. Add the following into a sauce pan and boil.
2. Bring to a full boil. Take off the heat. Then add 1/4 tsp. baking soda
3. Pour over popcorn in sack.  Shake bag until all popcorn is covered.  Make into balls
