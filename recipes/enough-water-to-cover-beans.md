# Enough water to cover beans

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. pkg.  bacon
- 1 medium onion - chopped
- chopped tomatoes (canned or fresh)

## Procedure
1. cilantro, chopped
2. Monterey jack cheese
3. Soak beans overnight.  Put beans, salt and water in slow cooker or pot on low heat.  Cook until tender.  May take several hours.  When beans are tender, put in cilantro & chopped tomatoes.  Top with cheese & tortilla chips.
