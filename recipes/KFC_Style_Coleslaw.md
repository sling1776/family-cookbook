# KFC Style Coleslaw

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

1 head cabbage, finely shredded (1½ to 2 lbs.)
¼ cup finely diced carrots
2 Tbsp. finely diced onions
½ cup sugar
1 tsp. salt
½ cup Miracle Whip
½ cup sour cream
2 Tbsp. vinegar
2 tsp. oil

## Procedure

Mix cabbage, carrots, onions, sugar and salt. In another bowl combine Miracle Whip, sour cream, vinegar, and oil. Mix well. Pour dressing on cabbage mixture and stir until veggies are covered with dressing. Chill at least one hour.

## Notes

This recipe came from the Empty Nesters gatherings we held in Albany. Each time the Empty Nester’s met at our home, I would ask LuDean Worthen to make and bring this salad.
