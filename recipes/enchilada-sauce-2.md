# Enchilada Sauce

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 1½ cups water
- 2 Tbsp. flour
- 1½ tsp. onion flakes
- 1 tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. chili powder
- 1/4 tsp. garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp. baking powder
- 1/8 tsp. pepper
- ½ tsp. paprika
- 1 Tbsp. Parmesan cheese

## Procedure
1. Combine all, but cheese, bring to a boil then simmer 10 minutes.  Blend in Parmesan cheese.
