# Citrus glaze:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 Tbsp. butter, melted
- 3 Tbsp. orange juice
- ½ tsp. vanilla extract

## Procedure
1. Mix grated orange rind with sugar. Roll each roll into a 9-inch rope. Tie in a loose knot. Roll each knot in melted butter and then in rind/sugar mixture. Place on baking sheet sprayed with cooking spray. Cover with plastic wrap and let rise until doubled in size. Remove wrap. Bake at 350° F 15-20 minutes. Remove from baking sheet and place on a cooling rack. Brush with citrus glaze while still warm.
