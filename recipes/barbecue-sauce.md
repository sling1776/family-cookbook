# Barbecue Sauce

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- 1 med. onion, chopped
- 2-4 Tbsp. butter
- 1 cup catsup
- 2 Tbsp. Worcestershire
- 1/4 tsp. pepper
- ½ cup chopped celery
- 1 cup water
- 2 Tbsp. vinegar
- 4 Tbsp. lemon juice
- 1 Tbsp. mustard
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 Tbsp. sugar

## Procedure
1. Brown onion in butter.  Add other ingredients and pour over spare ribs or other meat and let simmer.
