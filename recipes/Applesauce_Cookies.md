# Applesauce Cookies

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

½ cup shortening
1 cup sugar
1 egg
2 cups flour
½ tsp. each of cloves, cinnamon, nutmeg & salt
1 tsp. baking soda
1 cup applesauce
1 cup nuts
1 cup raisins

## Procedure

Cream shortening, sugar and egg together. Sift dry ingredinets: flour, cloves, cinamon, nutmeg, salt, and baking soda
Add to creamed mixture alternately with applesauce, nuts and raisins.
Drop by spoonfuls onto a greased cookie sheet. Bake at 350° for 12 minutes. Makes 3 doz.

## Notes

Every cookbook should have a very moist cookie recipe - applesauce cookies are great for this.
