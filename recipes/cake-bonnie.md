# Cake "Bonnie"

Author: Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. miniature marshmallows
- 1 yellow cake mix (mixed according to directions)
- 1 can cherry pie filling OR your favorite fruit

## Procedure
1. Cover bottom of cake pan with layer of marshmallows. Pour cake batter oven them evenly.  Spoon pie filling evenly over batter.  Bake in 350° for 30-35 minutes.
