# Egg Bread

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

¾ cup milk
2 eggs
3 cups bread flour
1 ½ tsp. salt
3 Tbsp. butter or margarine
¼ cup sugar
2¼ tsp. yeast

## Procedure

Place all ingredients into bread pan, select Light Crust & regular setting and push Start.

## Notes

This is my favorite loaf of homemade bread. It smells delicious and melts in your mouth. Makes a 1 ½ lb. loaf in the bread machine. Note: Heat up a slice for 10 seconds in the microwave to make it taste melt in your mouth smooth even two or three days after baking.
