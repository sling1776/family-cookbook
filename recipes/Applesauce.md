# Applesauce

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

6 large granny apples, cored and sliced
½ cup sugar
½ tsp. cinnamon
Little water (less than half a cup)

## Procedure

Prepare apples and place in large kettle with water. Simmer and stir until apples turn into sauce. Add sugar and cinnamon.
Makes 4 cups.

## Notes

We tried this recipe because our apples were too sour to eat and began turning soft. It turned out great. We enjoyed it as a side dish with our pork chops and we made some great cookies with it.
