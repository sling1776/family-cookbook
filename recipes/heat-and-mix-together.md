# Heat and mix together:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup honey
- ½ cup canola oil
- 2 Tbsp. cinnamon
- 1 tsp. nutmeg
- pinch allspice

## Procedure
1. Pour over cereal mixture, blending well.  Then place mixture on baking sheet in 225° oven for 1 1/2 hours stirring every 15 minutes.  Cool well and place in air tight container.
