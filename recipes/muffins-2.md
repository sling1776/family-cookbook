# Muffins

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 2-1/2 cups GF Quick mix
- 1 cup milk or water
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 egg, beaten

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 425°.
2. Butter muffin pans. Place mix in a medium bowl.  Add sugar and mix well.  In a small bowl, combine egg and milk or water.  Add all at once to dry ingredients.  Stir until just blended.  Fill prepared muffin pans 2/3 full.  Bake 15-20 minutes, until golden blown.  Serve hot with butter and honey, if desired.  Makes 12 large muffins.
3. Variations:  Raisin, Date or nut muffins: Add ½ cup finely chopped raisins, dates, or nut to dry ingredients. Before baking sprinkle generously with mixture of cinnamon and sugar.
4. Blueberry muffins: Add 1 cup of well drained blueberries to dry ingredients before adding liquid ingredients.
