# Broccoli Soup

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- 3-4 cups broccoli
- 1 (8 oz.) cream cheese
- 1 can evaporated milk
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 2 cups milk

## Procedure
1. Steam broccoli.  Add rest of ingredients and process in blender for a few seconds, so you can still see chunks of broccoli,  Heat but do not boil.  Add a sprinkle of Italian seasoning.
