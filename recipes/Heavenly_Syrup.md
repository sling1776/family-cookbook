# Heavenly Syrup

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

1 cup heavy whipping cream
1 cup sugar
1 cup light corn syrup
1 tsp coconut extract
1 tsp almond extract

## Procedure

Bring sugar & corn syrup to a boil, stirring often. Remove from heat, stir in whipping cream and extracts. Serve warm.

## Notes

This is simply known as “White Syrup” in our home. It makes anything for breakfast taste divine.
