# Beat the following on high until smooth;

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup shortening
- ½ cup dark molasses
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 2/3 cups brown sugar
- 2 egg whites
- 3 cups flour
- 1 tsp. salt
- 2 tsp. cinnamon
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. baking powder
- 2 tsp. ground ginger

## Procedure
1. Combine the following then mix well with the creamed mixture;
2. Refrigerate overnight.  Grease cookie sheets.  Flour counter, roll out to 1/8” thick.  Bake 350° for 6-8 minutes. Yield 2 dozen.
