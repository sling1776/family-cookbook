# Vanilla Peach Jam

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 5 ½ cups sugar
- 6 cups fresh peaches
- 2 Tbsp. lemon juice
- 1 ¾ oz. pectin
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract

## Procedure
1. Peel, pit and chop peaches.  In saucepan, combine peaches, lemon juice, and pectin.  Cook over high heat stir constantly about 5 minutes or until full rolling boil.  Stir in sugar.  Return to a full rolling boil.  Boil hard for 1 minute, stirring constantly.  Remove from heat and add vanilla extract. Skim off foam.  Ladle in hot sterilized jars process in boiling bath 5 minutes at sea level.  15 minutes for elevations between 3,000-6,000 feet.**NOTE-Most canning recipes give cooking times for sea level, unless otherwise stated.  Add the needed time to these times for the higher elevation in your area.
