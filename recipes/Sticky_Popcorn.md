# Sticky Popcorn

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

1½ cups brown sugar
½ cup corn syrup
6 Tbsp. butter
8 large marshmallows
1 tsp. vanilla
6 qts. popped popcorn
Optional: Add 1 cup roasted peanuts

## Procedure

Cook brown sugar, syrup and butter until it reaches soft ball stage. Add vanilla and marshmallows. Pour mixture over popcorn as soon as marshmallows have melted.

## Notes

Jared used this recipe on his mission to the Philippines. The grandkids love to make this Grandma Sue.
