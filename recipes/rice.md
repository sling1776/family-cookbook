# Rice

Author: Julie Chantry

## Ingredients
- Butter and parsley (or favorite spice)

## Procedure
1. Pour amount you want to cook in a good size and add lots of water, with your hands, swish rice to clean.  Pour out cloudy water and repeat until water is clear.
2. To cook:  Add enough water so that when index finger is resting on top of rice, water rises to just above the first joint.  Add salt if desired.  Bring water to boil, put on a lid (not too tight) and reduce heat. Remove from heat and put lid on tight. Let sit until ready to serve.
3. Top with:
4. Thick chicken soup
5. Canned cream soups
6. Oriental sauces
7. Vegetables (zero fat)
8. Salsa (zero fat)
