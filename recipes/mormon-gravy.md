# Mormon Gravy

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- 4 Tbsp. meat drippings (bacon, ham, sausage, or shortening)
- 3 Tbsp. flour

## Procedure
1. Heat in frying pan and add
2. This is the critical part of making the gravy.  If you don't brown the flour in the drippings you have white sauce instead of gravy.  Watch it closely and do not let it brown too much, so stir constantly.  When it is lightly brown set the pan off the heat and stir in 2 cups milk. Return to the heat and stir until thick and smooth.  Salt and pepper to taste.  Good on mashed potatoes, corn bread.  Good with crisp bacon or sausage.
