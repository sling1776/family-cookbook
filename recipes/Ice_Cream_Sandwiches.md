# Ice Cream Sandwiches

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

2 cups GF Basic Cake mix 
1/4 cup cocoa powder
1 tsp. vanilla
1 egg, slightly beaten
3/4 cup ice cold water
Ice cream

## Procedure

Stir ingredients until well blended. Pour into an 8 inch square pan. Bake at 350° in an 11 x 15 inches pan for 25-30 minutes.
Cool completely. Use ice cream in a rectangular box and cut into the size slices you like. Then cut the cake to fit the ice cream. Keep frozen until ready to eat. They are yummy!!
