# Chinese Noodle Fudge

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 package chocolate chips
- 1 package butterscotch chips
- 1 can Chinese noodles

## Procedure
1. Melt chips slowly in microwave using 30 second intervals.  When melted dump in noodles.  Stir until coated, drop by spoonful onto waxed paper.  Place in fridge to harden.
2. Note: Can add M&M almonds so they look like little bird’s nests.
