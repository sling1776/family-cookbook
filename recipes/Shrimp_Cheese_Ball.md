# Shrimp Cheese Ball

## Author

Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients

2 large pkgs. cream cheese
1 jar Kraft Old English cheese
½ cup grated cheddar cheese
1 tsp. lemon juice
¼ - ½ tsp. onion juice
¼ - ½ tsp. dried chives
1 tsp. dry parsley
1 Tbsp. mayonnaise
1 can (4.25oz) shrimp

## Procedure

Mix together well and form into ball, roll in chopped nuts. Freezes well.
