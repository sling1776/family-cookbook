# Jerky

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

1- 16 oz soy sauce
3 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce
1 ½ Tbsp. liquid smoke
1 tsp. scant garlic salt
1 tsp. onion salt
Tabasco - 10 shakes
pepper to taste

## Procedure

Cut deer meat into ½ inch thick strips, marinate overnight (shake container occasionally) Pound out, re-pepper and dry 6-8 hours or more if necessary.
