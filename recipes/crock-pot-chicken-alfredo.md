# Crock Pot Chicken Alfredo

Author: Sammi Jo Stewart

## Ingredients
- 4 chicken breasts
- 1 stick of butter (I use 1/2)
- 1 packet Good Seasonings Italian dressing mix
- 8 oz. cream cheese
- 1 can cream of chicken soup

## Procedure
1. Layer in crock pot: butter, chicken breasts, and dressing mix.  Cook 3-4 hours on medium-low.  Take chicken out and shred.
2. Add to crock pot: cream cheese, can of cream of chicken soup and 1/2 of a soup can of water.
3. Melt together and add chicken. Serve over your favorite noodles, rice or mashed potatoes.
