# Peanut Butter Cookies

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 3 cups cookie mix
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup brown sugar, firmly packed
- ½ cup chunky style peanut butter
- 1 tsp. vanilla

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 375°
2. Lightly greased baking sheets.  Combine all ingredients in a medium bowl.  Blend well.  Shape into 1 inch balls.  Place on prepared baking sheets and flatten with fork tines.  Bake 10 to 12 minutes, until edges are browned.  Makes 30 to 36 cookies.
