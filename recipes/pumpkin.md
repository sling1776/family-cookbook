# Pumpkin

Author: Pecan Friendship Bread

## Ingredients
- 3 cups chopped pecans, divided
- 1 can (16oz) solid-pack pumpkin
- 1 cup Starter (recipe follows)
- 4 eggs
- ½ cup vegetable oil
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup packed light brown sugar
- 4 tsp. ground cinnamon
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. ground nutmeg
- 1 tsp. ground ginger
- 1 tsp. ground cloves

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and flour 2
2. (9 ½ x4-inch) loaf pans. Set aside.
3. Toast 2 cups of pecans. Combine pumpkin, Starter, eggs, oil and vanilla in large bowl. Combine remaining ingredients in separate large bowl until well blended. Stir into pumpkin mixture just until blended. Stir in toasted pecans. Spoon batter evenly into prepared pans. Sprinkle reserved pecans evenly over batter. Bake 1 hour or until toothpick inserted into centers comes out clean. Yield: Makes 2 loaves
