# Marjean’s Yams

Author: Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients
- 2 ½ cups cooked sweet potatoes
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- ½ cup milk
- ½ cup real butter
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 cup brown sugar
- ½ cup flour
- 1/3 cup soft butter (real)
- 1 cup chopped pecans

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients until smooth.  Pour into greased 9x13” pan. Sprinkle with topping of
2. Bake at 350° for 30 minutes
