# Better Than Sex Cake

Author: Janine Hinkle

## Ingredients
- 1 box yellow cake mix with pudding
- 1 lrg, can crushed pineapple, undrained
- ½ cup chopped nuts
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 small pkg. vanilla instant pudding
- ½ cup coconut

## Procedure
1. Mix and bake cake as directed in 9x13 pan.  While baking, boil pineapple and sugar for 5 minutes.  Make pudding.  Spread pineapple mixture over baked cake while still hot.  Let cool.  Spread pudding on top.  Cool.  Spread Cool Whip next and top with nuts and coconut.  Refrigerate
