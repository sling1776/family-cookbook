# Crêpes

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

1½ cups flour
1 Tbsp. sugar
½ tsp. baking powder
½ tsp. salt
2 cups milk
2 eggs
½ tsp. vanilla
2 Tbsp. melted margarine

## Procedure

Measure flour, sugar, baking powder and salt into bowl. Stir in remaining ingredients. Beat until smooth. For each crêpe, lightly butter skillet; heat over medium heat until butter is bubbly. Pour 1/4 cup batter into skillet. Rotate pan until batter covers bottom. Cook until light brown; turn and brown on other side. Spread on thin layer of jam and roll up. Sprinkle with powdered sugar.

## Notes

Makes 12 crêpes
