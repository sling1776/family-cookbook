# Potato Candy

Author: Laurie Fryatt

## Ingredients
- 1 small egg size potato boiled until soft.  Remove skin and mash.

## Procedure
1. Add: about ½ package of powdered sugar or until it is play dough stiff.  Roll or pat out into square.  Spread peanut butter on it, then roll like jelly roll.  Slice into 1/4" slices.  As slices sit they dry and stiffen.  Enjoy!
