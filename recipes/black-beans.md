# Black Beans

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp. ground cumin
- 1 can black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 1/3 cup tomato juice
- 1 1/2 tsp. salt
- 2 Tbsp. fresh chopped cilantro

## Procedure
1. In a nonstick skillet, cook garlic and cumin in olive oil over medium heat until you can smell it. Add beans, tomato juice, and salt. Continually stir until heated through. Just before serving stir in the cilantro.
2. Note: You can add a can of corn, drained, to the beans with a dash of cumin and chili powder, it is AWESOME! I recommend doing this even though it's not "traditional Cafe Rio."
