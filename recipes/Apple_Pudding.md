# Apple Pudding

## Author

Alecia Bales

## Ingredients

2 cups sugar
½ cup butter
2 eggs
½ tsp. salt
2 cups flour
2 tsp. soda
2 tsp. cinnamon
1 tsp. nutmeg
4 cups grated apples
½ cup chopped nuts
### Best Ever Sauce
1/4 cup butter
½ cup sugar
1/4 cup of canned milk
1 tsp vanilla
dash of nutmeg
### Variation: Carmel Sauce by Bonnie Chantry
1 cup brown sugar 
½ cup Karo syrup
2 Tbsp. butter, 
dash of salt, 
½ tsp. vanilla
1 cup canned milk

## Procedure

Beat together sugar butter and eggs.
Mix together salt, flour, soda, cinnamon, and nutmeg and add to the above.
Then add apples and nuts.
Bake in dripper pan at 350° for 45 minutes.
### Best ever sauce
Cook until thickened. Do not boil.
### Carmel Sauce
Boil until thread spins (lift a small amount of sauce, should look like a hair spinning down)
Add butter, salt, vanilla and milk.

Stir & pour over each serving of pudding.
