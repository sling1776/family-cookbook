# Whole Wheat Chocolate Chip Cookies

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

1/2 cup butter
1/2 cup shortening
1 cup white sugar
1 cup brown sugar
2 eggs
1½ tsp. vanilla
2 cups whole wheat flour
1½ to 2 cups oatmeal, pulverized in blender
1 tsp. salt
1 tsp. baking soda
1 tsp. baking powder
24 oz. chocolate chips

## Procedure

Cream together butter, shortening, white sugar, and brown sugar. 
Add eggs and vanilla. Mix in flour, oatmeal, salt, baking soda, baking powder, and chocolate chips.
Bake at 375° for 10-12 minutes. (Make sure you take them out of the oven before they look done or they will be too crispy.)

## Notes

I am a whole wheat flour junkie. I got a wheat grinder to be able to use all that food storage wheat we kept buying and now I LOVE recipes that call for whole wheat.
