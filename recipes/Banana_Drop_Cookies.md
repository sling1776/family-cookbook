# Banana Drop Cookies

## Author

Suzanne Reber

## Ingredients

2 ½ cups flour
2 tsp. baking powder
½ tsp. salt
2/3 cup shortening
1 cup sugar
2 eggs	
1 tsp. vanilla 
1 cup mashed bananas
chocolate chips

## Procedure

Mix all ingredients. Bake at 400° for 10-12 minutes on a greased cookie sheet.
