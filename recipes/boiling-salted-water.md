# Boiling salted water

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup chopped onion
- 1 tsp. lemon juice
- salt and pepper to taste

## Procedure
1. Cook beans in the boiling water until tender crisp.  Melt butter in sauté pan.  Add onions and beans.  Sauté until onion is clear. Remove from heat.  Add cashews, parsley, lemon juice and salt and pepper.  Serve warm.
