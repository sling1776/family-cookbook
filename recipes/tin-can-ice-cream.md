# Tin Can Ice Cream

Author: Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup whipping cream
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ tsp. vanilla

## Procedure
1. Put  mixture into a quart bottle and then put bottle into a large Tang or Cannery #10 can.  Pack around quart bottle with ice and salt.  Put cover on large can and roll about 20 minutes.
