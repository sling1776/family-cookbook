# Homemade Noodles

## Author

Grandma Grace Fielding – This recipe was the #1 one requested recipe by family members as one they used and wanted to pass on in this new FFF cookbook.

## Ingredients

1 egg slightly beaten
1/4 tsp. salt
2/3 cup flour (OR enough to make stiff dough).

## Procedure

Mix all ingredients together. Knead dough and then let it stand covered for ½ hour. Roll it out very thin. Cut into strips with noodle cutter, or pizza cutter.
