# Cowboy Cookies

Author: Carolee Fielding

## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. baking powder
- 1 cup white sugar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup shortening
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup oats
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 6oz. butterscotch chips
- 1 ½ cups coconut

## Procedure
1. Bake at 350° for 10-12 minutes.  Don't overbake.
