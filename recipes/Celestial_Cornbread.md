# Celestial Cornbread

## Author

Trisha Olsen

## Ingredients

½ cup butter, melted
½ -2/3 cup sugar
2 eggs
1 cup buttermilk
½ tsp. baking soda
1/4 tsp. salt
1 cup cornmeal
1 cup flour

## Procedure

Beat butter, sugar and eggs well. Add buttermilk, Mix in dry ingredients. Pour into an 8” square greased pan. Bake at 375° for 30 minutes.

## Notes

While your cornbread is in the oven, sit, relax and think about the wonderful gifts you have been given and how much you are blessed to know the recipe to Celestial Cornbread.
