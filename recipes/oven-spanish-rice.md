# Oven Spanish Rice

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rice
- 2 cups water
- 2-3 cups tomatoes
- ½ cup chopped onion
- 1/4 cup chopped green pepper
- salt and pepper to taste

## Procedure
1. Sauté green pepper and onion, stir in 2 cups tomatoes, rice and water.  Bake at 350° for 20-30 min. or on the stove top.  Can add green chili peppers.
