# Garlic Bread

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Cut French bread lengthwise, spread with butter, sprinkle with garlic.  Spread with mayonnaise, top with grated cheddar and Monterey Jack cheese.  Sprinkle with Parmesan and paprika.  Cover with foil and bake.  Uncover and broil to toast.
