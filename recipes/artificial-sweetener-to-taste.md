# artificial sweetener, to taste

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1/3 tray of ice cubes
- Eggnog - Sue Reber - Cooking gives this a real mellow flavor.
- 1/3 cup sugar
- 2 egg yolks
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 4 cups milk
- 2 egg whites
- 3 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- ½ cup whipping cream, whipped

## Procedure
1. Mix in blender.
2. ground nutmeg
3. Beat 1/3 cup sugar into egg yolks.  Add salt, stir in milk.  Cook over medium heat stirring constantly, till mixture coats spoon.  Cool.  Beat egg whites till foamy.  Gradually add 3 Tbsp. sugar, beating to soft peaks.  Add to mixture then mix thoroughly.  Add vanilla and flavoring.  Chill 3-4 hours.  Pour into punch bowl or cups.  Dot with "island" of whipped cream, dash with nutmeg.  Serves 6-8.
