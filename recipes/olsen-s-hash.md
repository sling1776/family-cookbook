# Olsen’s Hash

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Brown and drain hamburger enough for your family.  Add chopped onions.  Add peeled, sliced potatoes.  Layer with onions on top of hamburger.  Sprinkle with salt and pepper.    Can add cream of mushroom or chicken soup and ½ can of water on top of the hamburger. Bake in covered pan 45-60 minutes at 350°.
