# Put in large bowl and mix with CLEAN hands

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 -18 oz. jar Chunky Peanut Butter
- 1 cup margarine (2 cubes)
- 1 lb. powdered sugar

## Procedure
1. Add: 3 ½ c. Rice Krispies
2. Mix well.  (May be done with spoon but it's more fun with the hands.) Take a spoonful at a time and roll in palm of hands until it forms a ball, about 1 inch in diameter.  Place on cookie sheet and freeze. Roll frozen balls in melted dipping chocolate.  The chocolate will set up right away and they are ready for eating, or you may put them back in the freezer for later.
