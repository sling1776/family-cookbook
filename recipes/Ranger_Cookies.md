# Ranger Cookies

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

1 cup shortening
2 cups flour
½ tsp. baking powder
1 cup Rice Krispies
¼ cup plus 2 Tbsp. milk
1 cup sugar
2 eggs
½ tsp. salt
1 cup coconut
2 cups quick oats
1 cup brown sugar
1 tsp. vanilla
1 tsp. baking soda
1 6oz. pkg. chocolate chips

## Procedure

Combine all ingredients. Bake 375° for 10- 12 minutes.
