# Chocolate Cake:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2 cups GF Basic Cake mix
- 1/4 cup cocoa powder
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 egg, slightly beaten
- 3/4 cup ice cold water

## Procedure
1. Ice Cream Sandwiches:
2. Bake the chocolate cake in an 11 x 15 inches pan. Cool completely. Use ice cream in a rectangular box and cut into the size slices you like. . Then cut the cake to fit the ice cream. Keep frozen until ready to eat. They are yummy!!
