# Cheese Cake

## Author

Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients

### Crust
1 2/3 cups graham cracker crumbs
½ cup melted margarine
### Filling
½ cup boiling water
1 small pkg. lemon Jell-O
2 Tbsp, lemon juice
2 cup cottage cheese (divided)
12 oz. Cool Whip
### Topping
1 cup sugar
1 pkg. unsweetened Kool Aid (raspberry)
4 Tbsp. cornstarch
2 cups water
Fruit (raspberries)

## Procedure

Combine crust ingredients and pat into bottom of 9x13” pan
Filling:
Blend water and lemon Jell-O in blender on low until blended then add lemon juice and 1 cup cottage cheese.
Blend on high then add 1 more cup cottage cheese. Blend. Blend. Pour into a bowl and add cool whip. Stir together. Pour on top of crust. Chill until set.
Topping:
Bring water to boil in pan. Add dry ingredients and cook about 3 minutes. Add fruit. Cool and then spread on top of cheese cake. Refrigerate.
