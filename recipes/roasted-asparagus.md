# Roasted Asparagus

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Heat oven to 400°.  Trim tough ends of asparagus. Place asparagus in a large bowl and toss with about 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, and salt and pepper. Spread the asparagus out on a cookie sheet and roast 10 to 12 minutes until the asparagus is tender and crispy at the tips.
