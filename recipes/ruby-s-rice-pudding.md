# Ruby's Rice Pudding

Author: Ruby Fielding

## Ingredients
- 6 cups cooked rice (approx. 3 cups uncooked)
- 6 Tbsp. cornstarch
- 12 large eggs
- 15 cups whole milk- Do not substitute
- 2 Tbsp. pure vanilla, no alcohol in ingredients
- 2 cups sugar

## Procedure
1. Place rice in a large roasting pan and combine all other ingredients in a blender.  Then stir contents together in the roasting pan. Bake at 350° for 1½ hours, stirring every 30 minutes.  After 1 hour of cooking the pudding is ready, the addition half hour is for thickness so cook to your desire of thickness after that.
