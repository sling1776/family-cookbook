# Sausage Cheese Balls

## Author

Angela Olsen

## Ingredients

3 cups Bisquick
1 lb. Jimmy dean hot sausage, uncooked
4 cups shredded cheese
1/2 cups milk

## Procedure

Mix together with hands- roll into balls and bake for 20-25 minutes at 350°.

## Notes

We make these the night before Christmas. I also bake them then & put them in the freezer so the kids can cook them in the toaster oven, for a quick filling breakfast before school.
