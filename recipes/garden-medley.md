# Garden Medley

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. Brown one pound hamburger, drain if necessary.  Top with one zucchini and one yellow squash.  Place thinly sliced onion on top of squash.  Cover and let simmer.  When squash can be pierced with fork, add 4 tomatoes that have been peeled and diced.  Place on top of onions.  Cover with grated cheddar cheese.  Cover and simmer until cheese is melted.  NEVER STIR.  Enjoy!
