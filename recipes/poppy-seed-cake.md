# Poppy Seed Cake

Author: Diane Anderson

## Ingredients
- 1 box of yellow cake mix (with pudding)
- ½ cup milk
- 1/3 cup oil
- ½ cup butter or margarine
- 4 eggs
- 1 cup sour cream
- 2 Tbsp. poppy seeds
- 1 Tbsp. almond extract
- 2 Tbsp. flour

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients and blend for 5 minutes.  Pour into a greased and floured Bundt pan. Bake at 350° for 40 minutes. Let it stand for 15 minutes before removing from the pan. (High Altitude: Bake at 375° for 35 minutes)
2. Variation: Use this recipe with other cake mixes omitting poppy seeds & almond extract.
