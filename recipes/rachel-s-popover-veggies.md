# Rachel’s Popover Veggies

Author: Rachel Mitchell

## Ingredients
- 1 cup milk
- 1 Tbsp. melted butter
- 1 cup flour
- ½ tsp. salt
- 2 eggs

## Procedure
1. Vegetables such as:  onions, broccoli, cauliflower, parsnips, zucchini, sweet potatoes, peppers, and eggplant
2. Beat until smooth milk, melted butter, flour and salt.  Then add eggs one at a time, do not overbeat.  Cut vegetables into bite size pieces.  Dip in the batter.  Fry in deep fat until puffy and golden brown.  Remove with tongs and drain on paper towel.
