# Lentil Soup

## Author

Grace Ann Skousen

## Ingredients

2 qts. soup stock (chicken or ham hock broth)
1 1/2 cups lentils (orange, not green)
3 - 4 carrots, grated
small turnip, grated (optional)
1 onion, grated

## Procedure

Place it all in a big pot and boil for 1 hour. Very healthy!

## Notes

Recipe from Scotland
