# Mexican S’mores

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- flour tortilla (small)

## Procedure
1. peanut butter
2. chocolate chips
3. miniature marshmallows
4. Spread peanut butter on tortilla, sprinkle with chocolate chips and marshmallows, then roll up the tortilla shell. Wrap in foil and twist foil ends. Cook on grill grate over your fire for about 5 minutes. Unwrap and indulge.
