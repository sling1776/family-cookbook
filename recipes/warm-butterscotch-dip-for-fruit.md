# Warm Butterscotch Dip for Fruit

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1- 4 oz. cook-before-serving butterscotch pudding mix –NOT INSTANT
- 1- 14 oz. can of sweetened condensed milk
- 1 ½ cups of milk

## Procedure
1. In a medium sauce pan combine pudding mix and both milks.  Cook and stir until thickened and bubbly: cook 2 minutes more.  Serve warm with apple slices, banana slices, pear slices or your favorite fruit
