# Graham Cracker Pie Crust

Author: Alice Chantry

## Ingredients
- 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
- 2 Tbsp. melted butter (or margarine)
- 1 Tbsp. sugar

## Procedure
1. Make graham cracker crumbs in food processor.   Thoroughly mix in the melted butter and sugar. Dump into a 9 inch pie pan. Press evenly around the edges.  Bake at 350 degrees for 10 minutes.  Let cool before adding filling.
