# Fry Bread

Author: Emma Largo

## Ingredients
- 2 cups flour
- 1 Tbsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. Warm water
2. Mix together with enough water to make a soft dough.  The success is in the kneading according to Emma's mother. Pinch off a 2 inch ball, flatten to 1/4 inch and fry in hot grease.  Can be served as a scone or the basis for Navajo taco.
