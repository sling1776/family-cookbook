# No Bake Cookies

Author: Ruby Fielding

## Ingredients
- 2 cups sugar
- ½ cup butter
- ½ cup milk
- 1/4 cup cocoa

## Procedure
1. Bring to boil, boil 1 minute.  Remove from heat, add 1 tsp vanilla add 3 cups quick oats.   Spoon onto waxed paper.  Chill 20 minutes.
2. Variations: Add 2/3 cup peanut butter, coconut or peanuts if desired.
