# Ice Cream Sandwich Dessert

Author: Sarah Manis

## Ingredients
- 12-15 ice cream sandwiches
- 1 lg. cool whip
- 1 Hershey bar

## Procedure
1. Unwrap ice cream bars and lay in bottom of 9x13” pan. Spread a layer of cool whip on next.
2. Put in freezer for a few minutes to harden cool whip.  Put on another layer of ice cream sandwiches & cool whip.  Grate chocolate bar and sprinkle on top. Freeze until very firm.
