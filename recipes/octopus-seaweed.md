# Octopus & Seaweed

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. beef flavored ramen noodles
- 4 hot dogs
- 5 drops liquid green food coloring

## Procedure
1. Cook noodles according to package directions.  Add food coloring.  Boil or microwave hotdogs to heat through.  Cut the bottom 2/3 of the hot in half, turn and cut in half again so you have 4 long sections. Slice each of the sections length-wise in half again. You will end up with 8 long legs dangling from the top 1/3 of the hot dog.  Place noodles in a bowl and them place hot dog on top with legs spread out.  Use ketchup or mustard to make eyes and a mouth on octopus.
