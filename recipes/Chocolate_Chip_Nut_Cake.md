# Chocolate Chip & Nut Cake

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

### Cake:
2 cups GF Basic Cake mix 
1 tsp. vanilla
1 egg, slightly beaten
½ cup milk
Dash of nutmeg, optional
### Topping
1/3 cup chocolate chips
1/3 cup chopped nuts
Bake at 350° for 25-30 minutes.

## Procedure

Stir ingredients until well blended. Pour into an 8 inch square pan.
Sprinkle toppings on top. Bake at 350° for 25-30 minutes.

## Notes

Hershey Heath bits are great, also!!
Or cinnamon or butterscotch chips.
