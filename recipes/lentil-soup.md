# Lentil Soup

Author: Grace Ann Skousen

## Ingredients
- 2 qts. soup stock (chicken or ham hock broth)
- 1 1/2 cups lentils (orange, not green)
- 3 - 4 carrots, grated
- 1 onion, grated

## Procedure
1. small turnip, grated (optional)
2. Place it all in a big pot and boil for 1 hour.  Very healthy!
