# Becky's Favorite Rice Pudding

## Author

Becky Concoby

## Ingredients

### 
2/3 cup sugar 
2 Tbsp. cornstarch
dash of salt
2 1/2 cups milk
2 Tbsp. butter
1 tsp. vanilla
2 cups cooked rice

## Procedure

Mix together in a glass bowl sugar, cornstarch, and salt
Add milk
Microwave on high for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring after 3 minutes. Beat 2 egg yolks and mix in. Microwave for 2 or 3 minutes in high, stirring after every minute. Cook longer as needed to thicken.
Add butter, vanilla and rice.

## Notes

P.S. this is gluten free
