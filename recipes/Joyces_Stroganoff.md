# Joyce’s Stroganoff

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

1 ½ lbs stew meat
1 pkg. onion soup mix
1 can cream of celery soup
1 can cream of chicken soup
1 pint sour cream

## Procedure

Brown stew meat
Add onion soup mix, celery soup, cream of chicken soup, and sour cream.
Cook 4-5 hours in 250° oven.
