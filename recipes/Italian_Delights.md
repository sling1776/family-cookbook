# Italian Delights

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

½ lb spaghetti
3 slices bacon
1 med. onion, chopped
1 clove garlic, minced
1 green pepper, chopped
1 can tomato soup
1 small can corn
½ cup grated cheese
3/4 lb. cooked & drained hamburger
1 can mushrooms
½ tsp. each salt, pepper, paprika, and cayenne.
Parmesan cheese

## Procedure

Cook spaghetti and drain
Sauté bacon, onion, garlic, and green pepper ingredients and then add remaining ingredients.
Mix all together and pour into buttered baking dish and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese. Bake for 30 minutes at 375°.
