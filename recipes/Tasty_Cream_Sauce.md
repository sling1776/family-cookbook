# Tasty Cream Sauce

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

4 Tbsp. of soup base
1 Tbsp. butter
1 tsp. chopped chives
OR 2 thinly sliced green onions
1 1/4 cup hot water.

## Procedure

Melt 1 Tbsp. butter in a small pan and add 1 tsp. chopped chives or 2 thinly sliced green onions before putting in the soup base. Add 
1¼ cups hot water and cook in a small sauce pan, over medium heat, stirring until sauce thickens.
