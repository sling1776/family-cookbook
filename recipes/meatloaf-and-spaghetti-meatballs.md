# Meatloaf and Spaghetti Meatballs

Author: Chrissy Probst

## Ingredients
- 4 pounds of raw hamburger
- 3 eggs
- 2 cups of Italian Bread Crumbs
- Minced onion to taste (or fresh onion diced)
- 1 cup of ketchup

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients together in mixer.  Form two loaves for meatloaf.  Put formed meatloaf on greased pans and freeze until firm.  Place loaves in Gallon size bags.  When ready to eat, preheat oven to 375 degrees and bake covered (with foil) for one hour.  Add ½ cup of shredded cheese to rest of mixture.  Roll into meatballs.  Place on cookie sheet and freeze until firm.  Place partially frozen meatballs in Freezer bags and label.  When ready to serve, place frozen meatballs in Spaghetti sauce, cover and simmer for 15-20 minutes or until done.  Enjoy!
