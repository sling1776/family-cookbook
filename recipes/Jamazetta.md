# Jamazetta

## Author

Amy Badger

## Ingredients

1 lb. ground beef
1 -3oz. pkg. cream cheese, softened
1 can tomato soup
1 small onion, chopped
½ pkg. egg noodles (or whatever available)

## Procedure

Sauté hamburger and onion together. Drain. Cut cream cheese into cubes. Add soup and cream cheese to meat and heat through. Cook and drain noodles. Mix all ingredients together and bake at 350º for 30 minutes.

## Notes

A wonderful recipe my friend Kelly Keesecker introduced me to in Alaska. Our boys were born exactly one month apart so they had fun going through those first year milestones soon after each other.
Note: For faster results, microwave until heated through.
