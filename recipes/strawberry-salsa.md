# Strawberry Salsa

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 Granny Smith apple
- 1 kiwi fruit
- ½ medium orange
- ½ of a 10 oz. package frozen, sweetened slice strawberries

## Procedure
1. Mango if desired
2. Preheat oven to 475°. Combine sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl and set aside.
3. Brush tortillas with water, using a pastry brush.  Sprinkle with cinnamon and sugar mixture.
4. Cut each tortilla into wedges.  If not using parchment paper, spray pan.  Place wedges on baking sheet without overlapping and bake 5-7 minutes or until golden brown.  Cool.  Peel and chop apple, kiwi, and orange.  Chop partially thawed strawberries.  Put all fruit in a cereal bowl. Fruit should be finely chopped such as in a salsa, not a fruit salad.  Mangos may also be added if desired. Serve with cinnamon chips as a low-fat snack.
