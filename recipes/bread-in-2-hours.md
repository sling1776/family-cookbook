# Bread in 2 Hours

Author: Diane Anderson

## Ingredients
- 4 cups warm water
- 4 Tbsp. yeast in ½ cup warm water
- ½ cup honey
- 1 Tbsp. salt
- 2 Tbsp. oil
- 12 cups flour

## Procedure
1. Mix together let rise half hour.  Form into loaves and put in pans.  Let rise half hour.  Bake at 400° for half hour.  4 loaves
