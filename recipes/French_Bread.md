# French Bread

## Author

Becky Concoby

## Ingredients

2 Tbsp. yeast dissolved in ½ cup tap water
3 Tbsp. sugar
1/4 cup oil
2 cups hot water
1 Tbsp. salt
6 cups flour
1 egg
sesame seed

## Procedure

Mix yeast and ½ cup water and let rest. Add sugar, oil, hot water, salt and 3 cups flour. Mix well, and then add 3 more cups water. Let rest for 4 ten-minute periods. Punch down after each 10-minute period. Roll out like a cinnamon roll. Place on greased sheet. Let rise 15-20 min. Slash with knife. Brush with beaten egg and add sesame seeds. Bake at 375° for 30 min.

## Notes

Makes two loaves.
