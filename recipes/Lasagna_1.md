# Lasagna

## Author

Janine Hinkle

## Ingredients

Lasagna pasta
Meat sauce (vermicelli)
Grated mozzarella cheese
Ricotta cheese (not cottage cheese)

## Procedure

Use Vermicelli sauce (in another recipe), except do not use caraway or celery seed. Make a thicker sauce. Use less sugar. Use more marjoram. 
Layer in a greased pan (use olive oil):
- Lasagna pasta
- Meat sauce
- Grated mozzarella cheese
- Lasagna pasta
- Meat sauce
- Ricotta cheese (not cottage cheese)
- Lasagna pasta
- Meat sauce
Top with a thick layer of mozzarella enough to completely cover sauce. Bake 375° for 30 minutes or until cheese starts to brown and is completely melted together.
