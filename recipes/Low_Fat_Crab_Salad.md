# Low Fat Crab Salad

## Author

Sarah Manis

## Ingredients

1 lb. imitation crab
1 medium cucumber
4 medium apples
½ tsp. garlic powder
1 tsp. white pepper
1 cup light Miracle Whip

## Procedure

Dice crab, cucumbers, apples. Mix last three ingredients and all to crab mix. Chill for 1 hour.
