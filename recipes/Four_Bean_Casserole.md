# Four Bean Casserole

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

1 lb. ground beef 
1 med onion, chopped
1 lg. can pork and beans
2 can Ellis white beans
1 can kidney beans
1 sm. can lima beans
1/4 cup brown sugar
1 tsp. salt
Pepper to taste
½ tsp. dry mustard
½ tsp. prepared mustard
½ cup catsup or chili sauce
½ Tbsp. apple cider vinegar

## Procedure

Sauté ground beef and onions. Add remainder of ingredients.
Put in large casserole or Dutch oven and bake at 375° for 45 minutes, covered.
