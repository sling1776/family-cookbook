# Sauté until rice is toasty:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2 tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. pepper
- 1 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 tsp. each: oregano, and basil

## Procedure
1. Add:  1 (28 oz) can tomatoes
2. Cook till rice is done, about 30-45 min. Remove from heat.  If desired puree soup in blender.  Add 2 cups hot milk and 4 Tbsp. butter.  Warm, but do not boil.
