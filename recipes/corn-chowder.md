# Corn Chowder

Author: Janine Hinkle

## Ingredients
- 5-6 slices of bacon, cut up
- 1 small onion diced
- 2 medium potatoes, diced (we use 9 medium)
- 1 can whole kernel corn
- 1 can of milk (or whatever milk is in the frig)
- salt and pepper to taste

## Procedure
1. We use a pinch of basil and a sprinkle of dried parsley - sometimes a touch of garlic.  Fry onions with bacon, brown lightly.  Add potatoes and enough water to cover (use water off corn also). Cook until potatoes are tender.  Add corn, milk, spices and herbs.  Simmer about 15 minutes.
