# Northern Cornbread

## Author

Becky Concoby

## Ingredients

3/4 cup sugar
1/4 cup shortening
2 eggs
1 cup cornmeal
1½ cup flour
1 tsp. salt
2½ tsp. baking powder
1¼ cup milk

## Procedure

Cream sugar and shortening. Add eggs and cornmeal. Sift dry ingredients and add alternately with milk. Bake at 375 for 20 to 30 minutes for bread or 10 minutes for muffins.
