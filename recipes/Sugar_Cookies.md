# Sugar Cookies

## Author

Julie Chantry

## Ingredients

1 cup shortening or butter
1 cup sugar
2 eggs
½ tsp. vanilla
3 cups flour
½ tsp. soda
½ tsp. salt
1 tsp. baking powder
½ cup sour cream

## Procedure

Cream butter/shortening and sugar then add 2 beaten eggs and rest of ingredients.
Chill, then roll 1/8 inch thick and cut into shapes. Bake 375° for 8 minutes.

## Notes

When Julie checked the family cookbook for a sugar cookie recipe, she found Barbara's recipe that called for cream of tartar. Went to the store and upon returning she made her cookies to find out that it called for sour cream. (Jolene's recipe) Later she realized she'd started with one recipe and ended with another. She was glad for the mistake. This one with sour cream turned out to be the most moist sugar cookie she has ever made. They are now her favorite and she makes them for her Primary class as well as her Sunshine Generation Students.
