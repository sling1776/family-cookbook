# Wedding Luncheon Chicken

## Author

Grandma Reber

## Ingredients

chicken breast
milk
flour
bread crumbs
oil
### WHITE SAUCE: 
Equal parts:
Chicken broth 
Light cream

## Procedure

Pound chicken breast into ½ inch thick. Dip in milk, then flour, then milk again. Then coat with seasoned bread crumbs. Heat ¼” oil in frypan. Fry until gold brown. Cover with white sauce and serve.
Make a thin white sauce with half chicken broth and half light cream. Season with salt and pour over chicken.
