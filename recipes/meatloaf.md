# Meatloaf

Author: Annalisa Fielding

## Ingredients
- 2 eggs
- 2 Tbsp. chopped onion
- 1 cup tomato juice
- ½ tsp. salt
- 2/3 cup rolled oats
- 1/4 tsp. garlic salt
- ½ tsp. sage
- 1 lb. hamburger

## Procedure
1. Combine in a bowl:
2. few shakes of savory salt
3. Put in loaf pan sprayed with Pam.  Top with Bull’s-eye BBQ sauce.  Cook at 350° for 45 minutes to an hour.
