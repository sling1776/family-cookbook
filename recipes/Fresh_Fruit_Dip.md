# Fresh Fruit Dip

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

1 pint marshmallow cream
1- 8 oz. cream cheese

## Procedure

Cream together. Use as dip for your choice of fruits - apples, banana, watermelon balls, or any other fruit.

## Notes

Variation: Add a few teaspoons of maraschino cherry juice for a lovely pink cherry dip.
