# Meat Loaf

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

1 lb. hamburger
1 cup soft bread crumbs
1 small onion chopped
1 tsp. salt
1 egg
½ cup milk

## Procedure

Mix well with hands. Place in loaf pans. Bake at 350° for 40 to 60 minutes. Let meat loaf stand 5-10 minutes before serving. This will allow it to cut easier.
