# fruit flavor (use yogurt container as a measuring cup for the remaining ingredients)

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 3 cups of flour
- 3 cups of sugar
- 1 cup of vegetable oil
- 3 eggs

## Procedure
1. Mix all the ingredients together well and pour into a cake pan that has been greased and floured.  Bake at 375°.  After 20 minutes check the cake.  Done when top is golden brown.
