# Sloppy Joes

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1 lg. onion, chopped
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 Tbsp. worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp. salt
- 3/4 tsp. curry powder
- 2/3 cup ketchup
- 2/3 cup tomato juice

## Procedure
1. serves 6-8
2. Brown meat and onion.  Add other ingredients and simmer 1½ to 2 hrs.
