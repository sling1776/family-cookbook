# Whole Wheat Pancakes

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

These pancakes have a nutty texture

## Procedure

Process in blender:
1 cup wheat (the whole kernel wheat)
1 cup milk - until well chopped 
Then add: 1/3 cup oil
2 tsp. baking powder 
2 Tbsp. sugar
1 tsp. salt
2 eggs
Fry on heated grill.

## Notes

These pancakes have a nutty texture
