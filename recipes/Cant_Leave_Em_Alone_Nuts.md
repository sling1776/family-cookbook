# Can’t Leave ‘Em Alone Nuts

## Author

Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients

½ cup water 
1 cup sugar
¼ cup brown sugar
5 Tbsp. cinnamon
2 1/3 cups almonds or other nuts

## Procedure

In a heavy pan bring water to a boil. Add in sugars and return to a boil. Add cinnamon and nuts and stir constantly until all liquid has cooked out. Pour nuts on a greased cookie sheet in a single layer. Bake at 325° for 15 minutes. Cool. Best when stored uncovered.
