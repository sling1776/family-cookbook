# Alfredo Sauce

## Author

Becky Concoby

## Ingredients

1/4 cup butter
1/4 cup flour
2 to 3 cups half and half or whole milk
3/4 cup parmesan cheese
2 Tbsp, parsley
½ tsp. salt

## Procedure

Melt butter then add the rest. Pour over pasta.

## Notes

Note: This recipe doubled is about right for a 22 oz. package of noodles.
