# Jell

Author: O Cake

## Ingredients
- 1 lemon cake mix
- 1 pkg. lime Jell-O
- 3/4 cup boiling water
- 1 pkg. vanilla instant pudding
- 1 pkg. dream whip
- 1½ cups milk

## Procedure
1. Bake cake as directed on package.  When cake has cooled for about 20 minutes, poke holes with fork at ½ inch intervals. Dissolve gelatin in boiling water.  Spoon over cake. Chill 3-4 hours.  Top with Cool Whip, or frost with:
2. Beat well together till light and fluffy.   Frost the cake.  Keep refrigerated.
3. VARIATION: Raspberry Poke Cake
