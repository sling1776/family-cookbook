# Then add:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup oil
- ½ cup sugar
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 10-12 cups flour

## Procedure
1. Mix to a good SOFT dough consistency.  Let rise, knead down and let rise again and punch down again.  Mold into a ball shape.  Put in greased dripper pan and let rise again.  Bake 350° for 20 minutes.
