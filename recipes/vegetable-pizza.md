# Vegetable Pizza

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. crescent rolls
- 6 oz. cream cheese
- 1/3 cup mayonnaise
- 1½ tsp. ranch dressing mix

## Procedure
1. Break apart package of crescent rolls.  Fit together to form a rectangle.  Pinch seams together.  Prick dough with fork to allow steam to escape.  Bake 350° oven for 8-10 minutes until golden brown.  Cool on cooling rack.
2. Filling: blend together the cream cheese, mayo and dressing mix. Spread on cooled crust.  Cut into squares to be served as an appetizer.
3. Topping: Dice vegetables such as cucumbers, carrots, broccoli and red pepper into small pieces and sprinkle on filling.  Sprinkle with grated mild cheddar cheese if desired.
