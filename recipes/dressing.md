# Dressing:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 quart of mayo
- 1 heaping cup sour cream
- ½ cup apple juice (or water from boiled chicken)
- 2 tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. pepper
- 1 tsp. garlic
- 1 tsp. onion powder

## Procedure
1. Mix dressing together and coat chicken
2. Add: one whole celery, chopped, 2 gala apples, cut up and halved red seedless grapes
