# Portobello Pork Chops

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 4 bone-in pork rib or loin chops ¾” thick
- 1 1/2 cups mild chunky salsa
- 1 cup chopped Portobello mushrooms
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 2 Tbsp. maple syrup
- 1/4 cup snipped fresh parsley

## Procedure
1. Heat oven to 375°. Trim fat from chops.
2. Spoon salsa into a 3-quart rectangular baking dish. Arrange chops on top of salsa. Combine Portobello mushrooms, cheddar cheese and maple syrup in a medium bowl. Spoon over chops. Bake, uncovered, for 25 to 30 minutes. Sprinkle with parsley. Serve chops with salsa mixture.
