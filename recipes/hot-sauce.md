# Hot Sauce

Author: Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients
- 8 qts. chopped tomatoes
- 4 -5 chili pepper, cut up
- 5 cups sugar
- 3 cups green onion, chopped
- ½ cup salt
- 2 cups vinegar
- 3 tsp. cloves
- 3 tsp. cinnamon
- 2 tsp. nutmeg
- 2 tsp. ginger
- 3 small yellow or green peppers

## Procedure
1. Tie spices in a cloth bag.
2. Boil onions, tomatoes and vinegar and peppers for a while before adding sugar.  Put in oven.  Cook 2 ½ to 3 hours.  Put in jars and seal.
