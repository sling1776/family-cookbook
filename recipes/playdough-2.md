# Playdough

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1½ cups flour
- 1 cup salt
- 1 Tbsp. powdered alum
- 1 Tbsp. oil
- 1 cup boiling water

## Procedure
1. Add:
2. Food coloring OR Kool-aid
3. Stir the mixture until cool, then knead in food coloring.  Keep dough in an airtight container when not in use. Add food coloring for different colors OR Kool-aid for a fun color and scented dough.
