# White Sauce

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

3 Tbsp. butter (2 Tbsp. for thinner sauce) 
4 Tbsp. F. Lite mix (2 Tbsp for thinner sauce)
1/8 tsp. salt
dash pepper
1 cup milk

## Procedure

Melt butter over medium heat. Stir in feather light mix, salt, and pepper. Let this mixture brown slightly (stirring regularly). Add milk. Stir with a whisk (to eliminate lumps) until sauce is thickened and boiling. Remove from heat.

## Notes

Note: 1 cup grated cheddar cheese may be added to this sauce, after it thickens, if you like. (Good for broccoli and baked potatoes)
