# Oh Boy Supper

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground beef, browned
- ¼ cup diced green pepper, optional
- 1 1/2 cups rice cooked
- 1 cup celery diced
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 can cream of chicken soup
- 1 Tbsp. soy sauce

## Procedure
1. Add: 1 small onion, diced
2. Simmer ten minutes, pour over Chinese noodles. Variation: Add ½ cup of uncooked rice & 1½ cups water  in place of cooked rice. Bake covered at 350° for 30 minutes.
