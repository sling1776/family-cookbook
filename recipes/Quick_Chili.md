# Quick Chili

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

3/4 lb. hamburger
2 Tbsp. flour
1 lg. can chili bean
1 can stewed tomatoes
1 Tbsp. chili seasoning mix

## Procedure

Cook beef in large pan. Stir in flour, then add remaining ingredients. Bring to boil, cover and simmer 10 min. Stirring occasionally. Makes 4 servings.
