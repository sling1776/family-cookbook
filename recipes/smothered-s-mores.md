# Smothered S’mores

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. bag of semi-sweet chocolate morsals
2. bag of marshmallows
3. sprinkles of all kinds
4. Melt chocolate in aluminum pie plate or disposable bread tin. Place marshmallow on skewer and roll in melted chocolate. Then roll in your favorite topping. Topping ideas: crushed graham crackers, nuts, or colorful jimmies, shredded coconut, etc.
