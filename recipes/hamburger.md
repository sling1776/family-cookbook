# Hamburger

Author: Potato Casserole

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 lbs. hamburger
- 10 med. potatoes, sliced thinly
- 2 cups gravy
- 1 can green beans, drained
- Salt & Pepper
- Minced onion
- 9 x 13-inch cake pan

## Procedure
1. Brown hamburger. Slice potatoes. Make gravy (your favorite or packaged). Put browned hamburger in cake pan. Put ½ of sliced potatoes in pan. Add spices as desired.  Add drained green beans & remaining potatoes.  Season again. Cover with foil. Bake at 350° for 1 hour.
