# GF Bread Stuffing

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- ½ cup finely chopped celery
- ¼ cup chopped red onion
- 2 Tbsp. shredded carrots
- ¼ cup margarine or butter
- ½ tsp. poultry seasoning
- 1/8 tsp. pepper
- 3-4 dashes of salt
- 4-6 cups dried bread cubes*
- ½ to ¾ cup chicken broth OR water

## Procedure
1. Sauté celery, onion, and carrot in butter until soft (but not browned). Remove from heat and add poultry seasoning, salt, and pepper. Place bread cubes in a bowl. Add onion mixture. Pour on enough broth to moisten the bread. Bake in a covered casserole dish at 375° for 20 – 30 minutes. (Or use to stuff an 8-10 lb. Turkey).
