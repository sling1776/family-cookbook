# Chocolate Sheet Cake

Author: Lion House

## Ingredients
- ½ cup margarine softened
- ½ cup shortening
- 1/4 cup cocoa
- 1 cup water
- 2 cups flour
- 2 cups sugar
- ½ cup buttermilk (or soured milk)
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 2 eggs beaten
- dash salt

## Procedure
1. Mix fats, cocoa, water and bring to a boil.  Pour over mixture of flour and sugar.  Mix well.  Add buttermilk and rest of ingredients. Mix well.  Bake 20 min. at 400° in 10x15” pan.  Frost while hot.
