# Banana or Zucchini Bread

## Author

Derlene Olsen

## Ingredients

1 cup sugar
½ cup shortening
3 mashed bananas OR grated zucchini
2 cups flour
1 tsp. baking soda
1/4 tsp. salt
1 tsp. vanilla
1 Tbsp. milk
2 eggs
½ cup nuts

## Procedure

Mix sugar and shortening well. Add bananas, flour and other ingredients. Mix well and bake at 350° for 45 minutes.
