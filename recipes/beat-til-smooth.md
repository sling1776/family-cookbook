# Beat 'til smooth:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 cup butter
- 1 cup shortening
- 2 cups brown sugar
- 1 cup sugar
- 3 tsp. vanilla, set aside
- 2 tsp. baking soda
- 1 tsp. salt

## Procedure
1. Add:  4 eggs
2. Mix: 5 cups flour
3. Mix dry mixture into moist mixture, adding more flour if necessary. Mix in chocolate chips, bake 375° for 9 minutes.
