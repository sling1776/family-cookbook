# Refrigerator Cookies

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

1 lb. margarine
1 egg
1 1/2 cup sugar
2 tsp. baking powder
½ tsp. salt
1 tsp. vanilla
5 cups flour
½ cup chopped nuts (if desired)

## Procedure

Mix all together and make into long roll. Chill overnight. Slice & bake at 375° for 8-10 min.
