# Beef Enchiladas

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 pkg. taco seasoning
- 1 can black beans
- 1 can enchilada sauce (will need more sauce if freezing)
- 2 cups Monterey jack cheese
- 8 to 10 flour tortillas

## Procedure
1. Brown ground beef & onion in large skillet, drain.  Add taco seasoning according to package directions.  Add black beans and 1 cup cheese.  Fill tortilla, place seam side down in a 9x13” baking dish.  Top with enchilada sauce and cheese. Bake at 350° for 30 minutes.
