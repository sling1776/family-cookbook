# A wonderful recipe that happens to be gluten free

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 8 cups water
- ½ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. black pepper
- 4 skinless chicken breast (in bite size pieces)
- 2 bay leaves
- 6 cups cubed red potatoes
- 1 ½ cups chopped onions
- 1 cup thinly sliced carrots
- 2 tsp. minced fresh cilantro
- ¼ tsp. cumin
- ½ tsp. garlic salt
- 2 tsp. paprika
- 3 cans (4.5 oz.) chopped green chilies

## Procedure
1. Combine above ingredients in pan.  Bring to a boil, cook for 10 minutes.  Discard Bay leaves.
