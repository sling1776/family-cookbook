# Monte Cristo Salad

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 6 cups torn salad greens
- 1 lg. tomato, cut into wedges
- 1 cup croutons
- 1 pkg. (9oz.) smoked turkey breast, cut in strips
- 4 Swiss cheese slices, cut into strips
- 2 hard-cooked eggs, quartered
- 1/4 cup honey-dijon mustard

## Procedure
1. serves 4
2. Combine greens, tomato, and croutons in a large serving bowl.  Add remaining ingredients; toss lightly.  Serve immediately or gets soggy.
