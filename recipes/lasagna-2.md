# Lasagna

Author: Janine Hinkle

## Ingredients
- Grated mozzarella cheese

## Procedure
1. Use Vermicelli sauce below, except do not use caraway or celery seed.  Make a thicker sauce.  Use less sugar.  Use more marjoram.  Layer in a greased pan (use olive oil)
2. Lasagna pasta
3. Meat sauce
4. Lasagna pasta
5. Meat sauce
6. Ricotta cheese (not cottage cheese)
7. Lasagna pasta
8. Meat sauce
9. Top with a thick layer of mozzarella enough to completely cover sauce.  Bake 375° for 30 minutes or until cheese starts to brown and is completely melted together.
