# Taco Soup

Author: Brianna Fielding

## Ingredients
- 1lb. ground beef
- 1 onion (opt.)
- 1 can corn
- 1 can dark red kidney beans
- 1 can peeled/diced tomatoes
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 1 packet ranch mix
- 3 cups water

## Procedure
1. Cook the ground beef with diced onion.  Drain and combine with all other ingredients.  Heat thoroughly and serve with sour cream and chips.
