# Christmas Potpourri

## Author

Kadie Allred

## Ingredients

4-5 fresh orange slices
2-3 fresh lemon slices
3 cinnamon sticks
8-10 dried whole bay leaves
1 tsp. whole cloves
6 cranberries (or more!)

## Procedure

Simmer with a pot of water and enjoy the wonderful smell of Christmas in your home!
