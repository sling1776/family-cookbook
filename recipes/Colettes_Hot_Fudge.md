# Colette's Hot Fudge

## Author

Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients

4-5 Tbsp cocoa
4 Tbsp. flour
2 cups sugar
1/2 cup butter
1 can evaporated milk
1 tsp. vanilla

## Procedure

Mix cocoa, flour and sugar in a bowl. Melt butter in a sauce pan and then add milk. Pour in sugar mixture. Whisk it together. Boil 3-5 min. Remove from heat and add vanilla.
