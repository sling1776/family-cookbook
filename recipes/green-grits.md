# Green Grits

Author: Aunt Gladys Reber Olson

## Ingredients
- 6 cups water
- 1 stick butter
- 2 tsp. savory salt

## Procedure
1. Add:  1 1/2 cup regular hominy grits
2. Cook until consistency of Cream of Wheat
3. Add: 3 beaten eggs
4. Remove from stove.
5. Add: 1 lb. cut up American cheese and 2 cans green chilies.  Put in 9x13” pan. Bake 250° oven.  2½ - 3 hours.
