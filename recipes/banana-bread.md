# Banana Bread

Author: Kadie Allred

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas
- 1 cup sugar
- ½ cup shortening
- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp. baking soda
- ¼ tsp. salt
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 Tbsp. milk
- 2 eggs

## Procedure
1. Chocolate chips (optional)
2. Mash up bananas. Mix in sugar and shortening. Add flour, baking soda, & salt. Then vanilla, milk, & eggs. Add chocolate chips or nuts. Bake at 350 degrees. Loaves-45 mins. Cupcakes-15 mins.
