## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup cold water
- 2 Tbsp. corn starch

## Procedure
1. Shake in some pepper, seasoning salt and powdered milk.  Use a whisk and beat until smooth.  Pour into the boiling water and constantly stir until it thickens.  You can leave out the butter.  If you use the powdered milk you get milk gravy.  If you leave it out you get regular gravy.
