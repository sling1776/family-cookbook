# Apple Pudding

## Author

Grandma Grace

## Ingredients

1 cup sugar
½ cup shortening
1egg
1 cup flour
4 cups grated apples
1 tsp. baking soda
½ tsp. salt
½ tsp. cinnamon
½ tsp. nutmeg
½ cup nuts or more
### Sauce
2/3 cup Karo syrup
2/3 cup canned milk
2 cups sugar
1/4 cup butter

## Procedure

Bake in a 350° oven about 35 to 45 minutes. Serve with sauce:
Bring  karo syrup, canned milk, and sugar to boil and add 1/4 cup butter

## Notes

Mama's recipe that she would make for the Walker Thanksgiving supper.
