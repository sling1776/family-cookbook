# Topping:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- ½ tsp. cinnamon
- ½ cup chopped walnuts (optional)

## Procedure
1. Beat together sugars and margarine. Add vanilla and eggs beat well. Combine flour, soda, salt. Add dry ingredients alternately with buttermilk, beat until well combined. Spoon 2/3 of batter into a greased 9x13 baking pan, spreading evenly. Sprinkle with rhubarb. Smooth additional batter on top. Mix sugar, cinnamon and nuts together, sprinkle on top. Bake at 350 for 40 to 50 minutes or until toothpick inserted comes out clean. Serve warm with whipped cream or ice cream.
