# Little Elvis Sandwiches

Author: Stephen Badger

## Ingredients
- 2 slices of your favorite bread
- Nutella

## Procedure
1. Peanut butter
2. Banana slices
3. Spread peanut butter on one slice of bread & Nutella on the other slice. Add banana slices and put slices together. Note: Try it grilled.
4. Homemade Butter – Catarina Badger – My Mommy comes into my classroom each Friday to teach about a famous artist, composer or scientist and to do fun activities with us.  One day for Scientist of the Month we learned how to turn a liquid into a solid & made this butter.
5. Fill a baby jar or container with a screw on lid half way full of whipping cream.  Shake the container for 6 to 8 minutes.  Taking turns with other kids is a good idea.  A ball of butter will form in the middle of the buttermilk liquid that is left over.  Enjoy on crackers.
