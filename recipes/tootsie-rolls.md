# Tootsie Rolls

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp. soft margarine
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- ½ cup corn syrup
- 2 squares melted chocolate (3 Tbsp. cocoa plus 1 Tbsp. shortening equals one square)
- 3 cups powdered sugar

## Procedure
1. Mix together
2. Add 3/4 cup dry powdered milk
3. Knead, roll into logs, refrigerate
