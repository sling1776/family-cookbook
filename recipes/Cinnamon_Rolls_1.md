# Cinnamon Rolls

## Author

Laurie Fryatt

## Ingredients

2 ½ cups milk
2 Tbsp. yeast
½ cup warm water.
1 cup sugar
1 1/2 tsp. salt
2 eggs
6 Tbsp. shortening
6-7 cups flour

## Procedure

Scald milk. Dissolve yeast in warm water. Separately, beat together sugar, salt, eggs, and shortening.Add scalded milk. Slowly add 6-7 cups flour. Add yeast. Let raise. Roll out with butter, brown sugar, nuts and cinnamon. Roll up like jelly roll. Let rise 1 hour. Bake at 375° for 15 min.

## Notes

I made these rolls and took them to work my boss asked me if I had made the roll and thought I was a professional. Note: I have even frozen the cinnamon rolls before letting them raise 1 hour, then when I want fresh rolls I thaw, raise, & bake.
