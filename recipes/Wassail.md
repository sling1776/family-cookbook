# Wassail

## Author

Janine Hinkle

## Ingredients

### Spice bag
 2 sticks of cinnamon
 1 piece ginger
 8 allspice berries
 10 whole cloves
 ### Syrup
 2 cups sugar
 1 quart of water
 ### Add
 1 qt. cider or apple juice
 2 cans frozen orange juice or 10 oranges
 1 can lemonade concentrate or 8 lemons

## Procedure

Boil sugar and water for 5 minutes and turn off heat. Put spice bag in for 1 hour. Stir occasionally. Add cider or apple juice and orange juice/lemon mixture. Heat but do not boil. If too strong, add a little more water.
