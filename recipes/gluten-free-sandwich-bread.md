# Gluten Free Sandwich Bread

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 ½ cups blanched almond flour
- ¾ cup arrowroot powder
- ¼ cup flax seed meal
- ½ tsp. celtic sea salt
- ½ tsp. baking soda
- 4 eggs
- 1 tsp. agave nectar
- 1 tsp. apple cider vinegar

## Procedure
1. In a medium bowl, combine almond flour, arrowroot, flax meal, salt and baking soda.
