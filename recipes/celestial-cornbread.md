# Celestial Cornbread

Author: Trisha Olsen

## Ingredients
- ½ cup butter, melted
- ½ -2/3 cup sugar
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup buttermilk
- ½ tsp. baking soda
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 1 cup cornmeal
- 1 cup flour

## Procedure
1. Beat butter, sugar and eggs well.  Add buttermilk, Mix in dry ingredients.  Pour into an 8” square greased pan.  Bake at 375° for 30 minutes.
