# Fresh Fruit Dip

Author: Jill Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 pint marshmallow cream
- 1- 8 oz. cream cheese

## Procedure
1. Cream together.  Use as dip for your choice of fruits - apples, banana, watermelon balls, or any other fruit. Variation: Add a few teaspoons of maraschino cherry juice for a lovely pink cherry dip.
