# Glazed Carrots

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 lb carrots, peeled and cut into rounds or carrot sticks (OR use baby carrots)
- 2 Tbsp. water
- 2 Tbsp. butter
- 2 Tbsp. brown sugar

## Procedure
1. Peel and slice carrots.  Microwave carrots in water in a covered microwave dish for 5 minutes or until tender crisp.  Drain. In separate bowl, melt butter in microwave then stir in brown sugar.  Add to cooked carrots & stir until well-coated.  Microwave 1 more minute.
