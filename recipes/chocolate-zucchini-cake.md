# Chocolate Zucchini Cake

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- ¾ cup sugar
- ½ cup packed brown sugar
- ½ cup (4oz) fat free cream cheese, softened
- 1/3 cup vegetable oil
- 2 large eggs
- 2 large egg whites
- 1 tsp. vanilla extract
- 2 ½ cups flour
- ½ cup unsweetened cocoa
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- ½ tsp. baking soda
- ½ tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. cinnamon
- ¾ cup fat free buttermilk (or soured milk)
- 2 cups shredded zucchini
- 2/3 cups mini chocolate chips
- ¼ cup chopped walnuts

## Procedure
1. Spray & flour 12-cup bundt pan.  Place sugars, cream cheese, and vegetable oil in a large bowl and beat well.  Add eggs, 1 at a time & vanilla.  Combine dry ingredients and add, alternating with the buttermilk.  Stir in the zucchini, choc. chips & nuts.  Bake at 350° for 1 hour.
