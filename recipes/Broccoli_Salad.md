# Broccoli Salad

## Author

Tiffanie Olsen

## Ingredients

4 broccoli stalks, chopped
3/4 cup golden raisin
½ cup chopped onion
½ cup sunflower seeds
5 strips crispy bacon, crumbled
OR ¼ cup bacon bits
### Dressing:
1 cup mayo
1/3 cup sugar
2 Tbsp. white vinegar

## Procedure

Mix together dressing. Toss with veggies.
