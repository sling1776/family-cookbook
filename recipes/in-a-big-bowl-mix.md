# In a big bowl mix:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 3 cups chocolate chips
- 2 cups chopped nuts
- 1 Tbsp. vanilla
- 26 large marshmallows OR 3 cups mini
- ½ lb. butter or margarine

## Procedure
1. Pour hot mixture on top and stir until dissolved.  Pour into lightly greased jelly roll pan. Chill.
