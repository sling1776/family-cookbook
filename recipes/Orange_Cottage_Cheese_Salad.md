# Orange Cottage Cheese Salad

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

1 pint cottage cheese
1 (8oz.) crushed pineapple
1 (11oz) mandarin oranges
1 (8oz.) cool whip.
1 small pkg. orange Jell-O.

## Procedure

Stir all ingredients together and serve

## Notes

This is a recipe from Great Grandma Doris Reber who kindly let us live with them for time when we moved to St. George. There our last name was pronounced with a short e, like web.
