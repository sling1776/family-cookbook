# Fry Bread:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 5 cups flour
- 2 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1/3 cup powdered milk

## Procedure
1. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, and milk together.  Stir in lukewarm water to form soft dough.  Knead for 5 min.  Let stand for ½ hour.  Melt shortening in frying pan to make 2 inches.  Pinch off two inches of dough and pat out into thin taco size.  Place in hot grease.  Brown both sides about 10 seconds.
