# Applesauce Cake

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- ½ cup shortening
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 egg
- ½ cup boiling water
- 2 tsp. baking soda (dissolved in the boiling water)
- ½ cup chopped nuts
- 1 cup chopped raisin
- 1 1/2 cups applesauce
- 2 ½ cups flour
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- ½ tsp. each cinnamon, cloves, allspice

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients then bake 350° for an hour.
2. Fruit Cake:  Add 1 cup gumdrops and maraschino cherries.
