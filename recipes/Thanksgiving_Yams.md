# Thanksgiving Yams

## Author

Becky Concoby

## Ingredients

2 cups mashed yams
2/3 cup sugar
3/4 stick butter (6 Tbsp.)
2 beaten eggs
1 tsp. vanilla
### Topping:
1 cup brown sugar
1/3 cup soft butter
1/2 cup flour
1 chopped pecans

## Procedure

Mix first 5 ingredients and put in a 9 x 13 pan. Spread topping over yams and bake at 350 for 30 minutes.
