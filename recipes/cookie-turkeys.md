# Cookie Turkeys

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 40 fudge-striped cookies
- 1/4 cup chocolate frosting
- 2 pkgs. (5oz. each) chocolate-covered cherries
- 20 pieces candy corn

## Procedure
1. Place ½ of cookies on a flat surface, solid chocolate side down. With frosting attach a
2. chocolate-covered cherry to the top of each base cookie. Attach another cookie perpendicular to each base cookie with frosting. With frosting, attach one piece of candy corn to the front of each cherry for the head. Let stand until set. Yield: 20 servings
