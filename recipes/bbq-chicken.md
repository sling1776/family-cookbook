# BBQ Chicken

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1 cup BBQ sauce (Bull’s-eye best brand)
- 1 cup water
- ½ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup vinegar
- 1 Tbsp. Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tsp. dry mustard
- 1 Tbsp. dry chopped onions

## Procedure
1. Pour sauce over chicken.  Cook for about 2 ½ -3 hours until very tender in a Dutch Oven at about 250°.  Can use slow cooker or regular oven
