# Chocolate Chip Cookies

Author: Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients
- 3 cups cookie mix
- ½ cup nuts or coconut
- 3 Tbsp. milk
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 1 egg
- 1 cup chocolate chips or sugar coated Chocolate candies

## Procedure
1. Preheat oven to 375°.
2. Grease baking sheet.  In a large bowl, combine
3. cookie mix, milk, vanilla, and egg.  Blend will.  Stir in nuts or coconut and chocolate chips.  Drop by teaspoonfuls onto prepared baking sheet.  Bake 10 to 15 minutes, until golden brown. Makes about 24 cookies.
