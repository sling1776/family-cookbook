# Birdseed

Author: Grandma Grace

## Ingredients
- No ingredients listed.

## Procedure
1. 1 qt soft wheat - either soak for 48 hours -  changing water once or slow boil until double in bulk about 1 ½ - 2 hours.  Drain and spread out to dry rest of moisture.  Fry small amount of wheat at a time in hot deep fat for 2-3 minutes. Don't burn or get too dark.  Light tan is just right. Roast 2 cups raw peanuts in 200° oven for ½ hour or until you can just see the oil coming on nuts.  Add to wheat.  You can add 1 cup sunflower seeds.  1 cup salted peanuts. Season lightly with onion salt or garlic salt.  Plain wheat can be popped in small amount of hot fat stirring so it won't burn.  This can be used in place of the deep fat fried wheat.  Rice can also be popped in hot oil and added.  If you don't have soft wheat, use what you have.
