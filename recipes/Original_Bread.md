# Original Bread

## Author

Janine Hinkle

## Ingredients

3 cups wheat flour
3 cups Quaker Oats
6 cups white flour
1 Tbsp. salt
2 Tbsp. yeast
2/3 cup oil
2/3 cup honey or sugar (honey tastes best)
5 cups hot tap water

## Procedure

Mix flour, oats, salt and yeast together. Then add the oil, honey or sugar, and hot water. Mix and knead only slightly. Let rise. Punch down and divide into 4 loaves and place into bread pans. Let rise until double in bulk. Bake at 350°. When you smell the bread baking set the timer for 5 more minutes. (Don't laugh, it works for me)

## Notes

I love making wheat bread. This recipe just evolved over the years. Hope you'll try it & enjoy the results.
