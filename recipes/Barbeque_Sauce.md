# Barbeque Sauce

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

3 cups brown sugar
3 cups ketchup
minced onion
1 can of crushed pineapple (you can use juice only if desired)
garlic salt and mustard to taste
Optional: 1/4 cup soy sauce can be added for extra zing!

## Procedure

Mix all together, marinate chicken, ribs, or anything else your heart desires!
