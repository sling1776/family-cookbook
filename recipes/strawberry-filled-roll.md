# Strawberry Filled Roll

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- ½ cup whipping cream
- 2 Tbsp. powdered sugar
- 2 cups sliced strawberries

## Procedure
1. About 1 hour before serving, beat cream and sugar until stiff.  Spread on unrolled cake. Arrange strawberries over whipped cream.  Roll up, sprinkle with powdered sugar. Chill.
