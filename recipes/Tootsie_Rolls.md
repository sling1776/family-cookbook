# Tootsie Rolls

## Author

Sue Reber

## Ingredients

2 Tbsp. soft margarine
1 tsp. vanilla
½ cup corn syrup
2 squares melted chocolate (3 Tbsp. cocoa plus 1 Tbsp. shortening equals one square)
3/4 cup dry powdered milk
3 cups powdered sugar

## Procedure

Mix together margarine, vanilla, corn syrup, melted chocolate.
Add dry powdered milk and sugar.
Knead, roll into logs, refrigerate
