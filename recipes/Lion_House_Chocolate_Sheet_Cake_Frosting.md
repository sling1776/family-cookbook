# Lion House Chocolate Sheet Cake Frosting

## Author



## Ingredients

½ cup butter
4 Tbsp. cocoa
1 tsp. vanilla
6 Tbsp. milk
4 cups powdered sugar
1 cup chopped nuts

## Procedure

Melt butter; add cocoa, vanilla and milk. Bring to a boil. Remove from heat add powdered sugar and nuts. Mix well, frost cake while still warm.
