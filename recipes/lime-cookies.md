# Lime Cookies

Author: Sammi Jo Stewart

## Ingredients
- 3 sticks butter
- 4 Tbsp. fresh lime juice
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 3 cups flour
- 4 Tbsp. grated lime zest (4 med. limes)

## Procedure
1. Cream butter and sugars until light. DO NOT over mix. Add: lime juice, lime zest and salt. Mix. Add flour and mix. Place dough on wax paper.  Shape into an 8 inch log. Flatten into a rectangle (1 ½”high & 2 ½” wide).  Refrigerate until firm (at least 1 hour, up to 1 day). Slice dough crosswise 1/2 inch thick  Bake at 350° for 8-11 minutes.
