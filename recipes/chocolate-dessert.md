# Chocolate Dessert

Author: Becky Concoby

## Ingredients
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup crushed nuts
- 4 oz. cream cheese, room temperature
- 3 cups milk
- 1 pkg. vanilla instant pudding
- 1 cube margarine
- 1 cup Cool Whip
- ½ cup powdered sugar
- 1 pkg. chocolate instant pudding

## Procedure
1. Mix flour, margarine and nuts.  Pat firmly into a 9x13” pan.  Bake at 350° for 15-20 minutes.  Cool.  Blend Cool Whip, cream cheese, and powdered sugar in mixer until smooth.  Spread carefully on cool crust.  Mix milk, chocolate and vanilla pudding according to directions on package.  Spread on top of cream cheese mixture. Top with remaining Cool Whip.  Garnish with crushed nuts or chocolate shavings.  Chill before serving.  Store in frig.
