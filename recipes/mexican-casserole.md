# Mexican Casserole

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. hamburger
- ½ onion, chopped
- 1- (12 oz) can refried beans
- 1 small can sliced olives
- 1 sm. can diced green chilies
- ½ pkg. taco seasoning
- 5 Tbsp. sour cream
- ½ cup shredded cheese

## Procedure
1. Brown hamburger and onion.  Mix in rest of ingredients.  Spoon onto flour tortillas and roll up.  Melt more cheese on top.  Serve on a bed of shredded lettuce and a scoop of sour cream.
