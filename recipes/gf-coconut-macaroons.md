# GF Coconut Macaroons

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 6 egg whites
- ¼ tsp. celtic sea salt
- ½ cup agave nectar
- 1 Tbsp. vanilla extract
- 3 cups shredded coconut
- Pinch each macaroon at the top (like a kiss)

## Procedure
1. In a mixing bowl whisk egg whites and salt until stiff. Fold in agave, vanilla and coconut.
2. Drop batter onto a parchment lined baking sheet, one rounded tablespoonful at a time.
3. Bake at 350° for 10-15 minutes, until lightly browned.
