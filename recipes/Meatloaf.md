# Meatloaf

## Author

Annalisa Fielding

## Ingredients

2 eggs
2 Tbsp. chopped onion
1 cup tomato juice
½ tsp. salt
2/3 cup rolled oats
1/4 tsp. garlic salt
½ tsp. sage
few shakes of savory salt
1 lb. hamburger

## Procedure

Combine ingredients in a bowl:
Put in loaf pan sprayed with Pam. Top with Bull’s-eye BBQ sauce. Cook at 350° for 45 minutes to an hour.
