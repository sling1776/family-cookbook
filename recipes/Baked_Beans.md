# Baked Beans

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

1 lb. hamburger
½ chopped onion
1 cup celery diced
1 gr. pepper chopped
1 ½ tsp. vinegar
1 ½ tsp. prepared mustard
1 lg. can pork and beans
1 can tomato soup

## Procedure

Brown hamburger, onion, celery, and pepper. Then add vinegar, mustard, pork & beans, and tomato soup.
Mix all together and bake for 35 min. at 350°.
