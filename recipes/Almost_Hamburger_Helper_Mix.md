# "Almost Hamburger Helper" Mix

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

2 cups nonfat dry milk
1 cup corn starch
1/4 cup beef bouillon powder
2 Tbsp. onion flakes
1 tsp. dried thyme
1 tsp. black pepper
2 Tbsp. dried parsley
1 Tbsp. garlic powder
1 tsp. dried basil

## Procedure

Mix the ingredients together and store in an airtight jar.
