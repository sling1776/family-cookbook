# Peanutty Granola

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 8 cups oats
- 1 ½ cups brown sugar
- 1 ½ cup wheat germ
- 1 cup cashew pieces
- 1 cup slivered almonds
- 1 cup shredded coconuts
- ½ cup sunflower seeds
- ½ cup water
- ½ cup oil
- ½ cup peanut butter
- ½ cup honey
- 2 tsp. vanilla
- 1 cup raisins or Craisins

## Procedure
1. Grease 2 cake pans.  In a large bowl mix oats, brown sugar, wheat germ, cashew pieces, almonds, coconut and sunflower seeds; set aside.  In a medium saucepan, mix water, oil, peanut butter, honey and vanilla.  Heat to boiling – stir well.  Pour over oat mixture and stir well coating everything. Bake at 200º for 1 hour, stirring 3 times.  Cool.  Add raisins.  Store cover in airtight container.
