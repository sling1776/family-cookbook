# Nut Roll

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1 (7½ oz.) jar marshmallow crème
- 1 tsp. vanilla
- 3 ½ cups powder sugar
- 1 lb. carmels
- 9 ½ cups chopped nuts

## Procedure
1. Combine marshmallow crème and vanilla. Add sugar gradually. Shape into rolls about 1” in diameter. Wrap in plastic wrap and freeze for at least 6 hours. Melt carmel in a double broiler. Keep water. Dip roll in carmel then in nuts until well coated. Store in covered container.
