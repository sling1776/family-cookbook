# Gluten Free Basic Cake Mix

## Author

Barbara Olsen

## Ingredients

6 cups GF flour*
3 Tbsp. baking powder
2 Tbsp. xanthan gum
4 cups sugar
2 tsp. salt
1 ½ cups shortening

## Procedure

In a large bowl combine well GF flour, baking powder, xanthan gum, sugar, and salt.
With a pastry blender, cut shortening into flour mix until it resembles cornmeal.
Store in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. If possible use within one month, although it keeps well for a longer period of time.

## Notes

*(I use 3 cups feather light mix, 2 cups GF flour mix and 1 cup Sorghum flour mix.)
