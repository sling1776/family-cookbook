# Pizza

Author: Sue Reber

## Ingredients
- 1 pkg. yeast
- 4 ½ cups flour
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1 ½ cups water

## Procedure
1. Combine yeast, 2 cups flour, and salt.  Heat water to 120-130°. Just blend with dry ingredients.  Beat 2 minutes.  Add rest of flour to make a soft dough.  Knead 8 minutes.  Let rise until doubled, about 1 hour.  In the meantime prepare sauce:
