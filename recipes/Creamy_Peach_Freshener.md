# Creamy Peach Freshener

## Author

Tawna Marsh

## Ingredients

1 cup fresh or bottled peaches OR any fruit you like
 2 cups milk
 1 scoop vanilla ice cream
 4-5 ice cubes
 ½ tsp. vanilla

## Procedure

Put together in blender. Blend until ice cubes are gone.

## Notes

Can add an egg and 1/4 cup wheat germ.
