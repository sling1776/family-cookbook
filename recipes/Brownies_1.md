# Brownies

## Author

Grandma Reber

## Ingredients

2 cups sugar 
4 eggs
1 cup margarine
1 (12oz.) pkg. chocolate chips
2 cups nuts
2 cups flour
1 tsp. baking powder
1 tsp. salt 
2 tsp. vanilla

## Procedure

Combine sugar and eggs. Separately, Melt together margaring, cholcoate chips, and nuts. Sift dry ingredients. Mix all of ingredients (add vanilla). Bake 350° for 40 minutes. Develops glaze as it bakes.
