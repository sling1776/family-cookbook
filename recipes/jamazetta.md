# Jamazetta

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. ground beef
- 1 -3oz. pkg. cream cheese, softened
- 1 can tomato soup
- 1 small onion, chopped
- ½ pkg. egg noodles (or whatever available)

## Procedure
1. Sauté hamburger and onion together.  Drain.  Cut cream cheese into cubes.  Add soup and cream cheese to meat and heat through.  Cook and drain noodles.  Mix all ingredients together and bake at 350ºfor 30 minutes.
2. Note: Or for faster results, microwave until heated through.
