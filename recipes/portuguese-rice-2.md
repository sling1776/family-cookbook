# Portuguese Rice

Author: Justin & Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 2 cups of rice, rinsed off
- 4 cups of water
- ½ cup chopped onions
- 1/4 cup shredded carrots
- 2 Tbsp. olive oil

## Procedure
1. This is the typical side dish for almost every meal in Portugal.  Justin & I met in Portugal on our missions and love being able to remember that time with the great food we learned how to make.
2. Brown the onions and carrots in the
3. olive oil.  Add rice & cook until slightly brown.  Add the water and bring to a boil.  Turn down heat to low and let cook 20 minutes.  Serve hot with cod fish, sardines, etc.
