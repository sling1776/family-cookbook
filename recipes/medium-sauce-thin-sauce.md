# Medium Sauce Thin Sauce

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 3 Tbsp. butter		2 Tbsp. butter
- 4 Tbsp. F. Lite mix 	2 Tbsp. F. Lite mix
- 1/8 tsp. salt		1/8 tsp. salt
- dash pepper		dash pepper
- 1 cup milk		1 cup milk

## Procedure
1. Melt butter over medium heat. Stir in feather light mix, salt, and pepper. Let this mixture brown slightly (stirring regularly). Add milk. Stir with a whisk (to eliminate lumps) until sauce is thickened and boiling. Remove from heat. Note: 1 cup grated cheddar cheese may be added to this sauce, after it thickens, if you like. (Good for broccoli and baked potatoes)
