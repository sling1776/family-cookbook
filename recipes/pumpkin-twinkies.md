# Pumpkin Twinkies

Author: Rachel Mitchell

## Ingredients
- 10 Twinkies
- 1 - 8oz. cream cheese (softened)
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1 - 8oz. Cool Whip (thawed)
- 2 - 3.4oz. pkgs. vanilla instant pudding
- 1 - 15oz. can canned pumpkin
- 1 1/2 tsp. pumpkin pie spice
- 1 cup milk
- cinnamon for sprinkling

## Procedure
1. Unwrap Twinkies and cut each in half (long ways). Place the cut Twinkies (uncut part down) in a greased 9 x 13 cake pan--set aside. In a mixer beat cream cheese and add powdered sugar--beat. Add 1/2 of the Cool Whip--beat. Spread cream cheese mixture on top of Twinkies. In a mixer add both boxes of pudding mix and milk--beat. Add pumpkin--beat. Add pumpkin pie spice--beat. Pour batter on top of cream cheese mixture. Spread remaining cool whip on top. Sprinkle cinnamon with if desired. Refrigerate overnight.
