# Olive oil

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- ½ lb. chorizo, Brazilian sausage
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 1 medium pepper, diced
- 3-15 oz. cans of beans, drained

## Procedure
1. Slice the chorizo and fry in a large skillet with the olive oil.  Add the onion and pepper after the chorizo is lightly browned.   Cook for several minutes until veggies are soft and then add drained beans.  Heat through and serve over hot Portuguese rice.
