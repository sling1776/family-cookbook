# Busy Day Casserole

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

1 pkg. refrigerator biscuits
1 can cream of cheddar cheese soup
½ cup milk
1 small chopped onion
3/4 cup frozen peas
1 can drained tuna

## Procedure

Layer one pkg. refrigerator biscuits into greased casserole pan.
Mix together: cream of cheaddarhesse soup and milk
Mix until smooth then add onion peas and tuna.
Stir until blended. Pour over biscuits. Bake 350° for 35 minutes. May be topped with grated cheese.
