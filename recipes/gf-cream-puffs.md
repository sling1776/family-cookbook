# GF Cream Puffs

Author: Emily Hammerstad

## Ingredients
- 1 cup water
- ½ cup or 1 stick of butter
- 1 cup GF flour (I like Bob's Redmill GF all- purpose flour)
- 4 eggs

## Procedure
1. Over medium heat melt butter in the water inside a pot.  When it is fully melted, turn heat off, add the flour and stir until it forms a ball.  Then add the eggs and stir until it is a consistent matter.  It should look similar to paste.  Use a small ice cream scoop and ball into a slightly greased mini cupcake pan.  Bake for 20 minutes at 350°.
