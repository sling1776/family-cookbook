# Sauté these ingredients and add:

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- 1 can tomato soup
- 1 small can corn
- ½ cup grated cheese
- 3/4 lb. cooked & drained hamburger
- 1 can mushrooms
- ½ tsp. each salt, pepper, paprika, and cayenne.

## Procedure
1. Mix all together and pour into buttered baking dish and sprinkle with Parmesan cheese. Bake for 30 minutes at 375°.
