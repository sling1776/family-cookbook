# Burrito Bake

Author: Liz Bunderson

## Ingredients
- 1 lb. lean ground beef
- 1 pkg. taco seasoning Mix
- 3/4 cup water
- 12 flour tortillas (6 inch)
- 1 can refried beans
- 1 cup shredded cheddar jack cheese

## Procedure
1. Heat oven to 350°.  Brown meat in large skillet; drain. Stir in seasoning mix and water. Bring to boil. Cook on low heat 10 min., stirring occasionally. Place 4 tortillas on bottom of greased 9-inch pie plate. Cover with half each of the refried beans, meat mixture and cheese. Repeat layers once. Top with remaining tortillas. Bake 30 min. or until heated through and cheese is melted. Cut into wedges to serve.  Serve burrito wedges topped with shredded lettuce, chopped tomato and salsa.
