# Enough green beans to feed the family

Author: Unknown Author

## Ingredients
- Bacon (1slice per each 2 servings)
- Chopped fresh onion (or dried minced onions)

## Procedure
1. Seasoning
2. Simply snip (or snap) the beans to bite size then cook them in a little water with some chopped or minced onion in the pot. They should be tender at the same time the beans are.  In the meantime fry the bacon until crisp, crumble on the drained beans. Pour the bacon fat over the beans and enjoy your veggies.
