# Homemade Noodles

Author: Grandma Grace Fielding

## Ingredients
- 1 egg slightly beaten
- 1/4 tsp. salt
- 2/3 cup flour (OR enough to make stiff dough).

## Procedure
1. Mix all ingredients together. Knead dough and then let it stand covered for ½ hour.  Roll it out very thin.  Cut into strips with noodle cutter, or pizza cutter.
