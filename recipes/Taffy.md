# Taffy

## Author

Bonnie Chantry

## Ingredients

2 cups sugar
½ cup vinegar
dash of salt
½ cup water

## Procedure

Boil to thread stage (crack). Pour in buttered cookie sheet. After it cools somewhat, add 1 tsp vanilla. Pull with buttered fingers until glossy. Pull into long strips and snip with buttered scissors.
