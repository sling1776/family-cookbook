# Maple Glazed Sweet Potatoes

Author: Grace Ann Skousen

## Ingredients
- 5 med. sweet potatoes
- ¼ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup pure maple syrup
- ¼ cup apple cider
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Procedure
1. Peel and cut up sweet potatoes into ½ inch slices, place in crock pot. Whisk remaining ingredients together and pour over potatoes. Cover and cook on low for 7-9 hours. Stir a few times to keep them coated.
