# Flour Tortilla

Author: Tiffanie Olsen

## Ingredients
- 1 cup flour
- 1 1/2 Tbsp. shortening
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1/4 cup water
- 1/4 tsp. baking powder

## Procedure
1. Combine flour, baking powder and salt. Work shortening in like pie crust. Add water and mix. Roll into 8 inch circles. Heat skillet to medium heat. Fry tortillas with no grease, turning over when side is flecked with brown.
