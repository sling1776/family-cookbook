# Whole Wheat Sugar Cookies

## Author

Ruby Fielding

## Ingredients

1 cup shortening
2 cups sugar
4 eggs
5 cups whole wheat flour
2 cups white flour
3 tsp. baking powder
½ tsp. salt
½ tsp. baking soda
1 cup sour milk (1 cup milk + 1 Tbsp. vinegar)

## Procedure

Cream shortening and sugar.
Add and beat 4 eggs. Add remaining ingredients.
Chill dough, roll 1/4 inch thick and cut into desired shapes. Place on lightly greased cookie sheets and bake at 375° for 8-10 minutes.
