# Oatmeal pancakes

## Author

Jill Olsen

## Ingredients

1/2 cup old fashioned oatmeal
1/4 cup low-fat cottage cheese or tofu
4 egg whites
1/4 tsp. cinnamon
1/4 tsp. nutmeg
1 tsp. vanilla

## Procedure

Mix ingredients in a blender until smooth. Pour onto griddle, cook till golden brown
