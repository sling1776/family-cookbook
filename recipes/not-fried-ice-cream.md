# Not Fried Ice Cream

Author: Amy Badger

## Ingredients
- 1/2 quart vanilla ice cream
- 2 1/2 cups cornflake cereal, crushed
- 1 Tbsp. butter
- 2 Tbsp. sugar
- 1 tsp. cinnamon

## Procedure
1. Make 8 vanilla ice cream balls and freeze on wax-lined cookie sheet for about an hour.
2. Mix remaining ingredients and spread on a cookie sheet to bake at 350°F until crisp and golden. (Cooking times will vary according to taste and oven temps.) Let cool. Roll ice cream balls in the cooled mixture and refreeze for 2 hours more. Serve with honey drizzled over each and a maraschino cherry.
