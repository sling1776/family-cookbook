# Sugar Cookies

## Author

Laurie Fryatt

## Ingredients

3 1/4 cup flour
1 tsp. baking soda
1 cup sugar
1 tsp. vanilla
½ tsp. salt
1 cup butter
1 egg
1 cup sour cream

## Procedure

Bake at 350° for about 8-10 minutes. Press to see if done ( lightly brown on bottom).

## Notes

My roommate gave me this recipe. We make these as often as we can.
