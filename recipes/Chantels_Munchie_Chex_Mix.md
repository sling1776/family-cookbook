# Chantel’s Munchie Chex Mix

## Author

Chantel Moody

## Ingredients

2 large boxes Corn Chex cereal
8 oz slivered almonds
2 squares butter
1 1/2 cup sugar
1 1/2 cups white Karo syrup
1 1/2 tsp vanilla

## Procedure

Mix in a large bowl chex cereal and almonds
In a pan mix butter, sugar, karo syrup.
Bring to a boil and boil for 2 full minutes. Remove from heat and add vanilla.
Pour over Chex mix.
