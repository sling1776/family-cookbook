# Deep Fried Cod

## Author

Grandma Reber

## Ingredients

Fish fillets
Milk
Cracker crumbs
Thyme
### Tartar Sauce:
1 cup mayonnaise
1 tsp. grated onion
1 tsp. minced parsley
2 Tbsp. minced dill pickle
1 Tbsp. pimento
dash of Tabasco sauce

## Procedure

Dip fillets in milk, then in fine cracker crumbs.
Deep fry 2-3 minutes on both sides. Salt slightly and sprinkle with thyme.
