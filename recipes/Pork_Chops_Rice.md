# Pork Chops & Rice

## Author

Angela Olsen

## Ingredients

1½ cups long grain rice
1½ cups shredded sharp cheese (Save ½ cup for later)
3 cups cold water
1 pkg. dry onion soup mix
pork chops
2 cans cream of mushroom soup

## Procedure

Layer rice, 1 cup cheese, water, dry onion soup mix in a 9x13” baking dish
Add pork chops (1 layer) then add on top 2 cans cream of mushroom soup
Bake uncovered for 1 hour at 350°. Uncover, spread on remaining cheese and bake for ½ hour more.
