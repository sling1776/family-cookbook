# Hot Rolls

Author: Grandma Grace via Aunt Joyce via Bonnie

## Ingredients
- 1/3-1/2 cup sugar

## Procedure
1. Soften 2 Tbsp. yeast in ½ cup warm water
2. Meanwhile, beat 3 eggs
3. Add ½ cup shortening
4. Cream, like for a cake.
5. Add 1 tsp. salt
6. and 1 cup flour
7. Add yeast mixture and mix.
8. Add 1 cup flour and beat
9. Add 1 cup warm milk and beat again
10. Add enough flour to make soft dough
