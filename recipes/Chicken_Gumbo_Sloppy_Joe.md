# Chicken Gumbo Sloppy Joe

## Author

Marjean Spencer

## Ingredients

1 lb. ground beef
½ tsp. salt
2/3 cups onion chopped
½ tsp. pepper
1 can chicken gumbo soup
1/4 cup water
2 Tbsp. catsup
1 Tbsp. mustard

## Procedure

Brown together beef, salt, onion and pepper then add remainer of ingredients.
Mix well. Simmer 30-40 minutes. Serve on hamburger buns.
