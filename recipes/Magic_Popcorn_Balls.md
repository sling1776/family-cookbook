# Magic Popcorn Balls

## Author

Chrissy Probst

## Ingredients

½ cup popcorn kernels
1/4 cup margarine
1 cup brown sugar
1/4 cup light corn syrup
1 1/2 tsp. water
1/4 tsp. baking soda

## Procedure

Pop the popcorn & put into a brown paper sack.
Add the butter, sugar, corn syrup and water into a sauce pan and boil.
Bring to a full boil. Take off the heat. Then add 1/4 tsp. baking soda
Pour over popcorn in sack. Shake bag until all popcorn is covered. Make into balls
